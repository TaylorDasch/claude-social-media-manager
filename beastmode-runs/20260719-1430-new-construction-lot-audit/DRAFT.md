# Draft

## Locked Concept

**Working title:** `How to Pick the Right New-Construction Lot in Temple TX`

**Thumbnail:** `LOT A, B OR C?`

**One viewer:** a Temple-area buyer preparing to reserve or compare a new-build lot.

**One decision:** which lot fits the buyer's stated priorities and which material facts still need proof.

**One proof:** Taylor applies the same five checks to three permissioned Temple-area lots using one fixed floor plan/confirmed placement, then shows every A/B/C result.

## Core Promise

Flooring, paint, and cabinets can change. The parcel, documented constraints, easements, orientation, access, and surrounding plan are much harder to change. The viewer leaves knowing what to observe, which documents to request, and what requires a licensed professional.

## Five Checks

1. Water and grade questions.
2. Orientation and daily exposure.
3. Documented constraints and apparent remaining area.
4. Street position and observed access friction.
5. Future edge, adjacency, and privacy fit.
