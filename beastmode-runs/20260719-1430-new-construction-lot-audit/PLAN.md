## Workstream Output Bar

- 7:45-9:00; current script target 7:45-8:45.
- First payoff inside 30 seconds.
- Visual or audio pattern change every 7-12 seconds.
- Three real lots, one buyer profile, one scorecard, and one final decision fork.

## Failure Points

1. Manufacturing a dramatic reject instead of following the documents.
2. Treating visible water or grade cues as an engineering diagnosis.
3. Letting exact lot availability, incentives, or premiums leak into the edit.
4. Calling one lot universally best without defining buyer priorities.
5. Showing documents, addresses, plates, workers, buyers, or private land without permission/redaction.
6. Creating a separate SEO page that cannibalizes the existing canonical new-construction page.
7. Promising a live automated download before Railway-dependent fulfillment is active.
