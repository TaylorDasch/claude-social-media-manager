# Mission

## Objective

Identify and fully develop the strongest next long-form video for Taylor Dasch's new-construction positioning after auditing the Living in Temple channel for duplication.

## Workstream

YouTube / New Construction / Owned Inbound

## Audience / Operator

- Operator: Taylor Dasch, EG Realty, Temple/Belton/Central Texas.
- Viewer: a Temple-area buyer who is about to reserve or compare a new-construction lot.

## Business Outcome

Create an evergreen decision asset that attracts buyers before they commit to a lot, converts qualified viewers into direct conversations, and strengthens the canonical new-construction page without relying on volatile builder incentives.

## Success Criteria

1. No material duplication of Taylor's 128 audited long-form uploads or planned production bibles.
2. One viewer, one decision, and one visible proof mechanism.
3. Builder-neutral and evergreen; no incentive, rate, inventory, ranking, or appreciation premise.
4. A complete 7:45-9:00 production system: word-for-word script, field plan, shots, graphics, packaging, publishing copy, lead asset, distribution, and page flywheel.
5. Primary or official support for every factual teaching point.
6. Legal, fair-housing, privacy, filming, and engineering boundaries are explicit.
7. Adversarial critique and verification are completed before handoff.

## Constraints

- Do not enter a job site or private property without permission.
- Do not diagnose drainage, soil, engineering, title, insurance, tax, or legal conditions.
- Do not imply that a FEMA Zone X designation means no flood risk.
- Do not describe a subdivision as safe, good for families, high-quality, or likely to appreciate.
- Do not name a builder negatively or turn the episode into a builder ranking.
- Do not mention current incentives, promotional rates, lot premiums, or availability.
- The exact lots, documents, and filming permission must be verified on film day.
- Railway-dependent website fulfillment remains held until Taylor renews Railway on Tuesday.

## Non-Goals

- Another general new-construction mistakes guide.
- A new-construction-versus-resale comparison.
- A model-home or builder-review video.
- An inspection-stage explainer.
- A current-market promotion video.
- Publishing, scheduling, or changing the live site without Taylor's approval.

## Approval-Gated Actions

- Obtain builder/developer or property-owner filming permission where needed.
- Publish or schedule the video, Shorts, GBP post, or social assets.
- Add the video, form, download, or schema to templetxhomes.net.
- Change the dirty content registry.

## Done Means

Taylor can secure three permitted lots, gather the required documents, complete the broker/professional reviews, and then film from this packet against a defined retention spine. The packet includes a real scorecard PDF and makes the remaining delivery test and Tuesday website activation steps unambiguous; it does not label the episode film-ready prematurely.
