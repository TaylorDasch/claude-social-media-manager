# Mission

Build an evidence-first Temple/Belton market-update filming package for `Living in Temple TX`, aimed at one relocating buyer, with exceptional hook/title/thumbnail work and a reusable Council audit-and-action prompt.

## Scope

- Temple and Belton only.
- Analyze all relevant Market Monitor point-in-time and historical sources.
- Explain what the data means for buyers and relocators.
- Include new construction only where stable data supports it; no changing promotion.
- Protect the separate Temple-versus-Belton location-comparison video.
- Produce and verify local artifacts; do not publish or mutate external systems.

## Definition of done

- Raw-data claims reproduce from a read-only script.
- Export-status traps, duplicates, rentals, stale snapshots, and classification errors are documented.
- Title, thumbnail, first frame, first 30 seconds, and script form one click contract.
- Full production and launch package is filmable by a solo local agent.
- Independent data, packaging, prior-video, and red-team critiques are reconciled.
- Council prompt instructs a future model to audit, implement safe fixes, verify, and stop before publish.
