Window: Thursday, May 21, 2026 through Sunday, May 31, 2026.

Goal: Use the double-usage window to build the durable system and produce enough draft packets to prove the system works.

| Target | Count | Quality bar |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Demand-led source packets | 10 | Each tied to a GSC page, registry item, MLS proof, or existing video. |
