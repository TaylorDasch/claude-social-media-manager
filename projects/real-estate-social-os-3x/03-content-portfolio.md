## Platform Routing

| Platform | Use for | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok | Buyer/relocator property tours, BSW/military/general home-context footage. | Investor math, long-form repurposing, desk scripts. |
| Instagram Reels | Short native visual explainers and repurposed Shorts without watermarks. | Thin captions and generic hashtags. |
| YouTube Shorts | Searchable Temple TX concepts from long-form and page topics. | Vague lifestyle clips without query intent. |
| LinkedIn | BSW, seller, investor, builder, economic development analysis. | Casual feed filler. |
| GBP | Local entity/citation posts linked to specific pages. | Homepage links and unsupported claims. |
| Newsletter | Deeper proof and conversion. | Mixed buyer/investor lanes. |
| BP/Reddit | Investor education and helpful answers. | Direct pitch or video-link dumps. |
