# The Leverage List

Taylor Dasch with EG Realty here in Temple. I reviewed the newest local inventory for listings where documented price history, market time, vacancy, or a separate public record may support a sharper buyer question. Only three made this issue: two conventional Temple and Belton picks, plus one clearly labeled Killeen watch.

> Issue 2 · MLS snapshot dated August 17, 2026 · live listing checks completed August 18, 2026.

## Market gauge

Temple and Belton had **885 active residential listings** in the August 17 snapshot, with a **73-day median DOM**. **493 of 885 active listings (55.7%)** were priced below their original list price, including **116 (13.1%)** reduced at least 8%. Among **149 closings from July 20 through August 14**, the median closed price was **96.28% of original list price**.

The read: buyer room exists, but a price cut does not prove value. Condition, original pricing, comparable sales, and seller response still decide whether an offer makes sense.

**Active at $300,000** · originally $340,000 · 91 DOM/CDOM · 4 beds · 2 full and 1 half baths · 2,550 square feet · built 2016 · vacant · no HOA shown in Matrix.

Five reductions have lowered the price by **$40,000 (11.8%)**. A clean cash test at **$282,000–$290,000** would be **3.3%–6.0% below the current ask** and **$50,000–$58,000 below the original list price**. The reductions, market time, and vacancy support asking. They do not promise acceptance.

**Estimated payment:** about **$2,674 per month** under the assumptions below. This is an estimate, not a lender quote.

**Verify before an offer:** condition, disclosures, repairs, insurance, title and restrictions, current comparable sales, and seller response.

## Trustee Sale Watch · 3301 Chisholm Circle · Killeen · MLS 615507

**Active at $165,000** · original price also $165,000 · 78 DOM/CDOM · 4 beds · 2 full baths · 1,039 square feet · built 1986 · vacant · no HOA shown in Matrix · no current-listing price reduction.

A matched public Bell County notice schedules an **October 6, 2026 trustee sale** for this property. That separate public record may create deadline pressure, but it does not prove the seller will discount the home.

**Estimated payment:** about **$1,610 per month** under the assumptions below. This is an estimate, not a lender quote.

**Important:** a scheduled trustee sale is not a completed foreclosure or bank-owned sale. It may be cured, postponed, or canceled. Recheck live MLS status, notice status, title, condition, and current comparable sales before acting.

## The play this issue

Separate the clean offer from the low offer. Use current comparable sales and the documented listing history to explain the number, include solid proof of funds or preapproval, and keep the terms as simple as the buyer's risk tolerance allows. Price alone should not be a reason to waive inspection, title, financing, or other material protections.
