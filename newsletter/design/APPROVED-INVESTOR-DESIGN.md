# Temple TX Investor Brief approved design

## Current selection

- **Status:** Approved as the working design standard
- **Selected:** July 19, 2026
- **Direction:** Investor Desk Brief / premium tech underwriting desk
- **Canonical prototype:** `temple-investor-brief-v1-desk-tech.html`
- **Publication:** Temple TX Investor Brief

This selection governs the biweekly Temple TX Investor Brief until Taylor approves a replacement. It approves the visual and content system only. It is not approval to stage, publish, or send an issue.

## Elements to preserve

1. Opportunity-scan status bar and issue metadata
2. Three-class executive shortlist: multifamily, single-family rental, and specialty play
3. Explicit desk status for every candidate: underwrite, watch, pass, or no buy
4. Tabular deal metrics with ranges instead of false precision
5. Plain-English investment thesis for why each property ranks
6. Visible financing, income, operating, vacancy, maintenance, and capital assumptions as applicable
7. Break Point / What Kills the Deal risk panel
8. Verify Next due-diligence checklist
9. Portfolio-level comparison across all three opportunities
10. Capital conditions or financing context for the edition
11. One rotating investor tip, post-mortem, operator note, or value-add idea
12. Taylor's desk note and reply-first `DEALS` CTA

## Visual standard

- Near-black graphite canvas with midnight panels
- Electric mint for live signals and constructive status
- Restrained signal blue for structure, emphasis, and model annotations
- Muted red reserved for material risks and walk-away thresholds
- System sans-serif headlines paired with compact monospaced labels and tabular numerals
- Hairline borders, quiet technical labels, and disciplined dashboard hierarchy
- Dense but scannable information with clear separation between thesis, numbers, assumptions, and risk
- Mobile stacking without horizontal overflow while preserving compact two-column metric grids
- Photography is optional; underwriting evidence and decision clarity take priority over decoration

## Production rules

- Bracketed copy, colored source tags, and workflow labels are production aids and must be removed from the delivered email.
- MLS facts must come from the current `/Users/taylordasch_1/market-monitor/` source set and be date-stamped.
- Listing status must be rechecked before staging and again before sending.
- Rent, resale, financing, tax, insurance, vacancy, management, maintenance, rehab, and restriction assumptions must identify their source or be labeled as estimates.
- If a class has no defensible candidate, use **No buy this round** and explain the failure instead of manufacturing a winner.
- The investor recipient list must remain separate from Temple TX Insider.
- Every issue still requires separate copy, assumption, link, recipient, staging, and send approval.
- Beehiiv remains the delivery layer so unsubscribe, suppression, bounce, and analytics controls remain intact.
