This selection governs the monthly Temple TX Insider market update until Taylor approves a replacement. It approves the visual and content system only. It is not approval to stage, publish, or send an issue.

## Elements to preserve

1. Defining statistic and plain-English headline
2. Taylor's immediate analyst verdict
3. Buyer, homeowner, and watch-next briefing strip
4. Five-number executive dashboard and market position
5. Buyer and owner decision brief
6. Home of the Month property study, including the honest catch
7. Best Opportunity Deal Desk, including best fit, opportunity, catch, and verdict
8. Three-property watchlist
9. Neighborhood or price-band watch
10. One practical monthly action
11. Rotating local or homeowner feature
12. Taylor's field note and reply-first CTA

## Visual standard

- Deep forest, near-black ink, warm paper, and restrained brass
- Georgia-led editorial headlines with system-sans body copy
- Strong light/dark section pacing
- Square, precise data modules with minimal decorative chrome
- One dominant idea per section
- Mobile stacking without horizontal overflow
- Photography used as evidence or editorial focus, not decoration

- Bracketed copy and colored source tags are production aids and must be removed from the delivered email.
- Real market figures must come from the current `/Users/taylordasch_1/market-monitor/` source set and be date-stamped.
- Home of the Month and Best Opportunity status must be rechecked before staging and again before sending.
- The approved design is the starting point, but every issue still requires separate copy, link, recipient, staging, and send approval.
- Beehiiv remains the delivery layer so unsubscribe, suppression, bounce, and analytics controls remain intact.
