## The ten-minute monthly conversation

Taylor only needs to answer these six prompts:

1. What felt different in the market this month?
2. Which home deserves Home of the Month, and why?
3. Which listing looks like the best buyer opportunity, and what is the catch?
4. What quick tip have you repeated to clients lately?
5. What local or personal extra should make this issue feel different?
6. What should readers reply about?

Codex handles the data pull, calculations, writing, layout, verification, and Beehiiv staging after those answers.

## Rotating-extra queue

1. Homeowner value-add project
2. Local business or community note
3. Neighborhood micro-spotlight
4. Approved client or home story
5. Tax, insurance, or maintenance calendar
6. Relocation reality check

Never force the rotation when a stronger current topic exists. Client stories and personal shoutouts require permission.
