## Results

Nine new, non-duplicative review items are documented in `supplemental-direct-review.csv`; eight remain in the final public-content findings after the currently unlisted `D-AUTH-05` record is excluded by the final build:

- `D-AUTH-01` and `D-AUTH-02`: direct family-suitability language.
- `D-AUTH-03` through `D-AUTH-08`: concrete investment, equity, rent, price, rate, incentive, or market-statistic claims needing evidence or terms review.
- `D-AUTH-09`: direct safety/avoidance wording retained only for source and neutral-consistency review.

`D-AUTH-05` is preserved in the evidence supplement for chain-of-review purposes but is not counted or reported as a current public-content item because `rtxESd1DzFk` was unlisted at final catalog build time.

No item is presented as a legal violation. In particular, rate/incentive rows do not infer Regulation Z, RESPA, FTC, compensation, or lender-role elements; school/crime/safety content remains in the source/consistency lane; and military/retiree language alone is not treated as protected-class discrimination.

Automated screen matches alone were rejected where the human review showed ordinary feature copy, puffery, personal biography, a conditional statutory illustration, generic military targeting, or no discrete claim. The homestead-exemption video, for example, uses a conditional local-tax-rate illustration and directs viewers to the county appraisal district; it was not escalated from a cue hit alone.

## Limitations

The review is text-based and offline. It does not establish audio accuracy for automatic captions, visual disclosures, current availability, authorial responsibility, data accuracy, lender/broker status, compensation, transaction facts, intent, or liability. The 24 inaccessible IDs and the six metadata-only records require recovery/playback before any caption- or visual-based conclusion.
