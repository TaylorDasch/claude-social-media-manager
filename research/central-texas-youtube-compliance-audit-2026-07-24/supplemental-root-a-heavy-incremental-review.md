## Guardrails applied

- Family/children, school, neighborhood, and safety language was elevated only when the public wording directly framed a place or dwelling as suitable for families/children. Military, VA, PCS, age, retiree, and young-professional references were not treated as independent Fair Housing categories.
- No Regulation Z, RESPA, FTC, VA, false-advertising, investment-guarantee, or Fair Housing violation is concluded. The builder/lender rows are limited to current material-terms/frame-level verification, and the lender-event cues did not establish the elements needed for a referral/thing-of-value conclusion.
- Historic posting dates do not prove a listing, rate, incentive, or program is current. The review does not infer stale status or lack of authority from an old listing or sparse public path.
- Ranking, volume, income, credit, and guarantee language is treated as a source, qualification, broker, lender, or counsel review item only. Visual overlays, linked pages, program documents, current authorization, and underlying records remain unobserved unless specifically stated in the row.
