## Specific classification check: age and military

**PASS.** The draft does not misclassify either category under the cited rules:

- **Age:** FHA §3604(c), TREC §531.19, and Texas Property Code ch. 301 do not identify age as a general protected class. Familial status is separately defined around minors/pregnancy/custody; the older-person housing exception is not a general age-protection rule.
- **Military/VA/PCS:** None of FHA §3604(c), TREC §531.19, or Texas Property Code ch. 301 adds military/veteran/PCS status to its listed protected classes. The report properly limits the “military families” concern to separate household-composition copy, not military status.

## Residual limits

This QA does not decide whether a particular creator is a Texas license holder, whether a channel is controlled by that person, whether a video is an advertisement, whether a transaction is a federally related mortgage loan, whether an Expedia link is compensated, or whether omitted visual/linked disclosures satisfy a rule.
