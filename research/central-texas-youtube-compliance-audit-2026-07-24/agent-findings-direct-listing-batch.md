# Direct-listing / thin-feed batch — evidence log

Audit date: 2026-07-24 (Central). Scope: public uploads dated 2024-07-24 through 2026-07-24. This is an evidence-led screening log, **not** a legal conclusion, TREC finding, or HUD enforcement determination.

## Method and limits

- Reviewed the public in-window title/descriptive metadata returned for the assigned thin-feed channels and every retrievable English auto-caption. The feeds are predominantly address-only listing tours or non-real-estate personal/podcast content; many have no public caption track or meaningful description.
- Titles and captions were not called stale or deceptive solely because the property may now be unavailable. A contemporaneous MLS/listing-history record, a current availability representation, or a later contradictory post is required before making a listing-status concern.
- Channel About text and every public direct-path revealed in the reviewed metadata were checked before disclosure findings. A blank or sparse profile is a channel-level follow-up, not proof that a video frame, end card, or rendered destination lacks compliant disclosure.
- Rule labels: Texas real-estate advertising identity/broker-disclosure follow-up (including TREC Rule 535.155); Fair Housing Act §804(c) / 24 C.F.R. §100.75 familial-status advertising risk; and misleading-advertising/substantiation risk. Objective, sourced school/base/commute information is not itself prohibited.

## Findings

1. **Address-only local listing channel with no visible broker identity in its About profile** — moderate severity; high confidence in the observed profile facts.
   - Channels: [Robert Herrings Jr](https://www.youtube.com/@robertherringsjr4101) (`UCGEXUfJdSCBPtgWeF8Dl4lw`), [Teresa Ingram](https://www.youtube.com/@TeresaIngram-cm9ih) (`UCDKBmfDQL51l_qsamkhGYHw`), and [Joanna Pearce](https://www.youtube.com/@joannapearcetxrealtor) (`UCMKRSmq9b8V07K9y4Zyfx_Q`).
   - Evidence: their current About profiles were blank. Each has in-window local real-estate tours titled only by property address (for example, Robert’s [171 Hurst Place Boulevard](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KZVdx2EsqL0), 2026-07-22; Teresa’s [606 Redbud Drive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XdWkk6bAiWs), 2026-07-23; Joanna’s [1706 Phyllis Drive, Copperas Cove](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iV0aBAwDwMw), 2026-06-26).
   - Rule/risk: TREC advertising identity/broker-disclosure follow-up. The channel/profile itself gives a prospective consumer no visible sponsoring-broker identity or direct disclosure path.
   - Missing context: channel banners, end cards, video descriptions rendered in YouTube, pinned comments, and any brokerage disclosure in the actual video frames. Do not characterize this as a completed violation without that review.

2. **Local listing/lease channel profile lacks a broker identity or disclosure destination** — moderate severity; high confidence in the observed profile facts.
   - Channels: [Sujin Park Henson](https://www.youtube.com/@sujinparkrealtor) (`UCZAiPx4wER-v-Q4avEhVTZg`) and [Spradley Properties](https://www.youtube.com/@spradleyproperties8911) (`UCfS8-4IPB1bN8oN1N2zqsSw`).
   - Evidence: Sujin’s About profile is blank despite in-window local sale/reel titles such as [JUST LISTED in Harker Heights](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gd8C2EtH98I) (2026-06-24). Spradley’s About says only “Your one stop shop for all real estate needs. Whether you are looking to purchase or lease, our team,” while current videos advertise specific Temple/Belton rentals, including [Cozy 1x1 at Autumn Chase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MYkFoAUWeMI) (2026-05-06) and [Your New Start in Temple](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKAqNfEX4ws) (2026-04-16).
   - Rule/risk: TREC advertising identity/broker-disclosure follow-up. This is especially important for lease advertising, but no tenant-selection or discriminatory screening wording was found in the six available Spradley caption tracks.
   - Missing context: rendered channel links, rental-application/landing-page disclosures, broker of record, end cards, and video-frame text.

3. **Familial-status preference language in a listing tour** — moderate severity; high confidence.
   - Video: [2601 Coach Drive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K5Gpv7s8qk4) (`K5Gpv7s8qk4`), Nahir Diaz REALTOR®, 2026-05-13.
   - Evidence: auto-caption at 00:00:49–00:00:51 calls the property “the perfect stress-free spot for military families looking for proximity.”
   - Rule/risk: FHA §804(c) familial-status advertising risk. Proximity to Fort Cavazos can be described neutrally; branding a property as perfect for “military families” is a preference/suitability statement based on family status.
   - Missing context: neutral property features, on-screen wording, and whether the listing is offered without any household-composition preference.

4. **Channel-level broker-identity omission in a real-estate listing feed** — moderate severity; high confidence in the observed profile facts.
   - Channel: [Nahir Diaz REALTOR®](https://www.youtube.com/@NahirDiazREALTOR) (`UCW0Vt4ukxSblQWRyLF_l6GA`).
   - Evidence: current About text identifies Nahir Diaz as a REALTOR® but does not identify a sponsoring broker or give a public destination link. The in-window feed contains 18 property tours/listing promotions.
   - Rule/risk: TREC advertising identity/broker-disclosure follow-up. This is not a conclusion that every video fails to disclose brokerage information; captions/metadata did not supply a broker identity.
   - Missing context: banner, thumbnail/end-card details, individual description panels, and any external profile link rendered by YouTube.

5. **Unsubstantiated “top 1%” ranking claim in brokerage-adjacent podcast promotion** — high severity; high confidence in the quoted language.
   - Videos: [Twin Cast: What Season Are You In?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOOJ9W4P4NE) (`zOOJ9W4P4NE`), 2026-06-05; [Overcomer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MtSaZ_wLYkM) (`MtSaZ_wLYkM`), 2026-05-29; and similarly worded current descriptions in the channel feed.
   - Evidence: the descriptions state, “We’re Cherie and Nicole … As **top 1% realtors** with over a decade of experience …” without identifying the ranking publisher, geography, period, metric, or denominator.
   - Rule/risk: Texas misleading-advertising/substantiation risk. A comparative/ranking claim needs a current, verifiable source and clear scope; the claim appears in content promoted to Central Texas buyers, sellers, and investors.
   - Missing context: ranking organization, reporting period, population/metric, supporting award documentation, and whether a clear source appears in video frames or the linked site.

6. **Team/profile identity and mortgage-affiliation disclosure follow-up** — low-to-moderate severity; high confidence in the observed information, not a final legal classification.
   - Channel: [Twins Realty Team](https://www.youtube.com/@Gottwinsrealty) (`UCiDEHqXl4HEWQZ4mZ1nsbtQ`).
   - Evidence: About text markets a first-time buyer education service but names neither the individual license holders nor a sponsoring broker. Descriptions link `https://twinsrealty.net`; its static page identifies “TWINS REALTY TEAM — Your Texas Real Estate & Mortgage Experts” and contains customer text referring to CMG Mortgage, but this pass did not establish TREC/IABS/CPN or mortgage-license disclosures on the rendered site.
   - Rule/risk: TREC advertising identity/broker-disclosure and mortgage-affiliation disclosure follow-up. No RESPA Section 8 kickback is inferred from the relationship reference; a referral/compensation arrangement cannot be determined from public marketing alone.
   - Missing context: brokerage name and license disclosures, NMLS/loan-originator disclosures if applicable, ownership/referral agreements, compensation, consumer choice language, and visible page/footer disclosures.

## No finding / do-not-overstate ledger

- **Listing status:** No stale-listing/availability finding was logged for Robert, Sujin, Joanna, Teresa, Spradley, or Nahir. Historic address videos, “for sale,” “coming soon,” and price-drop titles need MLS/listing-history validation before calling them stale. The review did not receive that evidence.
- **Property authority / sold role:** Address-only tours do not establish whether the uploader is listing broker, buyer agent, owner, or authorized marketing vendor. No authority claim was inferred.
- **Privacy:** Public street addresses in marketing videos are ordinarily listing information. No evidence indicated disclosure of nonpublic personal information, occupied-home security details, or an unauthorized address.
- **At Home in Texas, Rheajane Taylor, Lovejoy Homes Realty:** At Home’s current feed is third-party ColdwellBankerHomes.com listing material outside the targeted local-creator pattern; Rheajane’s seven uploads were personal travel/shopping; Lovejoy’s retrieved feed is DFW/North Texas rather than Temple/Belton/Killeen/Harker Heights/Copperas Cove/Nolanville. No local compliance finding was logged from those records.
