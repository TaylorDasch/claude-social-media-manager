## Rule applied

- `full_channel_local_creator`: the creator or operating business is actually based in Temple, Belton, Killeen, Harker Heights, Copperas Cove, or Nolanville. The entire public catalog in the audit window belongs in scope, even when an individual video is not named for one of those cities.
- `direct_target_videos_only`: the creator is based elsewhere or operates a broad syndication/statewide channel, but individual videos directly concern one of the six target cities.
- `exclude_nonhousing_or_unconfirmed`: reserved for channels whose housing connection or target-market connection could not be confirmed. None of the 47 met that condition.

Current official office/location evidence received the most weight. Where a brokerage's supervising-office address did not reflect the individual creator's residence, repeated first-person local activity, current transactions, association records, and a sustained local catalog were considered together. A service-area claim or one local listing by itself was not enough for full-catalog treatment.

## Evidence and limitations

The adjudication used the audit workspace's candidate table, channel descriptions, search results, authenticated catalog, and flat root shards, supplemented by current public creator, brokerage, association, and business profiles where the local files were ambiguous. It is a public-evidence scope decision as of July 24, 2026, not a legal conclusion about any creator or video.

This file adjudicates which videos should be reviewed. It does not itself decide whether any content violates TREC, fair-housing, advertising, licensing, or other rules.
