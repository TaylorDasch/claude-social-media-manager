## Rejected cue categories

The following automated or textual cues were reviewed and not separately escalated:

- “Your Home Sold Guaranteed” in a channel or video name alone. `ROOT-AUTH-06` is included only because the full description makes a separate, repeated “will buy yours” offer.
- Age-only, retiree, senior, young-professional, military, VA, PCS, or Fort Cavazos references. Military/age wording is retained only when an independent family/children suitability statement or a separately stated savings/incentive claim supplies the concern.
- School, crime, safety, neighborhood, and “best/safest” mentions without a direct household-routing statement, protected-class tailoring, or another independently actionable claim.
- Rate, lender, builder, title, preferred-provider, referral, affiliate, FTC, RESPA, or Regulation Z keywords that did not publicly establish the required elements. `ROOT-AUTH-07` is limited to a material-terms/frame-level verification item and makes no Regulation Z, RESPA, or FTC conclusion.
- Present-tense listing titles, “for sale,” “today,” availability, MLS/address details, or historic listing references. Publication age alone does not establish stale status or listing authority.
- Ordinary puffery, broad market commentary, and the already-adjudicated seller-performance/case-study issue represented in `S-AUTH-03`; those were not re-listed merely because another automated cue matched.
- Caption files that contained only music, empty text, or no useful context; those are coverage limitations rather than findings.
