  direct family/children suitability claim. Age and military status alone were
  not treated as protected-class findings.

  trigger terms, visual prominence, compensation, referral, or a material

  of a missing notice; “best realtor” was negated; and “number one thing” was
  not a ranking claim. These did not support a row.
- **Puffery and unsupported broad inference:** “top listing agent,” generic
  investment-opportunity language, “high traffic,” “low tax rate,” and similar
  non-quantified promotional wording were not elevated without a concrete,
  actionable comparative or financial claim. Brand or builder names alone did
  not establish a guarantee, endorsement, affiliate arrangement, or referral.
