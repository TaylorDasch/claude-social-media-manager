**Method:** I compared the current authenticated `authenticated-root` metadata and English JSON3 captions against `master-findings-adjudicated.csv`. I retained only material, public-record additions not already represented in that master. Duplicate uploads of the same substantive asset are grouped. These are apparent advertising/compliance concerns for broker/counsel review—not findings of a legal violation, discrimination, misleading advertising, RESPA, Regulation Z, or FTC liability. Captions and descriptions do not establish intent, authorship, visual disclosures, factual accuracy, listing status, compensation, licensure, or liability.
