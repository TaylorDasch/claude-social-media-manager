## Contextual rejections

The following were intentionally not turned into additional findings:

- Age, veteran, military, PCS, and military-family references standing alone.
  They are not FHA/TREC § 531.19 protected-class findings; the two Eric rows
  are included only for the separate direct “perfect for families” wording.
- School, crime, safety, and location information without protected-class
  tailoring or a direct discriminatory/steering context. For example,
  CazFamRealEstate's “good school area”/“great schools” statement was not
  escalated.
- Rate, down-payment, lender, builder, incentive, preferred-provider, or
  referral cues that did not establish a credit offer, material connection,
  thing of value, agreement, referral, or covered settlement service. No
  Regulation Z, RESPA, or FTC conclusion was inferred.
- Ordinary listing facts, current lease details, conditional education, or
  puffery. This includes the At Home in Texas duplex metadata, JJ Realty
  Group's expressly hypothetical owner-financing discussion, and caption
  phrases such as “bedroom number one,” which are not ranking claims.
- Old or present-tense listing titles and availability language. The review did
  not infer stale status, current availability, listing authority, or a
  misleading advertisement from publication age alone.
- The full-channel lane was reviewed on the creator-based scope: every public,
  in-window upload from a credibly local creator was eligible even if an
  individual property or topic was outside the six target cities. This
  correction produced `GDI-04`. Asad Shaikh's Leander “ideal for families”
  description was reconciled as a duplicate of the existing Asad family-copy
  group, and Vanessa Thomas's generic “Family-Friendly Cities” title was a
  duplicate of her family-suitability wording group rather than a separate
  concern. Direct-only lanes remained limited to their discovered local videos.

## Dedupe and evidence limits

Every proposed group was compared against
`master-findings-adjudicated.csv`, all existing `supplemental-*-review.csv`
files, and the `agent-findings-*.md` reports. Existing rows were not expanded
merely because the same channel supplied another similar phrase. The CSV
identifies the exact video IDs, URLs, dates, public wording, and local
authenticated source paths used for each new group.

Captions can be automatic and metadata can be syndicated. Neither alone proves
authorship, intent, program availability, compensation, an actual credit offer,
or a legal conclusion. Visual playback, direct-link disclosure paths, current
listing status, and supporting brokerage/lender records remain necessary for
any final compliance decision.
