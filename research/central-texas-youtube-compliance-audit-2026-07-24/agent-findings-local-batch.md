# Local channel batch — evidence log

Audit date: 2026-07-24 (Central). Scope: public uploads dated 2024-07-24 through 2026-07-24, inclusive. This is an evidence-led screening log, not a legal conclusion or a TREC/HUD enforcement finding. “High” means the quoted language is plainly present; it does not mean an agency has adjudicated it.

## Method and limitations

- Cataloged every public in-window upload on the five assigned channels (272 total): Mallory Anthony 53; John Hayes 28; Mathew Dick / Military Living in Central Texas 46; Jacob Martinez / LIVING IN CENTRAL TEXAS 40; Trent Babb 105.
- Reviewed titles, available descriptions, channel profiles, and every available English auto-caption in the in-window set. Caption availability is not the same as a completed visual review: on-screen text, thumbnails, spoken language omitted by auto-captions, and destination-page disclosures still need manual playback checks.
- Profile/direct-link check completed before considering disclosure findings. Mallory’s profile identifies RE/MAX Temple-Belton; Mathew’s profile identifies eXp Realty and a TREC number; Trent’s identifies Ranch Roads Realty. John’s profile is blank; Jacob’s profile identifies neither agent nor brokerage. Do **not** treat the profile facts as a determination that a linked site has, or lacks, an IABS/CPN footer.
- Rule labels used below: Fair Housing Act §804(c) / 24 C.F.R. §100.75 (advertising that expresses a preference based on a protected class, including familial status); Fair-housing steering risk (subjective neighborhood/school/safety advice); and Texas misleading-advertising / substantiation risk (TREC advertising rules). The HUD 2026 crime-and-school nuance is applied: objective, sourced school or public-safety information is not itself prohibited. The flagged issue is unsourced subjective comparative language, demographic preference, or a recommendation tied to a protected-class proxy.

### John Hayes
