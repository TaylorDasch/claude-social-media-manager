# Central Texas YouTube public-content compliance audit

**Draft date:** July 24, 2026  
**Public-review window:** July 24, 2024 through July 24, 2026, inclusive  
**Scope:** Publicly accessible YouTube channel/profile, title, description, caption, and limited direct-link evidence collected during the audit. The Taylor channels `UCqrLPGPR9eV7QUfK02dwtpQ` and `UCKuVz8ytHECKEAyRacDpm1g` were excluded.

## Outcome

This public-content audit identified **apparent concerns for broker/compliance review**, principally in four areas:

1. direct family/children suitability wording in housing advertising;
2. measurable investment, savings, valuation, ranking, and seller-performance claims without visible support or material qualifications in the reviewed record;
3. public identity/incentive/rate statements that need targeted broker, lender, or records review; and
4. school, crime, safety, and neighborhood-recommendation language that warrants neutral-source and consistency review.

This audit **does not determine that any person or channel violated a law, rule, or regulation.** Public text and captions cannot establish intent, authorization, current listing status, compensation, licensure, a lender's role, the accuracy of underlying figures, or whether a disclosure was visible elsewhere in the video or direct-link path.

No critical, public-record item establishes the elements of a Fair Housing, TREC, RESPA, Regulation Z, FTC, or other legal violation. The highest practical priority is prompt review and neutral revision of direct family/children suitability copy, followed by substantiation/terms review for concrete financial, ranking, rebate, and rate claims.

## What this report means—and does not mean

“Apparent concern” and “issue-spotting” mean that the cited public wording warrants review against the complete published asset and the responsible broker's records. They do not mean that a claim is false, that a creator intended to discriminate, or that a regulator would reach any particular result.

In particular:

- A title, description, or automatic caption establishes only that the quoted wording was publicly retrieved. Automatic captions should be checked against the audio before use in a disciplinary or enforcement setting.
- A public reference to a lender, builder, title company, mortgage program, incentive, or affiliate does **not** establish a kickback, referral-for-value agreement, material connection, or other RESPA/FTC issue.
- A rate, APR, payment, down-payment, or financing reference does **not** establish a Regulation Z issue without the complete final ad, advertiser/lender role, trigger terms, and prominent visual disclosures.
- School, crime, safety, military, VA, PCS, or geographic content is not, by itself, a Fair Housing finding. The review escalates only the wording and consistency questions described below.
- A sparse YouTube About page does not by itself establish that broker identification, the Consumer Protection Notice (CPN), or Information About Brokerage Services (IABS) is unavailable. TREC permits certain social-media disclosures through a readily noticeable direct path; banners, descriptions, end cards, and linked pages matter.

### Medium-priority source, substantiation, and consistency review

These items warrant practical review but have meaningful public-record limitations.

- **Seller and market comparisons.** Stephen Harris, [Central Texas Homeowners Should Watch This Closely](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5i1Q2eGhiS0), 00:08:45–00:10:00, describes 92.5%, 99.7%, and “100% on average” list-to-close performance and says a pricing approach will “likely sell very very fast.” PRC Properties Group, [Should You Buy a House Now or Wait for 2026?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BjOyE9UrCtk), 00:02:34–00:02:51 and 00:03:44–00:03:50, cites a 3–5% forecast and says waiting “almost always” means a higher price. These are apparent substantiation/qualification issues, not proof of a false claim.
- **Valuation, “free,” and ranking statements.** Mallory Anthony's [Priced Below Appraisal / Instant Equity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vo-XdLuZAJo), Mathew Dick's [FREE Solar Panels](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ys6_k7DqvlI), Linnemann Realty's “fastest growing” About copy, and Sandee Payne's [#1 RV Resort in Texas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fks5Vr6nQHo) should be supported by a current source, date, method, and clear scope. “Free” solar does not establish that a lease, lien, or PPA exists; that is a records question.
- **Deposit-alternative terms.** Expert Rentals & Property Management's [9013 Sage Valley](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=83phZrxwie8) description says “**KEEP MONEY IN YOUR POCKET WITH SECURITY DEPOSIT ALTERNATIVE POWERED BY OBLIGO**.” Review the actual program fee, eligibility, alternatives, lease flow, and traditional-deposit option. The public description does not establish a rental-law violation.
- **School, crime, safety, and neighborhood language.** Examples include Stephen Harris's [Harker Heights vs Killeen vs Copperas Cove](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rv_Mx_ptug), 00:03:49–00:05:22 and 00:09:56–00:10:03, and Jacob Martinez's [10 MUST-ASK Questions BEFORE Choosing a Neighborhood](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yooPsq8qio), 00:01:51–00:01:53. Review neutral source/date, consistent treatment, and removal of direct “avoid/go here,” demographic-composition, or household-tailored recommendations. These passages are not reported as Fair Housing violations on this evidence.
- **Direct disclosure-path follow-up.** Browser review found that Nestled With Whitney's current YouTube profile and linked `nestledonline.com` pages reviewed did not expose a sponsoring broker, IABS, or CPN link. This is a stronger apparent path concern than a bare About-page omission, but still needs banner, video-frame, and broker-record review before any final classification.

### Low-priority copy cleanup or verification

Terms such as “best,” “leading,” “amazing,” “stress-free,” and similar opinion-style promotion ordinarily read as puffery unless surrounding content supplies a measurable assertion. Current stale “For Sale” or “Active Listing” titles also require MLS/status confirmation before they can be treated as inaccurate. These are practical cleanup and verification queues, not public conclusions.

## Methodology

The audit team reviewed the supplied evidence records for public material in the two-year window. Depending on the channel lane, the work included public channel inventory, titles, descriptions, available English automatic captions, selected timestamp-level transcript review, current About-page text, and limited direct-profile/site browser checks. The team normalized the source findings against a common review matrix and excluded the two Taylor channel IDs listed above.

The applied evidence standard was intentionally conservative:

1. quote the public wording, URL, date, and timestamp when available;
2. classify the item as an apparent concern, source/substantiation review, or verification-only item;
3. record missing facts that prevent a conclusion; and
4. avoid presenting unverified visual, compensation, authorization, listing-status, lender, or private-communication facts as established.

The source reports include broad metadata/caption review in several lanes, targeted transcript review in others, and a partial RSS/title-only lane where YouTube bot controls prevented comprehensive retrieval. The signed-in pass has not yet produced final authenticated coverage figures.

## Limitations

This is not legal advice, an agency determination, an enforcement referral, or a finding of misconduct. It is a public-content issue-spotting report with the following material limits:

- Content can change after collection; a current page may differ from the evidence captured for the audit.
- Automatic captions may be incomplete or inaccurate; audio needs confirmation before reliance.
- The review did not authenticate or verify all thumbnails, title cards, lower thirds, end cards, pinned comments, maps, paid placements, captions, linked pages, or current statuses.
- Public content cannot prove client-specific steering, discriminatory intent, a protected-class preference in actual service, listing authority, privacy facts, a referral agreement, compensation, lender/creditor status, NMLS validity, rate availability, APR/loan terms, or the validity of performance data.
- The remaining-local-channel lane had a documented no-cookie bot challenge and RSS/title-only fallback. Its inventory is expressly partial for in-window coverage.
- Final authenticated coverage and visual-review counts are pending and marked **TODO** above.
