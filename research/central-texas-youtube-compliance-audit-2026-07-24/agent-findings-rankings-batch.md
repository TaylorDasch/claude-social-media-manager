## Scope, rule frame, and methodology

The requested window is 2024-07-24 through 2026-07-24 inclusive. Channel-video lists were retrieved with `yt-dlp`; each in-window public video was then queried for current metadata, full description, and available automatic English captions. Current About-page text and reachable direct-site disclosure paths were also checked.

This is an evidence/risk memo, not a legal conclusion. “Concern” means the public claim lacks visible supporting criteria, qualification, source, or disclosure in the retrieved record and should be reviewed by the sponsoring broker or counsel; it does not establish falsity, intent, or a rule violation.

Relevant standards:

- TREC Rule 535.155 and TRELA §1101.652(b)(23): advertisements must not be misleading and must identify the license holder/team and broker in the required manner.
- TREC Rule 535.154: team/assumed business names must not mislead the public into believing a team offers brokerage services independent of its sponsoring broker.
- FTC Act §5: commercial claims must be truthful, not misleading, and supported by a reasonable basis. This is a general advertising-principles screen, not an FTC determination.
- RESPA/Regulation X and Truth in Lending/Regulation Z: referral, compensation, and creditor/loan-advertising facts cannot be inferred from the available record; no such violation is alleged here.

TREC context verified from the [advertising FAQ](https://www.trec.texas.gov/node/323), [social-media guidance](https://www.trec.texas.gov/article/what-you-need-know-comply-our-social-media-rules), and [IABS guidance](https://www.trec.texas.gov/node/743).

## Remediation standard

For rankings and performance claims, name the issuer, category, year, market, methodology, and source/link—or narrow the language to a verifiable award title. For seller-performance claims, use a dated, broker-approved MLS study with clear comparator/property controls or avoid unqualified “faster”/“more money” language. Place the sponsoring broker and CPN/IABS path where a reasonable YouTube user can reach it directly.
