# Adjudication notes — Central Texas YouTube public-content audit

**Prepared:** 2026-07-24  
**Purpose:** Normalize the issue-spotting reports against `compliance-review-matrix.md`. This is not legal advice, an agency determination, or a conclusion that any creator violated a law or rule. “Priority” means the order in which a broker/compliance professional should review or remediate a public statement.

## Controlling guardrails

1. **Use the recorded words, not an inference.** An automatic caption, title, or description establishes that the quoted public wording was retrieved; it does not establish intent, a visual disclosure failure, authorial responsibility for syndicated text, a current listing status, compensation, licensing, or liability.
2. **Do not call ordinary school/crime content a Fair Housing violation.** Under the matrix’s HUD-2026 standard, school-quality, crime-rate, and safety information alone is not escalated. Direct "avoid/go here" guidance, demographic-composition commentary, or a recommendation tailored to a protected characteristic remains a broker/counsel issue-spotting item, not a violation finding on this record.
3. **Treat direct family/children suitability language differently.** In a dwelling advertisement, “perfect for families,” “family-friendly,” “designed for a growing family,” and comparable statements are avoidable familial-status preference language. The public wording supports remediation review even though this audit cannot establish an unlawful preference, exclusion, intent, or enforcement outcome.
4. **Age is not an FHA/TREC §531.19 protected class.** “Retirees,” “young professionals,” “younger couple,” “empty nester,” and “senior” references are not, without more, FHA/TREC discrimination findings. None of the source reports establishes a 55+ / Housing for Older Persons Act qualification, nor does any establish that children were excluded. Do not label these as HOPA violations; retain only any independent familial-status wording.
5. **Military status is not an FHA/TREC §531.19 protected class.** “Near Fort Cavazos,” PCS, VA, military, veteran, or military-family targeting is not by itself a Fair Housing violation. When “military families” is used as a dwelling-suitability statement, it may be reviewed only for the separate familial-status wording—not as military-status discrimination.
6. **Do not infer RESPA, FTC, or affiliate compensation.** A builder, lender, mortgage company, rebate, restaurant, product, interview, or preferred-lender mention does not establish a thing of value, agreement/understanding, referral, covered settlement service, or material connection. Those items stay verification-only unless the public record supplies the missing elements.
7. **Do not infer a Regulation Z breach from a rate reference alone.** The retrieved record generally does not establish creditor/advertiser status, an actual credit offer, complete trigger terms, or visual proximity/prominence. “APR” is a rate expression, not by itself proof that a particular set of additional terms was legally triggered or omitted. Rate/down-payment/incentive items are urgent frame-level lender/broker verification items, not violations in this report.
8. **Honor TREC’s social/direct-link path.** A sparse YouTube About page is not enough to say that broker name, IABS, or CPN is absent. The finding must account for a readily noticeable direct public profile/link path and the actual video/banner/end-card. The current browser check resolves PRC’s path; it materially strengthens, but does not finalize, Whitney’s profile-path concern.
9. **Separate measurable claims from puffery.** “Top 1%,” “#1,” “faster for more money,” stated price/return/savings figures, a specific percentage, and defined comparative performance are capable of substantiation review. “Best,” “leading,” “amazing,” “stress-free,” “steal,” “great,” and similar opinion-style promotion are ordinarily low-priority puffery unless the surrounding copy makes a measurable claim.

## Final-report wording model

> The cited public wording presents an apparent advertising/compliance concern and is prioritized for broker or counsel review. This audit does not determine intent, compensation, lender status, licensure, the accuracy of underlying data, the availability of visual disclosures, or legal liability.

Avoid “violated,” “illegal,” “steering violation,” “RESPA violation,” “TILA violation,” “HOPA violation,” “missing broker disclosure,” or “misleading” as a factual conclusion unless an independently verified record supports every necessary element.
