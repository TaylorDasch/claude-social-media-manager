# Graphics Brief

## Visual System

Premium field-journal aesthetic: warm neutral footage, charcoal document panels, white type, and one restrained survey-red accent. The recurring motif is a thin surveyor's line that traces boundaries and connects each observation to its source.

## Required Cards — Five Total

### G01 — A/B/C Cold Open

- One wide, real lot frame with simple `A`, `B`, and `C` markers.
- No builder logos, prices, or defect labels.

### G02 — Five-Check Rail + Proof Boundary

1. Water/grade questions
2. Orientation
3. Documented constraints
4. Street position/access
5. Future edge/adjacency

Show one active item at a time; keep the rail under 18% of frame.

For the water check, expand the rail into this three-step lower-third:

`WHAT I CAN SEE → WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS → WHO CONFIRMS IT`

This is the compliance and credibility signature of the video.

### G03 — Plot Plan, Orientation, and Documented Constraints

- Use the source document's north arrow and the confirmed house placement.
- Trace only lines shown on the source: boundary, shown building lines, and documented easements.
- Caption: `PREFERENCE TEST — NOT AN ENERGY-SAVINGS PROMISE`.
- Permanent overlay label: `ILLUSTRATIVE - NOT A SURVEY, SITE PLAN, OR BUILDABILITY APPROVAL`.
- Do not place a hypothetical pool, shed, fence, or addition.

### G04 — Future Edge

- Present-day field frame on left; dated official plan on right.
- Banner: `CURRENT RECORD - PLANS AND CONTROLLING DOCUMENTS CAN CHANGE`.
- Identify document type: recorded final plat, approved preliminary plat, pending application, adopted plan, zoning, or recorded easement/restriction.
- Show issuer, status, instrument/application number when available, approval/recording date, and access date.

### G05 — Decision Matrix and CTA

Rows are five checks; columns are A/B/C. Keep two result rows visually distinct:

- `BUYER FIT: FITS / TRADEOFF / CONFLICTS`
- `PROOF: VERIFIED / NEEDS DOCUMENT / NEEDS PROFESSIONAL`

Do not total a score. Transition the matrix into:

- `TEXT LOT`
- `254-718-4249`
- `Community + up to 3 lot numbers`

Small line: `Do not post a full address publicly.`

## Lower-Third Format

`SOURCE • TYPE/STATUS • INSTRUMENT/APPLICATION • EFFECTIVE/RECORD DATE • ACCESSED DATE`

Examples:

- `FEMA MSC • PANEL [#] / ZONE [X] • EFFECTIVE [DATE] • ACCESSED [DATE]`
- `RECORDED FINAL PLAT • INSTRUMENT [#] • RECORDED [DATE] • ACCESSED [DATE]`
- `CITY OF [CITY] • [DOCUMENT TYPE/STATUS] • [APPLICATION #] • [DATE]`

## Accessibility

- Minimum 4.5:1 text contrast.
- Never convey Buyer Fit or Proof Status by color alone; include icon and word.
- Keep citations readable for at least 3 seconds.
- Avoid flashing hazard effects.

## Deliverables

- 4K transparent overlays and 1080p edit versions for the five required cards.
- Editable source files.
- One 1280 × 720 primary thumbnail; build test variants only after the proof is known.
- Defer vertical and social exports until the long-form cut is locked.
