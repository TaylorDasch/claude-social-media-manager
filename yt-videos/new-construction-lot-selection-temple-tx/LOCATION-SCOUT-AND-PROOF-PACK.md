# Location Scout and Proof Pack

## Goal

Find three comparable lots that create a real decision without manufacturing drama.

## Selection Rules

Prefer three lots in one subdivision or phase and use one fixed floor plan with confirmed placement on all three lots so the comparison is controlled. The lots should differ in at least three visible/documented ways, such as:

- Rectangular versus tapered/corner geometry.
- Different rear or side orientation.
- Different relationship to an inlet, swale, easement, utility corridor, road stub, future phase, or subdivision edge.
- Different entrance/collector position.
- Different rear/side sightline.

Do not choose a lot merely because it makes a builder look bad. Do not promise one conflict in advance.

## Reconnaissance Only

The July 13 MLS export shows recent new-build concentration in areas including Pecan Creek, Three Creeks, Grove at Lakewood Ranch, Hubbard Branch, Mesa Ridge, Pepper Creek Crossing, Hills of Westwood, and Parks at Westfield. These are scouting candidates—not claims of availability, permission, or suitability.

Verify the exact location independently before filming. Avoid saying a lot is for sale or reservable in the episode.

## Permission Script

`I am producing a buyer-education video about how to compare lot orientation, plats, easements, access, and future phases. I will not discuss prices, incentives, builder rankings, or alleged defects. May I film Lots [identifiers] from [approved areas] on [date/time]? I will blur lot identifiers, workers, buyers, plates, and private documents. Please confirm any required escort, PPE, drone restriction, or review condition in writing.`

Do not grant a builder editorial approval over Taylor's independent conclusions. Permission covers access/filming, not the right to silently rewrite the analysis. Broker approval still governs Taylor's advertising and agency compliance.

## Document Packet Per Lot

- Exact legal lot/block identifier.
- Current recorded plat or survey.
- One fixed floor plan and applicable builder plot/site plan showing confirmed placement.
- Building lines/setbacks and known easements.
- Relevant title/restriction/HOA documents.
- Current FEMA panel/layer anchored to legal-lot coordinates, including panel/map number, zone, effective/access dates, revision/LOMC search, and local floodplain contact when material.
- Applicable city/county drainage or engineering document that can be shared.
- Current adjoining-edge record classified as recorded final plat, approved preliminary plat, pending application, adopted plan, zoning, or recorded easement/restriction, with issuer/status/number/dates.
- Parcel-specific taxing entities/special districts only if discussed.
- Address-level FCC/provider check only if internet service is material.
- Written answers to unresolved builder/developer questions.

## Professional Review Question

Send the final drainage/grade segment—not private buyer data—to a licensed inspector or civil engineer and ask:

`Does this wording stay within observable conditions and appropriate due-diligence questions without implying an engineering diagnosis? Does any sentence imply that an inspector, builder, map, or visible condition clears drainage design or grading adequacy? Which sentence should be removed or qualified?`

Record reviewer name, license/role, date, exact language reviewed, and response. Permission is required before identifying the reviewer publicly.

## Go / No-Go

Proceed only if:

- [ ] Access is lawful and safe.
- [ ] Written image-use permission or broker-approved public-record case-study route addresses reverse-identification risk.
- [ ] The synthetic buyer profile is disclosed and no client fact is used without a specific release.
- [ ] One fixed floor plan and confirmed placement makes the orientation/area/sightline comparison valid.
- [ ] At least two non-obvious conclusions have document proof.
- [ ] The lots create a real buyer-specific tradeoff.
- [ ] No conclusion requires Taylor to impersonate an engineer, surveyor, attorney, insurer, or tax professional.
- [ ] The episode remains truthful if no lot receives `Conflicts`.

If these fail, use the one-lot-plus-two-redacted-case-studies fallback and rewrite the hook/thumbnail to match.
