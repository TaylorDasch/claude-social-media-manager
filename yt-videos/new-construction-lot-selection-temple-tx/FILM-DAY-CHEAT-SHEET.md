# Film-Day Cheat Sheet

## The Sentence to Remember

`You can change the house. You cannot move the lot.`

## One Viewer / Decision / Proof

- Viewer: buyer about to reserve a Temple-area new-build lot.
- Decision: which lot fits the synthetic buyer's priorities and which facts still need proof.
- Proof: the same five checks across Lots A, B, and C, using one fixed floor plan and confirmed placement.

## Before Leaving

- [ ] Permission/public-right-of-way plan confirmed in writing.
- [ ] Lots A/B/C selected; status and identifiers kept off camera.
- [ ] Synthetic buyer priorities written on one card; no real client facts.
- [ ] One fixed floor plan plus confirmed plot/site plan for A/B/C.
- [ ] Current phase plat and relevant restrictions obtained.
- [ ] Current FEMA/city/county/planning screenshots saved with dates.
- [ ] Compass app, lav, tripod/gimbal, power bank, lens cloth, sunscreen, water, high-visibility vest/PPE if permission requires it.
- [ ] Phone privacy settings hide notifications.
- [ ] No incentive, price, rate, lot-premium, or availability signage in frame.
- [ ] Broker-approved advertising/profile disclosure path, completed IABS delivery, and current buyer-agreement workflow documented.
- [ ] One-page PDF and stable delivery file/link pass a cross-phone text test.

## Repeat This Shot Sequence at Every Lot

1. Locked 8-second wide from lawful location.
2. Slow front boundary pan.
3. Curb/grade/inlet detail without declaring a defect.
4. Combined side/rear sightline from a lawful, plan-accurate position.
5. Compass/orientation plus confirmed placement.
6. Infrastructure and access/future-edge receipt.

## Language Guardrails

Say:

- `This creates a question.`
- `The current document shows...`
- `For this buyer's priorities...`
- `I would verify this with...`
- `Plans can change; this document is dated...`

Do not say:

- `This will flood.`
- `This soil is bad.`
- `This is the safest/best family lot.`
- `This will appreciate more.`
- `The builder is hiding this.`
- `This is definitely allowed.`
- `Zone X means you're safe.`

## Performance Beats

- Hook: short, direct, no greeting before it.
- After every check, show A/B/C with separate Buyer Fit and Proof Status rows.
- Withhold the final decision until all five checks.
- If the evidence does not support a conflict, do not manufacture one. `Needs Proof` is a real verdict.
- One spoken CTA only: text `LOT` to `254-718-4249`.

## Safety

- No active job-site entry without permission and required PPE.
- No handheld filming while driving.
- No drone without Part 107, registration/Remote ID, airspace compliance, lawful takeoff/landing permission, and operations-over-people compliance.
- Do not record intelligible third-party conversations; avoid minors.
- Blur plates, faces, workers, buyers, street signs, exact lot identifiers, coordinates, QR/barcodes, and private documents; strip geotags.
- If asked to leave, leave immediately and use the desk-case-study fallback.
