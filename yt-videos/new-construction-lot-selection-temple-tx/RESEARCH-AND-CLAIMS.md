# Research and Claims Ledger

## Use Rule

The video teaches a due-diligence method. It does not make a property diagnosis. Every exact-lot statement must come from current film-day observation or a dated, authoritative document.

For every lot-specific assertion, record:

## Approved General Statements

- `A one-hundred-year floodplain means a one-percent annual chance, not one flood every hundred years.`
- `A current FEMA map is one screening source; it is not a promise that water cannot affect a property.`
- `A recorded plat and confirmed builder plot/site plan can reveal documented constraints and change how a buyer understands the apparent remaining area.`
- `A title commitment may list exceptions, restrictions, access issues, and easements that deserve review.`
- `Several taxing units or special districts can apply to one parcel.`
- `FCC availability data is provider-reported and should be confirmed with the provider.`
- `Plans can change, so show the issuing agency and document date.`

## Prohibited or Rewrite-Required Claims

- `This lot floods.` → `This visible condition creates a drainage question; here is what the current record shows and who should evaluate it.`
- `Bad soil.` → `The USDA map shows a mapped soil category; a site-specific professional must assess implications.`
- `The builder hid this.` → `The sales presentation focuses on the home; independently verify the parcel documents.`
- `Best lot / worst lot.` → `Best fit or conflicts for this synthetic buyer's stated priorities.`
- `This lot will appreciate more.` → remove.
- `This community is safe / good for families / has the best schools.` → remove.
- `No MUD/PID` or any rate → use only after exact parcel verification, and only if necessary.
- `Pool-ready` or `shed-ready` → `The buyer should verify whether all documents, design requirements, and professional reviews allow the intended structure.`
- `Gets afternoon shade` → `Offers the more favorable confirmed orientation for this buyer's shade goal`; actual shade requires a defensible sun-path study or repeated observation.
- `This view is permanent` → identify the exact document type/status and say that amendments or later approvals may remain possible.
- Any current incentive, promotion, interest rate, lot premium, price, or availability → remove from this evergreen episode.

## MLS Boundary

The July 13 market-monitor export is reconnaissance only. It does not expose a reliable explicit status field for the new-build subset and must not support claims such as `290 active new homes`. If a location or inventory status appears on camera, verify it independently on film day or crop it out.

## Source Age Rule

- Display source type/status, issuer, identifying number when available, effective/recorded/approval date, and access date on screen.
- Recheck every government map/document within 72 hours of filming if it affects a lot verdict.
- For FEMA, anchor to legal-lot coordinates and archive panel/map number, zone, effective date, access date, revision/LOMC status, and the correct local floodplain contact if material.
- Recheck any exact lot status on film day, but omit status from the final video unless absolutely needed.
- Archive screenshots used in the edit so a future refresh can reproduce the claim.
