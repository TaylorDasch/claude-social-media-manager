## Primary Thumbnail Brief

- Canvas: 1280 × 720.
- Taylor: roughly 40% of frame, waist-up, evaluating—not fake shock.
- Background: one permissioned, de-identified wide frame containing or honestly representing three comparable lots; A/B/C labels are large enough for mobile. Remove street signs, coordinates, unique document numbers, geotags, and current-purchaser identifiers.
- Text: `LOT A, B OR C?` with no subtitle or additional clue.
- Palette: Taylor's premium neutral/charcoal look with one restrained red signal color.
- No house interior, price, interest rate, incentive badge, builder logo, dollar amount, flood graphic, yellow hazard tape, or crowded checklist.

## Conditional `WRONG LOT?` Test

Use `WRONG LOT?` only after the final proof establishes that one verified condition conflicts with the synthetic buyer's named non-negotiable. If no lot reaches that evidence-led result, the variant is prohibited.

## Thumbnail Truth Test

Before upload, answer yes to all:

- [ ] All three images are real footage from the episode or clearly illustrative graphics.
- [ ] Written image-use permission, broker approval, and reverse-identification review are complete.
- [ ] The wide lot frame accurately represents the cases in the episode.
- [ ] The episode reaches a defensible buyer-fit result and separately labels missing proof.
- [ ] No image implies a confirmed defect that Taylor cannot substantiate.
- [ ] Text remains readable at 10% scale.

## Packaging Evaluation

After enough impressions for a directional read, compare:

- CTR by surface.
- First-30-second retention.
- Watch-time share.
- Qualified `LOT` comments/texts.
- Search terms and audience retention at the document-heavy checks.

Do not declare a winner from CTR alone.
