## Rule

## YouTube Short 1 — The Permanent-Part Test

**Hook:** `You can change the cabinets. You cannot move the lot.`

**35-45 second structure:**

1. Show Lots A/B/C.
2. Name three permanent checks: water/grade questions, documented constraints, future edge.
3. Close: `Text LOT to 254-718-4249 before you reserve.`

Do not reveal the long-form winner; send viewers to the full audit.

## YouTube Short 2 — A Bigger Lot Can Have Less Usable Yard

**Hook:** `Gross or platted lot area is not the same as the apparent area remaining after documented constraints.`

Show the exact redacted plat/plot-plan animation. Label it `Illustrative - not a survey, site plan, or buildability approval`. Explain shown building lines and easements as document questions. End with `See the three-lot comparison.`

## YouTube Short 3 — Empty Today Does Not Guarantee Tomorrow

**Hook:** `Before you choose a lot for today's view, turn around and check the dated record.`

Show present-day edge versus current official plan. Say `Plans can change`, and never state that a conceptual element is approved.
