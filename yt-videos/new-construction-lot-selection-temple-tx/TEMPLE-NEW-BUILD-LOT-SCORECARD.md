## Before You Pay a Reservation Fee

- [ ] Ask for the recorded phase plat.
- [ ] Ask for the exact builder plot/site plan and confirmed floor-plan placement.
- [ ] Ask for recorded restrictions and written answers about known easements/utilities.
- [ ] Check current approved phase/road records for adjoining edges.
- [ ] Verify in writing: refundability, deadlines, lot-change rights, and documents provided before payment.

## When Issued Later

- [ ] Review the title commitment, Schedule B/exceptions, and access/restriction items with the title company or attorney.
- [ ] Compare the final survey and legal description with the appropriate professional.
- [ ] Recheck any amended plan, restriction, drainage, or builder document.

A title commitment is an offer to issue coverage under stated conditions. It is not the final policy or a guarantee.
