## 7:12-7:52 — What to Get Before and After Reservation

**[Graphic: two stacks, not one all-purpose list.]**

Before reservation, ask for the recorded phase plat, the builder's exact plot or site plan, recorded restrictions, current approved phase or road documents, and written answers to the issues that matter to you. Reservation rules vary, so verify in writing whether any fee is refundable, which deadlines apply, whether the lot can change, and which documents you receive before paying.

When they are issued later, review the title commitment, final survey, exceptions, and any updated documents. A title commitment is an offer to issue coverage under stated conditions—not the final policy or a guarantee. The title company or attorney handles legal effect. A civil or project engineer handles drainage design and grading adequacy; an inspector stays within observable conditions and the agreed scope.

If soil, taxes, insurance, internet service, or a future improvement controls your decision, verify that exact item separately. Those deeper checks are in the scorecard, not crammed into this field test.
