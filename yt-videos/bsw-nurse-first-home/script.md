## BEAT 3 — The program you actually qualify for (2:00–3:30)
**[GRAPHIC, full-frame: "Home Sweet Texas (TSAHC)" card — income-based ✓ · 3–5% down help · MCC tax credit · 620 credit · Bell cap ~$153,180 eff. 6/13/26]**

> "It's called **Home Sweet Texas**, from the same state agency. The difference: it's based on your *income*, not your job. Instead of asking 'are you a teacher,' it asks 'do you earn under the limit for your county.' And for Bell County, that limit is high — north of a hundred fifty thousand dollars of household income. A single new-grad nurse is comfortably under it, with room to spare even if you've got a partner working too.
>
> If you qualify, you get three to five percent of the purchase price toward your down payment and closing — either as a grant you never repay, or a second loan that's forgiven if you stay three years. There's also a thing called an **MCC** — a mortgage credit certificate — that gives you a federal tax credit on your mortgage interest every year you own the home. Stack those, and the cost of getting in drops hard."
