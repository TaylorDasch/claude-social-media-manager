## Voice rules

- Same Taylor voice: analyst, blunt, data-first.
- BUT: Slightly warmer for FTHB audience. They're anxious. Don't be condescending. Don't be salesy either.
- Acknowledge the anxiety explicitly in the cold open — "Most first-time buyer content is either generic or designed to sell you something. This isn't either."
- Banned phrases as always (dream home, charming, nestled, vibrant, hidden gem, welcome home, turnkey, perfect neighborhood).
- Say "agent" not "broker."
