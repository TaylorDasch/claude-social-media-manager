## CHAPTER VI — THE DISABLED-VET TAX LEVER (9:15–10:10)
### *Mistake #4: being 100%-rated and underwritten to full-tax PITI*

**[VISUAL: Mistake-banner: "MISTAKE #4 — RATED, BUT UNDERWRITTEN TO FULL-TAX PITI". Then two separate statute cards so they're never conflated: "TX TAX CODE 11.131 — TOTAL exemption (100% / IU)" and "TX TAX CODE 11.22 — flat $ exemption by rating".]**

**ON CAMERA:**

> This is the first of the two numbers nobody shows you, and it's the single most under-used affordability lever a military buyer has in Central Texas. But it gets explained wrong constantly, so I'm going to keep two laws separate.

**[VISUAL: Card 1 — "11.131"]**

> **Texas Tax Code 11.131.** If you're VA-rated one-hundred-percent service-connected disabled, *or* rated individually unemployable, you get a *total* homestead property-tax exemption. The entire property-tax line of your PITI goes to zero. On a three-hundred-thousand-dollar home that's roughly five to six hundred dollars a month gone — the equivalent of buying your rate down a point and a half to two points, and it lifts your affordability ceiling something like fifteen to twenty-five percent. It's statewide, so it works in Bell County and in Coryell County — meaning it works in Copperas Cove too.

**[VISUAL: Card 2 — "11.22"]**

> **If you're rated below one-hundred percent, that's a different law — Tax Code 11.22.** It is *not* a graduated version of the total exemption — it's a flat dollar reduction off your assessed value by rating band: roughly five thousand at ten-to-twenty-nine percent, seventy-five hundred at thirty-to-forty-nine, ten thousand at fifty-to-sixty-nine, and twelve thousand at seventy percent and up. So a seventy-to-ninety-nine-percent vet gets the twelve-thousand flat exemption — real, but it's not the total exemption. Don't let anyone tell you 11.131 gives you a partial break. It's the total exemption, or the flat amount — two different laws. Verify the exact band amounts with the appraisal district.

**[CAPTION CALLOUT: "100% / IU = TOTAL exemption (11.131). Below 100% = flat ~$5K–$12K off assessed value (11.22) — different law. [VERIFY current band amounts]"]**

**ON CAMERA — the two things agents miss:**

> Two operational notes. **One** — it's the *homestead* only. The day this property converts to a rental on PCS-out, the total exemption ends. Plan your exit timing around that. **Two** — it isn't automatic. You file with the county appraisal district after the deed transfers — BCAD for Bell County, Coryell CAD for Copperas Cove. Miss it and you pay full tax that year.
>
> And if you're one-hundred-percent rated, make your lender underwrite you to the *exempted* PITI, in writing, before you tour. If they pre-approve you on full-tax PITI, they just quietly cut forty to sixty thousand off your real ceiling. Informational only — confirm with the appraisal district and your tax pro.
