## Where Fort Hood families default — and where the math points instead

About 40% of Fort Hood-stationed families live in Killeen. About 5% live in Temple. The default is proximity. The math, for most multi-year tours, says Temple or Belton.

If your tour at Fort Hood is three years or longer and you plan to convert the property to a rental on PCS-out, two things decide whether your purchase pays:

## Where do most Fort Hood families live — and is that where you should live?

About 40% of Fort Hood-stationed families live in Killeen. About 5% live in Temple. The remainder is split among on-post family housing, Harker Heights, Copperas Cove, Belton, Nolanville, and Salado. The default for most PCS-in families is proximity to gate. For most multi-year tours, the math says that default is wrong.

## The three variables that decide buy or rent

Tour length, exit strategy, income stability. Lean BUY if your tour is 3+ years and you plan to convert to a rental on PCS-out. Lean RENT if your tour is under 18 months or the Killeen subdivision you'd buy in has more than 30% military rentals — brigade-cycle risk is real.

## BAH 2026 (TX282 · with dependents)

E-5: $1,695. E-7: $2,055. O-3: $2,340. National 4.2% increase from 2025. Take BAH × 0.95 to get your max monthly PITI ceiling. At today's roughly 7% rate, that's an approximate purchase ceiling of $215K for E-5, $270K for E-7, $310K for O-3.

## Gate commute matrix

Temple to Bernie Beck Main Gate: 30-40 min off-peak. Belton: 25-35 min. Harker Heights: 8-15 min. Killeen city: 5-15 min depending on gate. Confirm which gate your unit uses before signing or closing — Bernie Beck to Clarke Road for the same Temple house is a 6-10 minute swing.

## The 100% disabled veteran exemption

Texas Tax Code 11.131 grants a total residence homestead property tax exemption to veterans rated 100% service-connected disabled. The property tax line of PITI becomes zero. On a $300K Belton home, the savings run roughly $500-600/month — equivalent to a 1.5-2 percentage point rate reduction. File with the Bell County Appraisal District after closing. It is not automatic.

## What to do before you tour homes

Lock your BAH. Decide tour length and exit strategy. Pre-approval with a VA-experienced lender who underwrites to BAH-to-PITI, not max DTI. Confirm your unit's primary gate. Run the 5-zone matrix on the pillar.
