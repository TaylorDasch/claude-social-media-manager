## SHORT B — "The 100% Disabled Vet Tax Exemption Nobody Runs the Math On" (55 sec)

**Vertical format note:** Open on whiteboard math (full screen), cut to talking head for verdict.

**HOOK — 0:00–0:04:**

> "If you're 100% disabled, your lender might be costing you 60 grand. Here's how."

**[CAPTION: "THE EXEMPTION MATH NOBODY RUNS"]**

**BODY — 0:04–0:40:**

> "Texas Tax Code 11.131. 100% service-connected disabled veterans get a total residence homestead property tax exemption.
>
> Total. The property tax line of PITI becomes zero.
>
> On a $300K Belton home — you save $500 to $600 a month versus the same buyer without the exemption. That's the equivalent of buying your interest rate down 1.5 to 2 percentage points.
>
> Two things most agents miss. One — your lender has to underwrite to the EXEMPTED PITI, not the full-tax PITI. Get it in writing. The wrong underwrite knocks $40 to $60K off your purchase ceiling.
>
> Two — the exemption isn't automatic at closing. You file it with the Bell County Appraisal District after deed transfer. Miss the deadline, pay full tax for that year.
>
> Rated 70 to 99%? You get a $12,000 assessed-value reduction. Modest. About $240 a year on a $300K home."
