## Why the qualifiers matter (broker logic)
- **"Fixed":** Lennar's 3.375% beats Stylecraft's headline number but is a 5/1 ARM at a *worse* APR (6.618% vs 5.691%). Without the word "fixed," the superlative is falsifiable by that ad.
- **"Advertised":** limits the claim to documented public offers — not "no lower rate exists anywhere."
- **On true APR, Stylecraft is also lowest** among every advertised offer found (5.691% vs 5.843/6.23/6.618) — so both the headline-fixed framing and the APR framing hold.
- Note: D.R. Horton's *Austin division* has advertised 3.99% (USDA) — that is NOT the Killeen-Temple division. Their Bell County pages show 4.99%.
