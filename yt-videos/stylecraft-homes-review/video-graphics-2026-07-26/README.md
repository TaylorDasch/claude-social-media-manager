# Stylecraft Review — Drop-In Video Graphics

Generated for the current Stylecraft Homes review in Taylor's Living in Temple brand.

## Which folder to use

- `fullframe-1080/`: opaque 1920×1080 cutaway cards for the current CapCut project.
- `overlays-1080/`: transparent 1920×1080 lower-thirds for the current CapCut project.
- `fullframe-4k/` and `overlays-4k/`: matching 3840×2160 masters if the timeline is rebuilt at 4K.
- `previews/stylecraft-graphics-contact-sheet.jpg`: visual index.

The filenames follow the script markers `G0` through `G12`. `00-title.png` is an optional opening title.

`G00-taylor-intro-card.png` is the reusable Taylor contact opener. It uses the
approved headshot and displays Taylor Dasch, EG Realty, 254-718-4249, and
dealswithdasch@gmail.com.

## CapCut placement

1. Import the PNGs from the `*-1080` folders.
2. Put a full-frame card or transparent overlay on the track above the A-roll.
3. Leave scale at the fitted default and position centered.
4. For overlays, use a short 6-frame fade in and out. For the full-frame math/comparison cards, a hard cut or 4-frame dissolve works better.
5. Keep full-frame cards on screen long enough to read:
   - simple verdict/warning: 3–4 seconds;
   - math/choice/comparison: 5–7 seconds;
   - five-term checklist: 7–9 seconds.

## Claim controls

- G1–G3 and G10 contain time-sensitive financing context. Stylecraft's official incentives page was checked July 26, 2026 and still showed 4.99% or up to $20,000, not combinable, with an August 10 close-by date and a first-come rate pool.
- G2 and G3 reproduce the illustrative comparison spoken in the prepared script; they are labeled as P&I-only/illustrative.
- Re-check the live offer before the final export. If terms rotate, regenerate those four cards before publishing.
- No builder logos are used. The cards are original Taylor/Living in Temple graphics.
