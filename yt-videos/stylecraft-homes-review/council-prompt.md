## Verified facts to build from (as of July 2026 — re-verify at film time)
- **Financing (the hook):** 3.99% subsidized buydown (Years 1–2) → 4.99% (Years 3–30), 5.691% APR, via **Kangaroo Home Lending** — the builder's captive lender (49% Stylecraft-owned). Verified still live on **move-in-ready** homes. Flat build rates run ~4.99%–5.49%. "Your Way" flex credit now **up to $15,000**. **Every incentive requires financing through Kangaroo** — use an outside lender and you forfeit all of it.
- **Footprint:** 8 active Bell County communities (Temple, Belton, Killeen, Nolanville), **$224,900–$479,900**, 1,262–3,285 SF, 3–6 bed. Entry: South Pointe. Top: The Homestead. 4 ISDs: Belton, Temple, Academy, Killeen.
- **Scale:** ranked #74 nationally; 857 homes / $277M revenue in 2024. Founded 1988, HQ College Station, President Randy French. BBB A+. Eliant scores 2.3–5.0 (volatile).
- **Build:** engineered slabs (Blackland Prairie clay), James Hardie cement-fiberboard siding + stone/brick, granite + smart-home standard. Manual-J HVAC sizing they will NOT let you override.
- **The catches (the honest half):** recurring drainage/grading defects (graded "by eye"), plumbing failures within weeks of closing, LVP flooring lifting; 1-2-10 warranty enforced rigidly with a hard 365-day workmanship cutoff and a warranty dept that requires escalation. MUD overlays (Northgate ~2.96%, Three Creeks ~2.28%) the base price doesn't advertise. Year-2 escrow jump of $150–$350/mo when the county reassesses.
- **The single most important buyer move:** schedule an independent **11-month warranty inspection** ($400–$500) before the Day-365 cutoff.
