# Stylecraft Review — 16:9 Branded Video Cards

**Purpose:** eight 1920×1080 editor masters for the *Living in Temple TX* Stylecraft review. Use the full canvas for 2–6 seconds where the recorded image is unusable or shows a D.R. Horton build. Where the live-action image is clean, animate the same content panel as a lower-third so Taylor remains visible.

## Shared visual system

- **Canvas:** 1920×1080, 16:9. Keep primary copy inside a 140 px safe margin. Reserve the bottom 120 px for source/date text.
- **Background:** radial midnight gradient `#0F172A` → `#070B14`, with a very faint 80 px emerald grid.
- **Primary panel:** `#1E293B`; key accent `#059669`; bright accent `#34D399`; text `#F8FAFC`; secondary text `#94A3B8`; money accent `#D4A853`; red `#EF4444` only for hard deadlines or risk markers.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond for one short editorial headline; Outfit for all data, labels, and body copy; JetBrains Mono for eyebrows and source/date lines.
- **Channel ID:** top-left diamond + `LIVING IN TEMPLE, TX`; bottom-right `TAYLOR DASCH · EG REALTY`. No sales-volume claim.
- **Motion:** 6–8 frame fade/slide, numbers count once, no bounce. Hold the completed card long enough to read. Do not animate fine print.
- **Dated-number rule:** cards 2 and 3 must carry an as-of/source line and be checked again immediately before the final render.
