## S4 — "The tax trap" ⭐ HIGHEST SAVE-RATE CANDIDATE

**Cut:** `5:43.87 → 6:09.90` (26.0s)
**Why:** the most broadly useful clip — applies to every new-construction buyer in Texas, not
just Stylecraft. Least brand-dependent, so it travels furthest.

**Hook card:** `That tax number on Zillow is wrong.`
**Spoken spine:** "They're not going to tell you what the tax is gonna be — they put the
principal and the interest on the payment. And if you're looking at Zillow or Realtor.com, the
taxes are based on the lot with just the land, without the house on it. So they're gonna be
significantly lower than whenever the property is built and they're valuing the full value. So
what I do is take the percentage in the Bell CAD and base that off of the asking price."
**End card:** `Underwrite the finished home, not the empty lot`
**Caption:** The tax figure on a new-construction listing is usually assessed on the bare lot —
before the house existed. Your real bill lands on the finished value. Take the Bell County rate
and apply it to the asking price instead. #TempleTX #NewConstruction #PropertyTax
