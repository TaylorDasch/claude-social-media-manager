## CapCut handling

- **Import at 1080×1920.** Scale to **Fit**, position centered, no crop — they're already
  frame-sized. If CapCut auto-scales, reset scale to 100%.
- **Hooks and END:** hard cut in, hard cut out. No fade — a fading hook card wastes the
  scroll-stop.
- **Stat plates:** 4–6 frame fade in and out.
- **Everything sits inside the UI-safe band** (y 340–1380, clear of the right-hand action rail),
  so nothing collides with the TikTok/IG caption, username, or like/comment/share buttons.
- **Burn captions on all five.** Most feed views are muted.
