## The job
Answer, on camera, the question a BSW hire actually types: *what can I afford here?* The page does the interactive
math; the video does the judgment the page can't — what the number feels like, where people actually land, and when
buying is the wrong call.

## Hard facts to build on (verified 2026-08-10, do not drift)
- MLS closed sales, Temple + Belton, 365 days ending 2026-08-05: **730 sales, 19 neighborhoods** with ≥8 sales.
- Closest: **Canyon Creek, 5 min, $215,500 median**. Farthest in set: **Dawson Ranch, 24 min, $375,000**.
- 12–14 min band holds most newer inventory: Mesa Ridge $296,270 · North Gate $314,150 · Hartrick Ranch $306,370 ·
  Lake Pointe $265,000.
- **Every neighborhood in the set is inside 24 minutes.** Commute is rarely the real constraint in Temple.
- Property tax ~2.1–2.3%; **Three Creeks ~2.279% including MUD**. Zillow often applies a city rate to MUD/PID homes.
- A $60k resident on physician-loan terms lands near **$141,665** — below every entry price in the table.

## The angle to pressure-test in council
The honest headline is not "you can afford more than you think." For residents it is the opposite, and saying so is
the differentiator. Competing content leads with a salary number and implies buying; this should lead with the
payment and let the viewer reach their own verdict.
