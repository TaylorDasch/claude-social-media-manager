# Thumbnail Brief — BSW "Start Here" Cornerstone

**Final art is outsourced to Pikzels** (better than AI-gen). Send Pikzels this brief + a clean front-facing face photo of Taylor for the composite. Ask for **2 variants to A/B**: one "START HERE" authority cut and one "BEFORE YOU TOUR" curiosity cut.

**Concept:** Authority-orientation thumbnail that reads instantly as "the one to watch first" for someone who just scanned a booth QR.

**Big text (3–4 words max, bold, high-contrast, mobile-readable):**
- Primary: **BSW? START HERE**
- Alts: "MOVING FOR BSW" with a smaller "START HERE" tab, or "BEFORE YOU TOUR"

**Secondary element:** a clean numbered stack `1 2 3` or three short stacked labels — **RENT? / WHERE? / BUILD?** — to telegraph the 3-decision framework at a glance. Channel accent color on the "3" to imply structure.

**Face / expression:** Taylor, chest-up, direct eye contact, calm-confident and approachable (NOT salesy grin, NOT shocked-face). Slight head tilt / one hand gesturing toward the text works. Read as a credible peer to a physician, not a hype agent.

**Background:** real Temple / BSW context — soft-focus Temple skyline, a recognizable Central Texas residential street, or the hospital-area exterior. Dark/desaturated so text + face pop. Avoid generic stock-house imagery.

**Color/contrast:** high contrast, one accent color, large type — survives at small suggested-feed size. WCAG-level legibility on the headline.

**Banned-word check:** none present. "START HERE" / "BEFORE YOU TOUR" are clean.
