# Filming Notes & Shot List

**SETUP:** Standing talking-head. It's a cornerstone/authority "start here" — standing reads more confident and energetic than seated, and the 3-decision structure benefits from a little movement between sections. Desk is acceptable if energy is high, but standing is the recommendation. Direct eye contact to lens, especially on the cold open and the CTA.

**DELIVERY:**
- Cold open (first 2 sentences) is the whole game — say it flat and direct, no smile-warmup, no "hey guys." NO name or logo in the first 15 seconds.
- The credibility line is ONE breath — say the numbers and move on; lingering makes it read like an ad.
- The "stay to the end" loop must sound like a genuine heads-up, not a YouTuber tic.

**PACING / CHAPTER CUTS (cut points double as YouTube chapters):**
- Hard cut after the hook into credibility.
- Hard cut on each "Decision one / two / three" — natural chapter breaks; lead each with the surprising line, THEN the label where possible.
- Cut into caveats and into the CTA so momentum doesn't sag.

**LOWER-THIRDS / OVERLAYS** (prefer lower-third over full-screen per channel rule; full-frame only for the map):
- Cold open: small lower-third "~6 weeks to decide" (optional).
- Credibility: lower-third "Taylor Dasch · EG Realty · Temple 76502 · $30M+ / 100+ deals" — the ONLY place identity appears on screen, and only AFTER the 15-sec mark.
- Campus orientation line: the ONE full-frame moment — a clean map showing BSW main campus just north of downtown with a soft ring around it.
- Each decision: lower-third label "DECISION 1 · RENT OR BUY," etc.
- ISD gotcha: lower-third "Wyndham Hill = ACADEMY ISD (not Temple ISD)" — high-value retention/credibility beat.
- Every routing mention: lower-third with the exact video title so viewers can screenshot it; reinforce with a YouTube info-card at that timestamp.
- Tax stat: lower-third "~2.2–2.7% effective · as of filming · varies by MUD/PID" so the hedge is visually locked in.

**B-ROLL** (sparingly — keep face-forward for retention):
- Temple skyline / BSW campus exterior under the orientation line.
- A few seconds of a closer-in older street vs a newer-construction street under the Decision-2 ring tradeoff (do NOT turn this into a neighborhood tour — that's the Feb video).
- Avoid generic stock house footage.
