### How should Morgan's Point Resort homes be compared?

> Morgan's Point Resort homes should be compared with sales that compete for the same buyer, using similar market area, site, size, age, condition, style, and legal characteristics. The July 30, 2026 active set spans 1966–2026 build years and includes newer developments as well as original Morgan's Point Resort sections, so the full active range is not one comp set.

### Can a Morgan's Point Resort home have a private dock on Belton Lake?

> The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers' December 2018 Belton Lake Master Plan states that private shoreline uses are not allowed at Belton Lake and identifies privately owned floating docks and fixed or movable piers among those uses. Buyers should not treat a lake view or an MLS water-access statement as proof of shoreline ownership or private-dock rights.

### Does a Lake Belton view mean the property owns the shoreline?

> No. A view describes what may be visible from a property; it does not establish a parcel boundary. In the July 30, 2026 CTXMLS pull, all 26 Morgan's Point Resort records had `WaterfrontYN` marked False. Buyers should verify the exact boundary with a survey or Corps boundary record.

### Do Morgan's Point Resort homes have septic systems?

> Some do. Four of the 26 July 30, 2026 listing-remark records mention septic, including descriptions that pair city water with aerobic septic. Buyers should verify the exact system, service history, maintenance agreement, and transfer requirements for the property.

### Does Morgan's Point Resort have an HOA?

> It varies by property. In the July 30, 2026 CTXMLS set, 24 records showed `HOA = None` and 2 showed `Mandatory`. That is a market pattern, not a guarantee for an address; buyers should review the property's recorded documents.
