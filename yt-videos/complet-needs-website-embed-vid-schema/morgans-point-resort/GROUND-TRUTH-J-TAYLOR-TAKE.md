Large mature trees close to the lake. Deer in just about every corner. New custom builds sitting alongside

### ⚠️ Corrected 2026-07-28 — a substring match is not a claim

An earlier version of this file said *"13 of 29 listings mention a boat"* and used it as evidence of storage
freedom. **That was wrong and Taylor caught it.** Eleven of those thirteen hits are either the standardized
`NeighborhoodAmenities` picklist (`Boat Ramp`, `Boat Dock` — agent-selected checkboxes, not prose) or generic
lake-lifestyle copy about Belton Lake ("boating, fishing, swimming"). Only **two** referenced boat parking on
the property.

**Standing rule for every future remarks scan: a keyword hit is a lead, not a finding.** Read the surrounding
sentence and classify it before it becomes an on-camera number. The RV and workshop counts above were
re-derived by reading all matches in context; the boat count was retired.

**RESOLVED BY OMISSION 2026-07-28.** One listing's amenity field reads *"Several Boat Ramps, 3 Marinas"*
while the live templetxhomes.net page states there is **no public boat ramp inside city limits**. Amenity
picklists are agent-entered and frequently describe the wider lake area rather than the city, so the two are
not necessarily contradictory — but neither is verified. **Taylor's call: the video makes no boat-ramp or
marina claim at all.** He describes the area and its location; the access claim is cut. The marina shot (old
G5) is removed from the shot list. Do not reintroduce either on location or in the edit.

Another listing states it is *"currently operating as an Airbnb"* — worth knowing that STR exists here in
practice despite the Specific Use Permit requirement. Not a video point (buyer lane), but real.

**Bonus finding from the remarks scan:** one listing notes *"you can pay an optional access fee to MPR
Community Center and Pool."* That likely explains the HOA field split — 27 None / 2 Mandatory — because
amenity access here is **opt-in**, not a mandatory assessment. Worth one line on camera; it's a real
question buyers ask and the answer isn't obvious from the HOA field alone.

Also from the remarks: one listing sits with *"natural Corps land directly behind"* — USACE land adjacency
is a genuine feature (permanent open space behind you) and a genuine constraint (it's federal land with its
own rules). Verify per property.

## J3. 🔑 The manufactured-home / no-restrictions point is the video's closing argument

**Why it matters more than anything else in the package:** it is the one material fact about buying here
that **no amount of remote research will surface**. It is not in the MLS. It is not on Zillow. It is not in
the tax record in any form a buyer will find. Taylor knows it because he has driven these streets.

That makes it the perfect close, because it completes the video's own spine:

> The data showed you the town is cheaper and older than its name suggests.
> Here is the part the data cannot show you at all — and it's the part that decides whether you're happy
> in three years.

**And it resolves the video's central tension honestly.** The freedom and the risk are *the same fact*:

| Minimal deed restrictions means… | …and it also means |
|---|---|
| Park the boat in the driveway | Your neighbor can too |
| Build a detached workshop | So can the lot beside you |
| Store the RV on your lot | Nothing controls what goes up next door |
| A new custom build on an older street | A wide mix of housing types on one block |

Taylor's "checks a lot of boxes" and Taylor's "almost no restrictions" are not two observations. **They are
one observation seen from two directions** — and saying that out loud is the most honest thing in the video.

### ⚠️ Compliance guardrails — non-negotiable on this segment

Discussing deed restrictions, zoning, and property-type mix as **resale and lifestyle variables** is
legitimate, standard buyer counsel. Discussing them in terms of **who lives there** is a fair-housing
violation. The line is absolute:

**DO:**
- Talk about **restrictions, zoning, and property types** — these are characteristics of land and rules
- "There is no citywide HOA. City zoning is the only control. Housing types vary widely block to block."
- "Nothing prevents what's next to you from changing. Price that in, or don't buy the lot."
- **Give the action:** "Drive the specific street. Not the town — the street. At the hour you'd be home."

**DON'T:**
- Never characterize residents, income, class, family status, or any protected characteristic — not by
  implication, not by tone, not by B-roll choice
- Never say or imply manufactured homes lower values. That is a valuation claim Taylor has not evidenced,
  it invites a fair-housing complaint, and it is the single most screenshot-able line in the video
- Never film an identifiable manufactured home as an illustration of a downside. **Do not shoot this
  segment at all** — deliver it to camera or over a map/graphic
- Never steer toward or away from any section of the city. Give the buyer the variable and the method;
  let them decide

**Shipped phrasing (use verbatim):**

> "Here's the thing the data can't tell you, and it's the most important thing I'll say.
>
> Morgan's Point Resort has no citywide HOA and very few deed restrictions. That's genuinely part of the
> appeal — you can park a boat in your driveway, put up a workshop, keep the RV on your own lot. Thirteen of
> the twenty-nine listings mention a boat. That freedom is real and people move here for it.
>
> But it cuts exactly one other way. Housing types vary a lot block to block here, and there is very little
> in the rules that controls what goes up beside you. In some sections you'll find a new custom build and a
> much older home and a manufactured home within sight of each other. I'm not going to tell you how to feel
> about that — I'm telling you it's a variable, it changes by street, and it's not something you can check
> from a listing photo.
>
> So do this: pick the street, not the town. Drive it. Drive it at the hour you'd actually be coming home.
> That single trip tells you more than every number I just gave you."
