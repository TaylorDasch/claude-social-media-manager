## 6. Verification and disclaimer

This video provides general real estate information, not legal, lending, insurance, tax, survey, or appraisal advice. MLS fields and listing remarks can be incomplete or change after the data date. Verify the exact property's condition, septic system, insurance eligibility, recorded restrictions, school assignment, survey, Corps boundary, water access, and title documents during your due-diligence period.

The July 30 CTXMLS pull shows `WaterfrontYN = False` on all 26 Morgan's Point records. The USACE master plan separately states that private shoreline uses are not allowed at Belton Lake and identifies privately owned floating docks and fixed or movable piers among those uses. A map or GIS view does not establish a parcel boundary; use a survey or Corps boundary record for the property.
