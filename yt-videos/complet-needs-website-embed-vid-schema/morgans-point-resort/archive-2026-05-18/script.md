### 7:30 – 8:15 — Disclosure Reality (FAQ moments — questions spoken aloud)

*[A wide. Each Q appears as caption card as Taylor says it.]*

> **"What about flood insurance?"** Some lakefront and lake-adjacent parcels sit in FEMA AE zones. That can triple your insurance vs an inland parcel. Verify the current FEMA map at msc.fema.gov before you write.
>
> **"What about septic?"** Most MPR homes are on septic, not city sewer. The median year built here is 1998, and 40% are pre-1990. Ask for the septic permit and last pump-out date during your option period.
>
> **"Can I dock here?"** Dock permits are regulated by the USACE Belton Lake Resource Manager's Office. A home being marketed as lakefront does not guarantee a current or approved dock permit. Verify directly with USACE before you sign.
>
> **"Can I Airbnb?"** Morgan's Point Resort does not freely allow short-term rentals. The city requires a Specific Use Permit. Most owners don't have one. If rental income is part of your math, verify SUP status before you write.
>
> **"What about windstorm?"** Lakefront and exposed parcels often carry windstorm requirements that inland comps don't. Different insurance product, different premium. Factor it in.

### 8:15 – 8:45 — Hidden Sub-Tier (analyst sidebar)

The 1B parcel may be permittable, but you're buying the right to apply, not the right to use. That's a real comp gap most appraisers won't surface."
