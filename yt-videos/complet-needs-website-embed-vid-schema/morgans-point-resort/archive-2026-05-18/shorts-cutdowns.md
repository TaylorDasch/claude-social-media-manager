**Source timecode in long-form:** 2:30–4:00 + 8:15–8:45 (Tier 1 walkthrough + Hidden Sub-Tier)

**Hook (0–3s):**
> "This listing says 'lakefront.' Look at the parcel line."

- VO: "True lakefront at Morgan's Point Resort means your parcel backs to USACE-leased shoreline. The lease is real. But the dock permit isn't automatic. Some lakefront listings have shoreline, no current dock approval. Same broad tier, very different product."

**End card (45–60s):**

## SHORT 3 — "The Pricing Trap"

**Source timecode in long-form:** 5:00 + 6:45–7:30 (chart card + pricing trap)

**Hook (0–3s):**
> "Same $249,500 median. Three completely different per-sqft."

**Body (3–45s):**
- Visual: bar chart, three bars per-sqft difference. Then three side-by-side comp cards.
- VO: "The MPR median is $249,500. But the per-square-foot price for lakefront is materially different from lake-access, and lake-access is different from inland. Same median price, three different products. If your offer is comping all three tiers as one comp set, you're either overpaying or panic-walking. Both happen here every month."

**End card (45–60s):**
> "Comp every tier against itself. That's it."

**Caption text:**
> The MPR median is misleading. Three different products under one number. The fix: separate by water tier before you write.
