## #5 ALTA VISTA — *"newer + a school surprise"*
- Median **$228,500** · $213K–$244K · ~1,600 sqft · built ~2017 · D.R. Horton/Ashford · No HOA
- **Academy ISD** (not Temple ISD) — real plus if schools drive the move; smaller, strong district, inside Temple city limits
- **7 min** to BSW
- ❗Negative: thin under $250K (3 active); most of Alta Vista now $250K–mid-$300s → it's a timing play
- Best for: family wanting newer + Academy schools

## #4 OAK RIDGE — *"brand-new, zero projects"*
- Median **$236,675** · $219K–$250K · ~1,415 sqft · built **2025 (NEW)** · D.R. Horton/Flintrock · **Temple ISD** · Mandatory HOA
- New-build perks: warranties, modern open layouts, nothing to fix day one
- Spice-themed streets (Cilantro, Saffron, Turmeric, Peppermint) — visual hook
- **11 min** to BSW (farthest, still close)
- ❗Negatives: mandatory HOA; still filling in (construction, few mature trees); smallest median sqft for the money
- Best for: buyer/young family wanting move-in-ready new construction
- ✅ **Temple ISD** (Garcia Elem → Lamar MS → Temple High) — verified via D.R. Horton community page

## #3 HERITAGE PLACE — *"most homes to tour right now"*
- Median **$220,000** · $195K–$240K · ~1,325 sqft · built ~2014 · **15 ACTIVE (most inventory)** · **Temple ISD** · Mandatory HOA
- 2000s–2010s, red brick + white stone, open plans (Kiella + others)
- Colonial streets (Roanoke, Jamestown, Vicksburg, Petersburg)
- **9 min** to BSW
- ❗Negatives: smallest established homes (~1,325 sqft); mandatory HOA
- Best for: first-time buyer wanting newer + move-in ready + most current choices

## #2 WESTERN HILLS — *"most house & land per dollar"*
- Median **$210,000** · $100K–$250K · ~1,734 sqft (biggest) · built ~1985 · **No HOA** · Temple ISD ✓
- Established west Temple, big lots, mature trees, homes pushing 2,000+ sqft
- Native-American + Texas-river streets (Apache, Comanche, Brazos, Chisholm)
- **8 min** to BSW
- ❗Negative (the big one): **condition varies wildly** — $100K end = fixer w/ foundation issues; top end = full remodels. Inspect carefully.
- Best for: wants space + yard + no HOA, willing to vet/update

## #1 CANYON CREEK — *"the all-rounder + closest to BSW"* ✅ footage exists
- Median **$219,000** · $190K–$250K · ~1,558 sqft · built ~1985 · **No HOA** · **15 ACTIVE** · Temple ISD ✓
- Checks every box: price, inventory, no HOA, mature trees (established since late '70s)
- French/English streets (Bordeaux, Brighton, Chelsea, Kensington)
- **5 min to BSW — closest on the whole list.** Lead with this.
- Prestige ceiling: "Cliffs of Canyon Creek" pocket runs past $900K → good for long-term value
- ❗Negative: 1980s core (some dated homes); no HOA = upkeep varies; under $250K = entry tier, not the Cliffs
- Best for: most people moving for the hospital — closest commute, trees, room to grow

## HONORABLE MENTION — CIMARRON (~15s)
- Median **$197,000** (from $153K — cheapest entry) · ~1,150 sqft · built ~1983 · No HOA · south Temple off 31st, by Lions Junction water park · **7 min** to BSW · some twin homes
- For tightest budgets / under $200K
