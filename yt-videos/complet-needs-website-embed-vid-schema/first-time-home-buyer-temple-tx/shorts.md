## Short B · "$5K–$10K You're Leaving On The Table"

**Length target:** 50–60 sec
**Vertical format:** 9:16 · 1080×1920 · captions burned in
**Source chapter:** Long-form 5:05–6:10 (TSAHC + MCC stack)

### Hook (first 3 sec)

**[SHOT: Tight on Taylor. Caption: "MOST BELL COUNTY BUYERS MISS THIS."]**

> "If you're a first-time home buyer in Bell County, there's a chance you're leaving $5,000 to $10,000 on the table — and your lender may not tell you."

### Body (3–45 sec)

**[GRAPHIC: Two stacking layers build under VO — TSAHC grant + MCC.]**

> "Two programs. Layer one — **TSAHC.** Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation. They give first-time buyers a **3% to 5% down payment grant.** Not a loan. A grant. Never repaid. Most Bell County zips qualify if your income's under roughly $92K to $108K.
>
> Layer two — **MCC.** Mortgage Credit Certificate. Federal income tax credit. **Up to $2,000 a year. For the life of the loan.** On a $280K loan, your first-year credit is about $1,800 to $2,000.
>
> Stack them. Grant covers your down payment. Tax credit drops your effective monthly payment for as long as you own the home. Some Bell County buyers close on a $280K home with **under $3,000 out of pocket.**"

### Body (3–35 sec)

**[GRAPHIC: 28/36 callout builds live. Calculator math overlay.]**

> "Housing under 28% of your gross monthly income. Total debt under 36%. That's it.
>
> Run the math. Household earning $7,000 a month gross — about $84K a year. Target maximum PITI: **$1,960 a month.** At current rates, that maps to roughly a **$260K to $280K** purchase price.

> Here's the line that matters: if your housing payment hits **32% of your gross income, you're house-poor before you move in.** The walls are nicer. The bank account is empty.
