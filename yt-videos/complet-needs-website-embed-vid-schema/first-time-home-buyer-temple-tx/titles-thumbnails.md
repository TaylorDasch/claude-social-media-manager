## Thumbnail concepts (3)

### Concept A — "$2,000/year nobody told you about"

**Visual:** Money in foreground (loose $100 bills fanned, or a $20K stack tilted toward camera, slightly out of focus on the bills themselves). Taylor in the right third, neutral expression, slight forward lean — not smiling, not frowning. Living-in-Temple color palette (slate/emerald accents). Background out of focus, hint of residential exterior.

**Text overlay (left two-thirds):**
- Top line (large, white, bold): `$2,000/YR`
- Middle line (smaller, yellow accent or emerald): `NOBODY TOLD YOU ABOUT`
- Bottom corner micro (Taylor brand): "TEMPLE TX · FIRST-TIME BUYER"

**Why it works:**
- Money + dollar amount = strongest CTR pattern for finance content
- Specific number ($2,000/yr) beats generic
- Pairs with Title #2 directly

**File spec:** 1280×720. JPEG. ≤2MB.

### Concept B — "What you signed" vs "What it costs" (Builder Upgrade Trap)

**Visual:** Split-frame thumbnail.
- LEFT HALF: Model home interior shot, well-lit, "perfect" looking — design center brochure visible. Caption banner: `WHAT YOU SIGNED` in clean white.
- RIGHT HALF: Same model layout but austere, with red highlight callouts marking the $30K of upgrade markup — countertops, fixtures, trim, smart panel. Caption banner: `WHAT IT COSTS YOU` in red.
- Taylor's face in a small circle (bottom-right corner), 10% of frame, neutral.

**Text overlay:** Two banners only. No headline at top — let the visual carry it.

**Why it works:**
- Side-by-side comparison is YouTube's most click-tested format
- Anti-builder angle drives the "I need to know this" click
- Pairs with Title #4

**File spec:** 1280×720. Bold contrast. Test mobile-first preview.

### Concept C — The Six-Step Ladder

**Visual:** Clean, design-forward thumbnail. Vertical six-step ladder graphic in the left half, each rung labeled with a clean icon and a step name (Prequalify / Budget / Search / Offer / Inspect / Close). Top of ladder shows house keys. Bottom shows a credit card / paperwork stack. Taylor in the right third, calm framing, slight nod-forward.

**Text overlay:**
- Top line: `THE 6-STEP FIRST HOME PLAYBOOK`
- Bottom line (smaller): `TEMPLE, TX · 2026`

**Why it works:**
- Most measured/calm of the three — for the audience that distrusts hype
- Highest expected retention pull from anxious buyers (because it signals "this is actually a plan, not a sales pitch")
- Pairs with Title #1 or #3

**File spec:** 1280×720. More design-heavy than A or B — needs cleaner production.
