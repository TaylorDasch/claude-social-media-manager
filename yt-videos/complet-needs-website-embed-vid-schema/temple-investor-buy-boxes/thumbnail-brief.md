# THUMBNAIL BRIEF — Temple TX Investor Buy Boxes
**Taylor Dasch · investor lane (separate from the Living in Temple buyer channel) · text overlays ≤4 words each**

Design rules across all concepts:
- Mobile-legible: ONE dominant text block, huge weight, ≤4 words. Numbers read as numbers (people scan digits faster than letters).
- Honest/contrarian tone, NOT hype — matches "I post the math" brand. No fake stacks-of-cash, no Lambo, no shouting-face clickbait.
- Color: deep dark base (charcoal/near-black or deep navy) for the data-terminal feel; ONE accent — caution red/orange for the negatives, a single green for the one winner. High contrast.
- Taylor expression: skeptical/level, slight head-tilt or flat "don't kid yourself" look. Arms-crossed or one hand gesturing to the number. NOT smiling-salesy.
- Keep right or left third clear for Taylor; data/number occupies the opposite side.

---

## CONCEPT A — "THE TRAP" (recommended primary; matches cold-open)
- **Overlay text:** `TEMPLE WON'T CASH FLOW` (4 words) in massive condensed bold.
- Visual: Taylor right third, skeptical look, faint Temple aerial / hospital skyline behind, darkened. Left side: a row of small house icons with red down-arrows, ONE glowing green.
- Color: charcoal base, red arrows, single green house. "WON'T" in red, rest in white.
- Why it works: the contrarian gut-punch is the whole hook; red-vs-one-green visually previews "1 of 8."

## CONCEPT B — "THE 0.8% RULE" (data-credibility angle)
- **Overlay text:** `THE 0.8% RULE` (3 words) — oversized `0.8%` as the hero element, glowing, with a thin horizontal "line" graphic through it (the cash-flow line).
- Sub-kicker (small, optional): `25% down`.
- Visual: Taylor left third pointing at the number; right side a minimal dark spreadsheet/ratio bar with rows above/below the line (green above, red below).
- Color: near-black base, electric green or cyan for `0.8%` and the line, red rows below it.
- Why it works: gives the curious investor a concrete framework promise; "rule" implies a takeaway, not just doom.

## CONCEPT C — "ONLY 1 WORKS" (solution/payoff angle)
- **Overlay text:** `ONLY 1 WORKS` (3 words) — or `WHERE IT ACTUALLY WORKS` (3 words) for the softer version.
- Visual: the 8-row buy-box table teased dark/blurred behind, with ONE green row pulled forward and circled/highlighted (the self-managed MTR). Taylor right third, pointing or gesturing toward the green row.
- Color: dark table, 7 red/muted rows, 1 bright green highlighted row; text white with "1"/"WORKS" in green.
- Why it works: pairs the negative hook with a payoff so it isn't pure doom; the single-green-row visual is the literal thesis.
