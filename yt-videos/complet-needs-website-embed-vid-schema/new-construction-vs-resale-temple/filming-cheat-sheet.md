# Filming Cheat Sheet - New vs Resale in Temple TX

**Video:** New vs Resale in Temple TX: The 77-Day Gap (2026)  
**Channel:** Living in Temple TX  
**Format:** A-roll explainer + screen/data graphics + local B-roll  
**Viewer:** Temple buyer/relocator deciding whether a builder offer beats resale  
**Core proof:** June 18 Temple MLS closed-sales pull + June 19 builder-incentive feed  
**Primary CTA:** Comment or DM `MUDCHECK`

## Pre-Shoot Checklist

- [ ] Open `script.md` and confirm the locked numbers still match the card exports.
- [ ] Confirm `https://templetxhomes.net/new-construction-vs-resale/` loads.
- [ ] Grab screenshots/screen recordings of the builder-incentive feed with addresses blurred or cropped.
- [ ] Verify any MUD/PID example before naming it. Use Three Creeks/Bell County MUD No. 1 only as the Bell County public-record example already in the script.
- [ ] Do not say any active rate, credit, or builder incentive without the qualifier: subject to lender terms, buyer qualification, fees, preferred-lender rules, and change.
- [ ] Do not show private client info, full MLS addresses, license plates, children, or private residents.

## Gear Setup

- Camera/phone on tripod for A-roll.
- Lav mic or close shotgun audio.
- Laptop or monitor for screen-recorded proof.
- One small light or window-facing setup.
- Phone/gimbal for B-roll.
- Optional: drone only where legal and practical.

## Visual Treatment

**Style:** practical, local, data-first.  
**Pacing:** fast hook, clean proof cards, short B-roll inserts every 20-35 seconds.  
**Primary locations:** desk/office, one new-construction street, one established resale street, laptop screen.  
**Avoid:** builder logos without permission, identifiable private homes as examples, hype adjectives from MLS remarks, and anything that looks like a specific promise.

## Editing Notes

- Start cold. No logo animation before the hook.
- Use a subtle punch-in on the first mention of "two and a half months longer."
- Put the first stat card on screen before 0:45.
- Cut to B-roll whenever the script names a physical situation: model home, resale street, tax bill, sign-in desk.
- At 5:20, make the leverage card a full interruption. Sound hit is fine; keep it clean.
- Keep all numbers large and readable on mobile.
- Blur/crop data screens before export.
- Add the verification caveat as small lower-third text on incentive graphics.

## Lines To Nail

- "Two houses in Temple. Same price - call it two-ninety."
- "One has been sitting unsold about two and a half months longer."
- "Slow market time is not your problem. It is your leverage."
- "When a finished spec home sits on the MLS, the builder may be carrying interest, taxes, and insurance."
- "The model-home agent works for the builder, not for you."
- "Those two gates are pass/fail."
- "Comment or DM me MUDCHECK."

## Do Not Say / Do Not Show

- Do not say builder incentives are guaranteed.
- Do not say buyer-agent help is always free. Say typically, and confirm in writing.
- Do not say every new build has a MUD/PID.
- Do not identify a specific active listing as a deal unless verified that morning.
- Do not show full MLS sheets with addresses/contact info.
- Do not film inside model homes, sales offices, or private property without permission.
- Do not quote listing-agent marketing copy.

## Fast Shoot Version

If time is tight, film only:

1. A-roll full script at desk.
2. New-build street B-roll: 5 wide/medium/detail clips.
3. Resale street B-roll: 5 wide/medium/detail clips.
4. Staged sign-in card.
5. Laptop/screen proof clips.
6. CTA close-up.

That is enough to edit the video with cards and screen graphics.
