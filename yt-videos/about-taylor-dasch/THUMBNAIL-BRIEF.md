# THUMBNAIL BRIEF — About-Taylor Video

---

## Concept (Winner)

**Subject placement:** Taylor in left-third of frame, arms crossed, neutral/skeptical expression (not smiling, not angry — competent and slightly intense). Shot from chest-up. Slight upward angle to convey authority without arrogance.

**Background:** Slightly blurred Temple streetscape or BSW campus tower visible. Color-graded toward midnight navy tones to create depth separation from subject.

**Text overlay:**
- Line 1: "I ONLY WORK" — white, bold condensed sans-serif (top-right area)
- Line 2: "3 BUYER TYPES" — emerald green (#059669), larger weight, same font
- Small "Temple TX" label in bottom-right corner, midnight navy with slight opacity

**Color palette:**
- Emerald green (#059669) for accent text and subtle border glow on left edge
- Midnight navy (#1e293b) for background vignette and corner darkening
- White for primary text
- High-contrast face lighting — warm key light, no flat/overcast look

**What NOT to do:**
- No smile
- No pointing at text
- No house photos in background
- No dollar signs or numbers
- No red/yellow clickbait energy
