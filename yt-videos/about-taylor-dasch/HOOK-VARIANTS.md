# HOOK VARIANTS — About-Taylor Video

Ranked by predicted retention at 30 seconds. Taylor can A/B test these by filming all 5 and checking analytics.

---

## #1 (WINNER — used in final script)
"You're about to move to Temple, Texas — and the agent you picked knows nothing about your situation."

**Why it wins:** Viewer-facing threat. Creates immediate stakes. Works for BSW physician, military PCS, AND investor watching. Doesn't depend on knowing who Taylor is — works for cold traffic from search.

---

## #2
"Three types of people move to Temple. Most agents treat them identically. That's why they get hurt."

**Why it's strong:** Specificity ("three types") + consequence ("get hurt") creates curiosity gap. Slightly more aggressive. Good A/B test against #1.

---

## #3
"The agent who helps a physician buy a house should not be the same agent who helps a retiree downsize. Here's why Temple proves it."

**Why it's strong:** Immediately tells BSW physicians "this is for you." Geographic anchor. Slightly longer (7.5 sec) — needs tight delivery.

---

## #4
"Every week I turn down clients. Not because I'm busy — because their situation isn't one of my three lanes."

**Why it's strong:** Contradiction-first. The refusal creates status signaling without bragging. Works best if Taylor is already somewhat known (better for /about/ embed than cold YouTube traffic).

---

## #5
"If your agent can't explain how your VA loan interacts with Temple HOA covenants, you've got the wrong agent."

**Why it's strong:** Ultra-specific. Military viewers immediately know this is for them. BSW viewers think "if he knows VA that well, he probably knows physician loans that well." Weakest for cold generalist traffic.
