## Context

Taylor Dasch is a real estate agent and active investor at EG Realty in Temple, TX. He's planning a 4–6 minute YouTube video that lives on the **Living in Temple, TX** channel (relocation/buyer lane), is **also embedded on the /about/ page** at templetxhomes.net, and serves a dual purpose: (1) act as the canonical "who is this agent" answer for new YouTube viewers and AI assistants citing him for BSW/relocation queries; (2) **double as actual relocation-buyer content** so the algorithm doesn't punish it as brand fluff on a non-brand channel.

**Hard constraint:** the spoken script must NOT depend on saying "I've closed $30M" or "100+ deals" verbally. Numbers can appear in on-screen lower-thirds and supers, but the hook and narrative must work without dollar figures. The video should remain shippable even after the numbers change.

- Active investor: 100+ personal deals (flips, BRRRR, mid-term rentals, buy-and-hold)

- Three lanes (ordered by share): Investor (~55%), BSW Medical relocation (~35%), Military PCS / Fort Hood (~10%)
- Preferred BSW lender: Matt Levant at Acre Mortgage; backups Extraco, First Lonestar, Regions, Truist
- Public surfaces: Living in Temple (YT, ~148 subs), Investing in Central Texas (YT, ~52 subs), Temple Insider newsletter (Beehiiv), Investor Brief newsletter (Beehiiv), BiggerPockets, templetxhomes.net

### Taylor's voice + style (mandatory)

## The thinking task — answer all 10 in depth

### 1. Audience triage
Map the three audiences who will land on this video, ranked by likelihood:
- (a) Someone arriving at templetxhomes.net/about/ from a Google or ChatGPT result for "real estate agent near BSW Temple TX" or "best Temple TX agent"
- (b) A YouTube viewer who watched a Living-in-Temple neighborhood video and is asking "who is this guy"
- (c) A BiggerPockets / referral lead who Googled Taylor's name
For each, name the **single decision they need this video to help them make** and the **specific fear or skepticism** they bring.

### 2. Channel-fit risk
The Living in Temple channel is a relocation/buyer channel. A generic "About Me" video could read as brand and depress topical authority. Identify the 4 highest-risk framing choices that would trigger algorithmic relegation, then identify the 4 framings that REUSE about-the-agent material as relocation-buyer content (e.g., "Why I only work three lanes" answers "is this guy right for my BSW relocation").

### 3. Hook competition
Find 8–12 real YouTube videos posted in the last 24 months by real estate agents in similar markets (Temple TX, Killeen TX, Belton TX, Waco TX, Bryan-College Station, secondary Texas markets). Note their titles, first 7 seconds of hook, and 30-day view counts where visible. From that, identify:
- The 3 hook patterns these agents over-use
- The 3 hook patterns NONE of them use
- The single contradiction Taylor's positioning offers that no competitor's hook captures (likely: "I only take three types of clients" — but verify)

### 4. Hook generation (NO DOLLAR FIGURES)
Generate **12 distinct hook openers**, each fitting Taylor's canonical hook formula `[Specific number or contradiction] + [Who this is for] + [Delayed payoff]`, and each landing inside 7 seconds. Constraints:
- At least 8 must use a CONTRADICTION (not a number)
- The 4 that do use numbers must use non-dollar numbers (three lanes, two channels, etc.)
- All 12 must work as a Living-in-Temple thumbnail-and-hook combo
- All 12 must be defensible to a BSW physician AND a buy-and-hold investor watching the same video
Rank the 12 by predicted retention at 30 seconds and explain the ranking.

### 5. Narrative arc design
Design the 4–6 minute video as 3 acts × 7 segments. For each segment specify:
- Segment job (what changes in the viewer's understanding)
- Visual setup (A talking-head vs B b-roll)
- Pattern-interrupt placement (mandatory every 60–90 seconds in the middle third)
- Specific retention risk and the design move that mitigates it
- Whether the segment generates a Shorts cut as a byproduct

### 6. The "Three Lanes" thesis
Stress-test the core thesis that Taylor only works three lanes (Investor, BSW medical, Military PCS). Identify:
- 3 ways this thesis is genuinely contrarian for the Temple TX market
- 2 ways it could backfire (potential clients who self-disqualify and never call; perception of being too narrow)
- The 2 specific lines a BSW physician needs to hear to feel "yes, he gets me"
- The 2 specific lines an out-of-state investor needs to hear to feel "yes, he gets me"
- Whether the military lane gets a meaningful mention or gets cut for runtime

### 7. Local moat shot list
Identify 5–8 specific Temple/Belton locations that ONLY a Temple-based agent could film at, and for each name the segment of the video it would slot into. At minimum: BSW Temple campus exterior, downtown Temple, the I-35/Loop 363 interchange (commute reality), a Belton ISD–zoned neighborhood, a Lennar/StyleCraft active build site.

### 8. Conversion design
The video sits on /about/ and on a public YouTube channel. Map the conversion path for each surface:
- YouTube: what's the pinned comment, the description, the end-screen CTA, the UTM-tagged link back to /about/?
- /about/: what's the embed location, and what specific objection does the embed solve that the page text alone doesn't?
Identify the **2 micro-commitments** the video should ask for (lowest friction first, e.g., "subscribe + check the about page" before "call me").

### 9. Shorts byproducts
Identify 3 vertical Shorts cuts (45–60s each) that can be pulled from the long-form during the same filming session without a second shoot. Each should:
- Stand alone (not require having watched the long-form)
- Match TikTok's buyer/relocator-only constraint (no investor-only content on TikTok cuts)
- Have a distinct hook from the long-form
Title each and identify the timestamp range in the long-form that becomes the Short.

### 10. Thumbnail + title variants
Produce 6 title variants ranked by predicted CTR with reasoning, and 3 thumbnail concepts described in concrete visual terms (subject placement, text overlay, color palette consistent with Taylor's emerald/midnight brand). At least one title and one thumbnail must center the "three lanes" contradiction. None may include a dollar figure.
