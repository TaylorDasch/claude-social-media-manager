# Videographer Brief — "Temple House Stress Test" pilot (one day)

**What we're making:** a documentary-style episode where Taylor (real estate agent) re-walks a house his buyer client now owns and stress-tests it on camera — findings, dollar figures, and a buy/negotiate/walk verdict. Docu-real look, natural light + practicals, handheld/gimbal energy outside, locked-off precision on evidence details. Not a listing video, not glossy — evidence-forward.

**The repeatable unit — every "stress point" is the same 7 shots (×5 locations):**
1. Approach POV toward the zone (gimbal, 5–8s)
2. Context wide — Taylor at the finding, one spoken line (7–12s)
3. Evidence macro — crack / age plate / rot, rack focus (7–12s)
4. Hands-on insert — tape across crack, level app, flashlight into panel (7–12s)
5. Severity MCU — Taylor direct to camera (7–12s)
6. Locked-off 4s plate (clean hold — a graphic stamp lands here in the edit)
7. One still + one native-vertical pickup (Shorts)

While A-cam runs 1–2, B-cam covers 3–4. Taylor A/B-cuts in 7–12s blocks — coverage matters more than perfection.

**Run of day (~10 hr, 07:45–17:45):** morning exteriors + a walking credential shot → three exterior stress points → cold-open push-ins → midday offload → two interior stress points → **thumbnail stills session (10–12 stills, 3 locations, expression variety, clean backgrounds — these matter as much as the video)** → desk segment: light it, lock it, shoot it, then **record a 2-min walkthrough of your lighting/framing + a one-page settings card so Taylor can rebuild the desk look solo** → brand package (intro sting elements: card slap, stamp hit, tape snap, boots — no house facades) → doorstep verdict + walk-away.

**Technical (agreed before any card is formatted):**
- 4K, **23.976p — everything, both cams, no mixed rates**
- Dual-card record where the body allows; midday offload to two drives
- Audio: Taylor's DJI wireless mics — feed into A-cam + keep the backup track; grab room tone at each location
- Deliver: all raw footage on SSD, day-of or next day
- Rights: Taylor receives all footage with a perpetual license for YouTube/website/social, no watermark (confirmed with broker)

**Optional if time allows:** 60–90s of lot-context ambient — static wide from the street with natural road/traffic sound held clean (no music will hide it in the edit), plus one slow drive-by past the frontage. Real environmental audio is evidence in this format.

**Do NOT capture (hard rules):** street address, street signage, house numbers, neighboring houses in frame, the old listing's main "hero" angle, any geotagged stills. If owners are home: nothing with them in frame unless Taylor says the cameo is on.

**Definition of done — leave with all of it:** 5 full stress-point recipe blocks · cold-open package (3 cinematic evidence push-ins + approach) · walking credential shot · tailgate scorecard standup (2 angles) · desk segment + document b-roll (redacted page-turns, pen/card macros) · desk replication walkthrough + settings card · doorstep verdict + walk-away · thumbnail stills bank · 4 vertical Shorts pickups · intro-sting/outro brand elements · room tone + 3 CTA wild lines.
