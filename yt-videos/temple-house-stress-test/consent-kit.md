## 3. Release — one-page draft (broker/attorney review, then reuse every episode)

**VIDEO RELEASE AND CONSENT — "Temple House Stress Test" episode**

Property: [address — appears in this document only, never on screen] · Owner(s): [all titled owners] · Agent: Taylor Dasch, EG Realty

1. **What I'm agreeing to.** Taylor Dasch may film the interior and exterior of my property (aerial still photos included only if separately noted below) and publish the resulting episode on YouTube, templetxhomes.net, and associated social accounts. The episode analyzes the condition findings from my own purchase — flagged on tour, verified by the licensed inspection I obtained, and documented in my transaction file — and how they shaped the final deal.
2. **Recognition.** People who know this home, or who compare it to its old listing photos, may recognize it. The video will not show the street address, street signage, house numbers, or a geotag, and will avoid recreating the old listing's main photo angle — but **no one can make a house anonymous, and no anonymity is promised.**
3. **Numbers.** There is no private version of this video's math. The old listing's asking price is already public, and the video's own numbers point at what I paid. My real choice is between the exact figure and a rounded one — and "no" to the whole episode is always available. I elect: ☐ **Exact** — the purchase price and all math appear exactly. ☐ **Rounded Band** — the price appears only as a rounded figure ("about $[X]K"), percentages are computed against that rounded figure, and any price-reduction amounts are shown rounded. Repair costs, quotes, and credits appear exactly under either option.
4. **Future sale.** This episode is a permanent, searchable public record of the home's inspected-and-resolved condition history, findable by future buyers' agents, appraisers, and insurers. My seller's-disclosure obligations under Texas law exist independently of this video. The episode documents cures and payments with receipts as well as the original findings. **Pause option:** if I list the home for sale, I may ask in writing that the episode be unlisted, and it will be unlisted within 7 days for the duration of the listing.
5. **Identity.** Name used: ☐ full name ☐ first name only ☐ no name. On-camera appearance: ☐ yes (≤ 20 seconds, optional) ☐ no. I may have personal belongings reviewed/blurred at the preview.
6. **Documents.** Transaction documents appear only in redacted form (names, addresses, loan/account numbers, signatures, MLS numbers removed).
7. **Preview and approval.** I will see the final cut and give written approval before upload. Silence is not approval. I may withdraw consent entirely at any time up to the moment of publication. After publication, concerns (blurs, trims) are handled in good faith; removal after publication is at Taylor's discretion — stated here plainly.
8. **Editorial line.** My approval rights cover my identity, my belongings, and the privacy elections above, plus my right to withdraw the whole episode before publication. They do not extend to rewriting the analysis — the findings, figures, and verdict are the agent's own work from the documented file.

Signature(s), all titled owners · Date
