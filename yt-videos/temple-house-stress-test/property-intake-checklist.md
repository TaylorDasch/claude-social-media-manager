# Property Intake Checklist + Fact & Source Log — Stress Test pilot

Fill this per candidate property. Nothing enters the spoken script without a row in the Fact & Source Log. Items marked **[pre-film]** must exist before the videographer is booked; **[edit-ok]** can be verified during the edit but before publish.

## MANDATORY (no episode without these)
- [ ] **Signed owner release** with price-tier election (consent-kit.md) **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Licensed inspection report** — full copy from the deal file **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Per-finding pricing** — for every finding that gets a dollar on screen: its inspection-report line PLUS a written quote, invoice, repair-amendment line, or closing-statement credit. One amendment cannot source six numbers. Unpriced findings → severity-only lane or a `[current quote — MM/YYYY]` refresh **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Negotiation paper trail** — repair amendment / credit / price-change docs showing what the findings actually moved (Gate 4: ≥ ~1% of price) **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Closing disclosure** (redacted) — feeds THE REAL COST card: actual P&I, taxes, insurance escrow, credits **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Contract timeline** — effective date, option period dates, closing date (grounds the "while my buyer could still walk away" line) **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Inspector agreement read** + written permission for report excerpts, or the no-report-imagery path chosen (§1.7-4) **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Gate-6 scout map** — finding-by-finding filmability (≥3 filmable today) **[pre-film]**
- [ ] **Redaction pass** on every document that appears: names, addresses, loan/account numbers, signatures, MLS numbers **[edit-ok]**
- [ ] **Broker pre-review** scheduled for the final cut **[edit-ok]**

## STRONGLY PREFERRED (each one upgrades a beat)
- [ ] **Appraisal** from the deal (USDA/VA/FHA/conventional — it exists on any financed deal) → the value anchor at THE VERDICT **[pre-film]**
  - ⚠️ Appraisal redaction rule: show the value/date line only, heavily redacted. **Comparable-sales pages never appear on screen** — comp photos/addresses are OTHER people's homes and the Series Law (no third-party property imagery without a release) applies to them too. Subject-property photos from the appraisal are fine (released). State the appraised value as a fact; never frame the spread as equity/profit.
- [ ] MLS sheet + **listing history** (list date, price changes, DOM, archived ask — also feeds the Broadcast Ledger reconstruction audit) **[pre-film]**
- [ ] Seller's disclosure (what was known vs. what the inspection found — handle without characterizing the seller) **[pre-film]**
- [ ] Tax record (current assessed value + rate, dated) + HOA docs/dues if any **[edit-ok]**
- [ ] Insurance quote or actual policy premium (dated) → REAL COST card **[edit-ok]**
- [ ] HVAC / water-heater / roof ages from age plates or documents **[pre-film]**
- [ ] Buyer's actual loan terms (rate, program, down payment) — shared at the level the buyer approves **[edit-ok]**
- [ ] Cleared tour-day macro photos for repaired findings ("tour-day photo" label rule) **[pre-film]**

## OPTIONAL (use only if clean)
- [ ] Aerial imagery (Taylor-flown stills, Part 107/LAANC gate) · survey · utility bills (seller-provided or buyer's first months) · prior sale history · lot-context ambient audio (road noise held clean)
- [ ] Owner cameo decision (≤20s, never pressured) · buyer's on-record quote about the decision

## BUYER SCENARIO CAPTURE (real buyer, shared per release election)
- Buyer profile in one line (owner-occupant; relocation/first-time/move-up — no protected-class framing)
- Budget ceiling · down payment · loan program & rate (actual) · monthly comfort line
- Top 3 priorities on tour day · risk tolerance notes (reserves available)
- **"Different buyer" beat for THE VERDICT:** one scripted line on how the verdict flips — e.g., "with $5K in reserves instead of $20K, this same scorecard is a WALK; the thresholds hold, the buyer changes." Keeps the verdict buyer-specific without inventing a composite.
