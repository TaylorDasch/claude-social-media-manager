The live-deal energy comes back later via the "tracked live, filmed after close" variant (Ep. 3+): dated desk-log entries while a real deal runs, de-identified, published only after close + release.

**Why this survives the "it's staged" attack:** ownership + consent disclosed on camera by 0:32; the thumbnail/hook *spend* the repair total up front ("You've had the number since you clicked — what you don't have is what it means"); the selection effect is admitted on camera (the sourcing gates make NEGOTIATE the expected output); and you grade your own tour-day calls against the inspector's report **including at least one miss**.
