## 2. What the data actually says (interpretation Taylor must own on-camera)

**The headline:** Temple is a **buyer's market for resale under $400K**, a **builder-controlled market for new construction**, and a **stale market for anything over $500K**.

**Three things a buyer needs to know going into May 2026:**

1. **Price cuts are the norm, not the exception.** If a listing has been on market 30+ days and has not cut, the seller is either anchored or doesn't need to sell. Both are red flags for negotiability.
2. **DOM 65–75 days is the new normal.** This is roughly 2x the COVID-era 30-day DOM. Sellers who priced for 2022 are getting punished. Buyers who waited are getting paid back.
3. **Builders are competing with the resale market for the first time in years.** 29% of active inventory is new construction. Builder incentives (rate buydowns, closing cost credits) are now table stakes — resale sellers cannot compete on financing terms, so they have to compete on price.

**Honest negatives that go in the video:**
- DOM is up because mortgage rates are still elevated — buyers SHOULD wait if they're not under a forcing function (PCS, BSW start date, lease end)
- "Median sold $293K" is misleading without showing the distribution — a chunk of those are sub-$200K starter homes that don't reflect the BSW/military buyer's $300–450K reality
- New construction looking like a "deal" via incentives often costs more long-term once HOA/MUD/PID dues stack up
- The luxury market over $500K is genuinely stuck — those sellers will eventually capitulate; do not buy at list
