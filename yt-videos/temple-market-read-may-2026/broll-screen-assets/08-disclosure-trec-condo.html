The information that is the subject of this notice is published on the Texas Department of Public Safety ("DPS") website at https://publicsite.dps.texas.gov. The Texas Code of Criminal Procedure Article 62.005(c) provides that the registration information for certain registered sex offenders is public information and shall be made available to the public.

Texas Code of Criminal Procedure Article 62.0061 provides:
(a) A person seeking information regarding registered sex offenders residing in this state may obtain the information by submitting a written request, in the manner prescribed by the department, to the department or to the local law enforcement authority in the municipality or county in which the person seeking the information resides.
(b) On receipt of a written request submitted under Subsection (a), the department or local law enforcement authority shall, as soon as practicable and within a reasonable amount of time, provide to the person any information that is required to be made available to the public.
