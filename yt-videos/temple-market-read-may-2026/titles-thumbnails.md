**Deepmode v3 versions.** Council-run alternate at `titles-thumbnails-council.md`.

## 3 TITLE OPTIONS

≤70 chars. No banned words.

### 1. (Brief-match, 65 chars)
`What's Actually Happening in Temple TX Real Estate — May 2026`

*Strength: matches the original brief verbatim. Plain. "Actually" promises an unfiltered read. SEO holds on the exact "Temple TX real estate" match.*

### 2. (Data-anchor, 67 chars) — RECOMMENDED PRIMARY
`54% of Temple TX Listings Have Price Drops — Here's What That Means`

*Strength: defensible specific stat (53.6% rounded). Open-loop ("means" forces watch). Survives fact-check. Highest projected CTR.*

### 3. (Cohort-anchor, 62 chars)
`Temple TX Buyers in May 2026: The Read Before You Tour a House`

*Strength: audience-tagged ("buyers"), action-tagged ("before you tour"). Lower viral pull, higher intent match.*

**Recommended A/B:** #2 vs #1 over first 72 hours, thumbnail held fixed. SEO floor protected either way.

## 3 THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS

### Thumbnail 1 — "54% HAVE PRICE DROPS" — PRIMARY

**Image direction:**
- Taylor center-frame in EG Realty polo, analyst-serious (NOT smiling, slight furrow)
- Left hand holding open laptop tilted toward camera; on-screen text "MLS Temple — 2026-05-14" with column of price-drop rows highlighted yellow
- Right hand pointing at the screen with extended index finger
- Background: kitchen-table or home-office wood, soft natural light from window-left
- Bold yellow text overlay top-left, two lines: "54% HAVE / PRICE DROPS"
- Bold white text overlay bottom-right, one line: "TEMPLE TX · MAY 2026"
- Small white-on-navy "LIVING IN TEMPLE" lozenge bottom-right

**Why it works:**
- Specific number anchor — survives fact-check
- Physical artifact (real MLS screen) = analyst credibility
- High contrast at YouTube grid size
- Date stamp on thumbnail signals freshness

### Thumbnail 2 — "TWO POOLS. ONE CITY."

**Image direction:**
- Vertical split screen, clean white divider down center
- LEFT half: Taylor with hands open, slight shrug; behind him an out-of-focus aged "FOR SALE" sign with "Price Reduced" rider visible
- RIGHT half: Taylor in identical pose-mirror but holding a "SOLD" rider; background freshly-staged front porch
- Bold yellow text overlay center-top, spanning divider: "TWO POOLS."
- Bold white text bottom-center: "ONE CITY."
- Small lozenge bottom-right: "LIVING IN TEMPLE · MAY 2026"

**Why it works:**
- Visual metaphor for editorial premise (note: "two pools" matches script language; we deliberately moved off "two markets" which is a real-estate cliché)
- Split-screen drives curiosity
- Verifiable artifact pairing

### Thumbnail 3 — "DON'T LOWBALL THIS HOUSE"

**Image direction:**
- Taylor in EG Realty polo, looking down at his phone with a frown, MLS listing visible on phone screen (over-the-shoulder angle)
- Other hand at chin in analyst-thinking posture
- Background: Temple street with a "FOR SALE" sign at a 1,800–2,200 sqft brick-front three-bed (the priced-right move-in-ready archetype)
- Bold red text overlay top: "DON'T LOWBALL"
- Bold white text below, smaller: "THIS HOUSE."
- Bottom-right lozenge: "TEMPLE TX · MAY 2026"

**Why it works:**
- Counter-intuitive — flips the 53% headline most viewers expect
- Pairs to the rule at 3:15 ("not real on a clean 1,800-to-2,200 sqft home in a desired feeder zone")
- Highest curiosity gap of the three

## A/B test protocol

- **Primary thumbnail:** #1 (data-anchor) for first 72 hours
- **A/B variant:** #3 (curiosity gap) — swap in if CTR drops below 5% at 24h
- **Tertiary:** #2 (two-pools metaphor) — swap if #1 and #3 both underperform at 72h
- **Hold the title fixed** during thumbnail A/B — only swap image
- **After 72 hours:** if Title #2 + Thumbnail #1 is the leader, hold. If Title #2 + Thumbnail #3 outperforms, hold both. Only swap title to #1 or #3 after 72h with documented CTR delta.
