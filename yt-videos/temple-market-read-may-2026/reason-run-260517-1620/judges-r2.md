## Judge 2: Contrarian

**Lens:** Attack the strongest claims. Take the opposing view.

**Vote: SHIP**

**Rationale:** The script earns its "buyer's market" framing because it explicitly pairs every leverage claim with the cost (rates 6.5–7.25, DTI 43% ceiling, refinance uncertainty). It does not pretend the buyer is winning — it argues the buyer has THIS particular leverage in THIS particular condition. That's defensible.

**Critiques (one MAJOR, two MINOR):**
1. **MAJOR:** "Half have cuts. The other half are going to" (luxury segment, 9:30) is a forecast. The script earlier disclaimed forecasts. Tighten to: "Half have cuts. The other half is sitting at original list past their normal selling window — make of that what you will." Removes the forward-looking claim while preserving the implication.
2. **MINOR:** "Sellers blink first now" (1:05 opening line of Change 1) is a colorful frame but unprovable for the universe of sellers. Soften to: "Sellers are the ones moving their numbers now."
3. **MINOR:** "The 2021 'see it Friday, offer Saturday, beat by an out-of-town cash offer Sunday' market is done" is great content but "done" is a strong verb. Consider "is gone for now" — same point, smaller forecast surface.
