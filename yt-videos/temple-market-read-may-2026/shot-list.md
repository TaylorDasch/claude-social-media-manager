## Filming Notes

1. **Pre-stage the laptop tabs.** Two browser tabs side-by-side, named exactly "MLS 05-08-2026" and "MLS 05-14-2026." Pre-load to the views Taylor wants on camera. Practice the 180° rotation 3 times before tape rolls.
2. **Print the cover sheet** before the shoot. "MLS Snapshot, City of Temple, 2026-05-14" in 24pt sans-serif, centered, on plain paper. Should be legible from 4 ft (the lens distance).
3. **Print the sample-listings list** for Pool A reveal — same paper stock. Three highlighted in yellow (the most-egregious examples from the lineage data — Lakeaire, Cliffs of Canyon Creek, Whatley Acres).
4. **Lower-third date stamp** must persist from 1:00 onward. Set in Descript / Final Cut as a static layer.
5. **BSW campus B-roll** — confirm location-safe before filming. Public sidewalk shot of the campus exterior is fine; do NOT film signage in a way that could be read as implied endorsement or licensed partnership. If in doubt, use a generic medical-office exterior.
6. **Filming target window:** week of 2026-05-19. Re-pull MLS data within 24-48hr of camera-on. Update the script verbally if any headline number moves >3 percentage points.
