# Titles + Thumbnails — Temple TX May 2026 Market Read

## 3 TITLE OPTIONS (CTR-tested intuitions, distinct angles)

### Title 1 (RECOMMENDED — leverages the headline statistic)
**"Temple TX Real Estate Right Now: Half of Sellers Already Cut Their Price (May 2026 Data)"**

- Length: 84 chars (under YouTube 100-char cap, full display on mobile)
- Angle: data-shock — "half of sellers cut" is the most striking single fact
- Search intent: "Temple TX real estate" + "right now" (recency) + month-anchored
- Audience pull: BSW/military/relocator searching "Temple TX real estate market"
- Why it wins: The number alone earns the click without theater

### Title 2 (Broader appeal — generic search match)
**"What's Actually Happening in Temple TX Real Estate (May 2026 Market Read)"**

- Length: 73 chars
- Angle: explanatory — direct answer to the most common search query format
- Search intent: "what's happening Temple real estate"
- Audience pull: Anyone Googling "Temple TX market" without a specific question
- Trade-off: more searches but less click-distinguishable in the SERP

### Title 3 (Decision-frame angle)
**"Buy a House in Temple TX Now or Wait? The Honest Numbers (May 2026)"**

- Length: 72 chars
- Angle: decision-frame — captures the high-intent "should I buy" searcher
- Search intent: "should I buy a house Temple TX"
- Audience pull: BSW physician + DFW relocator both have this exact question
- Trade-off: more transactional, but the script's "who waits" section earns it

**Recommendation:** Test Title 1 for first 24-48 hours. If CTR <4%, swap to Title 3. Title 2 is the fallback "no risk" option.

## 3 THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS

The script's "one creative element" rule applies to in-video graphics. The thumbnail is a separate decision — present 3 distinct concepts and pick on the strongest one.

### Thumbnail 1 (RECOMMENDED — data-anchored, analyst-tone)
**Composition:**
- Vertical split, 50/50
- LEFT: Taylor on-camera, neutral expression, looking directly at viewer, EG Realty polo, plain background
- RIGHT: Single massive number "52.6%" in sans-serif (Helvetica/Inter), white on dark background
- Below the number, smaller: "of Temple sellers cut their price"
- Bottom-right corner: "MAY 2026" date stamp in small caps
- Background detail: faint pattern of repeating "PRICE REDUCED" stickers, low opacity

**Why it wins:** The number does the work. No red arrows. No exclamation marks. The viewer sees a statistic that matters and a person who looks like he can explain it. Analyst, not alarm. Matches the channel voice.

**Avoid:** any face expression that telegraphs "shocked" or "concerned." Neutral or slight inquisitive. Eyes to camera.

### Thumbnail 2 (Map-anchored, geographic specificity)
**Composition:**
- Top half: a clean, stylized line map of Temple-Belton with red pins on the top 5 subdivisions (Three Creeks, Mesa Ridge, Pecan Creek, Hubbard Branch, Oak Ridge), each labeled
- Bottom half: Taylor on-camera, plain background, with text overlay "MAY 2026 MARKET READ" in clean caps
- Color: muted blue + white + single red accent on pins

**Why this might win:** Search-anchored — when a viewer searching "Temple TX neighborhoods" sees the map thumbnail, it disambiguates from generic real-estate clickbait.

**Trade-off:** Less data shock, more orientation. Better for the slow-build search traffic; worse for impulse CTR.

### Thumbnail 3 (Question-frame, decision-anchored)
**Composition:**
- Center: Taylor on-camera, on the left third
- Right two-thirds: two stacked words — "BUY?" (top, in green) and "WAIT?" (bottom, in amber) — large, bold
- Below: small "Temple TX • May 2026" in caps
- Background: blurred-out For Sale sign

**Why this might win:** Pairs with Title 3 if Taylor chooses the decision-frame title. The question-mark face activates curiosity.

**Trade-off:** Slightly more clickbait energy than Thumbnail 1. May erode analyst voice for repeat viewers.

## CHOICE GUIDANCE

- **For first publish:** Thumbnail 1 + Title 1. The data shock + analyst tone is the channel's strongest positioning, and the first monthly read sets the precedent.
- **For A/B test on Day 2 if CTR <4%:** Swap to Thumbnail 3 + Title 3 (decision-frame combo).
- **For the long-term search archive:** Thumbnail 2 + Title 2 may outperform on evergreen "Temple TX real estate market" search over 6+ months.
