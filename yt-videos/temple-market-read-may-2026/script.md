## TWO INVENTORY POOLS (1:30–4:00)

### 1:30–2:30 — Why "53% have price drops" reads wrong when flat

**(A) [Back to kitchen-table wide. Printed list of 8 sample listings, three highlighted.]**

"The headline mistake. Someone reads 'fifty-three percent of listings have price cuts' and concludes the market is broken. Or that every Temple listing is open to a lowball. Both wrong. The fifty-three percent is true. But it averages two completely different inventory pools that have to be read separately.

Of those 459 listings with drops, the median drop is fifteen thousand on a $287K median. Four-and-a-half percent. That's not panic — that's a seller who priced ambitiously, watched a listing sit thirty days, and corrected. A working market doing what it does.

Then there's the other tail. Eighty-six listings have dropped ten percent or more. That's where the real reductions are. And they cluster in two specific places — once you see which two, the fifty-three percent number tells a much more useful story."

**(A) [Same set. Taylor reads from the printed list.]**

"Pool A — the ten-percent-plus drops. I pulled the eight largest current drops in Temple active inventory. They cluster in two segments.

**First segment: older luxury, north of $800K, priced into a 2022 market, now finding 2026 air.** Lakeaire, Cliffs of Canyon Creek, Wildflower, Crooked Creek. Custom homes from the nineties and early two-thousands. Sellers anchored to peak comp. Market moved. The ones still on the board today haven't moved enough.

## MILITARY PCS-WINDOW BUYERS (5:30–6:45)

**(A) [Same shot frame.]**

"Military PCS — summer 2026 move. School-year deadline is the constraint. Back-solve from August first.

If your kids start school in Killeen ISD or Temple ISD by August fifteenth, you need the household out of a hotel by August first. VA appraisal and close typically runs forty-five to sixty days from contract — and I'd pad that another ten to fourteen for Temple's appraiser availability, which is finite this time of year. Working backward, that means you need to be under contract by mid-June. From mid-May, you have roughly four weeks of shopping window.

That changes how the fifty-three percent price-drop pool serves you. The leverage is NOT to wait for fresh listings to age into a discount. You don't have that calendar. The leverage IS to target listings ALREADY past forty days TODAY. Those sellers have already absorbed the wait. They want a buyer with financing and a defined close window. A VA-financing offer with a real pre-approval and a clean appraisal request lands well there.

What I'd do:

**One.** Pull your real BAH-to-house number first. Most households I work with come in fifteen to twenty-five percent low on the first pass — meaning they can afford more house than they initially budgeted, especially at current rates in 76502 and 76504. Calculator in description.

**Two.** Filter your MLS search to listings already at forty-plus days on market in your zip code and price band — that's where the May-into-June leverage lives.

**Three.** Pad your close calendar by ten to fourteen days against what your lender quotes. I've watched VA closes slip a calendar week because the appraiser was already booked. That's not Temple-specific, but it bites here.

**Honest counter:** if your orders shifted and your August deadline moved, the rest of this changes. Re-pull this section against your actual date — same logic, different math."

## WHAT COULD CHANGE THE READ (7:45–9:00)

**(A) [Back to kitchen-table wide. Laptop visible to side, closed.]**

"Three things could change the read in the next thirty to sixty days. Most market-update videos skip this part — that's the part I want you to take seriously.

**One. Rates.** Every number in this video assumes mortgage rates roughly where they sit at the date stamp on screen. If rates move fifty basis points either direction over the next thirty days, Pool B — priced-right move-in-ready — either tightens further as more buyers come in, or Pool A grows as more sellers get stuck. I have no edge on the Fed. Track the actual rate.

**Two. Builder supply.** Temple's months-of-supply, around five point eight months on active-to-thirty-day-sold, gets pushed by builder release schedules. If three or four big builders push large spec inventory into late May and June, months-of-supply rises, price-cut percentage rises, leverage shifts further toward buyers. I track builder incentives separately — link below — early warning.

**Three. The eighty-four-day median DOM is backward-looking.** It's median DOM on listings closed in the last thirty days. The listings going under contract over the next thirty to sixty days become future DOM data. If contract-to-close cycles run shorter than what's in current data, DOM drops in the June and July pulls. If longer, it rises. I'll re-pull in thirty days and post an update.

If any of these three flips materially, follow-up video. Subscribe if you want it. If none flip, the read I gave you holds through June."
