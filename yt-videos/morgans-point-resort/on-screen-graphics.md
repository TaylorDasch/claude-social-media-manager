## Visual treatment

- **Palette:** near-black, warm white, lake blue, one amber warning color.
- **Type:** condensed sans for numbers; clean sans for qualifiers.
- **Motion:** reveal one idea at a time. Do not show all lines at once.
- **Maps:** use a clean outline of the city and lake. Parcel boundaries require a `VERIFY WITH SURVEY` label.
- **Water card:** show a shoreline photo with a property-line overlay stopping before the water; label it `ILLUSTRATION — NOT A PARCEL SURVEY`.
