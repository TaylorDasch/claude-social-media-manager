# Morgan's Point Resort — Shot List

> Rebuilt 2026-07-31 for the three-check buyer-decision script.

## Before filming

- Confirm public access and launch/landing rules for every location.
- Confirm FAA airspace and Part 107 requirements before drone use.
- Do not enter private property without written permission.
- Keep addresses, house numbers, occupants, vehicles, and yard signs unreadable.
- Do not use an identifiable manufactured home as the visual for a downside.
- Check the current lake level before selecting shoreline framing; do not imply normal pool from an old image.

## Minimum pickup list

1. Hook clean read, two takes.
2. Comp-rule line clean read.
3. Property-specific disclaimer clean read.
4. USACE dock-rule block clean read.
5. Survey/GIS boundary line clean read.
6. Fair-housing-safe street-variable block clean read.
7. CTA clean read.

## Editing rhythm

- First 30 seconds: visual change every 4–6 seconds without cutting inside the hook sentence.
- Middle: one visual or audio reset every 7 seconds.
- Put the words `HERE'S ONE`, `HERE'S TWO`, and `HERE'S THREE` on screen as spoken.
- Hold the USACE prohibition card long enough to read, then cut back to Taylor.
- Do not use drone footage as a standalone scenery segment; every aerial carries a buyer decision or sourced graphic.

## Fallbacks

- No drone permission: ground gimbal, public overlook, and map animation.
- Poor lake level/appearance: use current ground footage and state nothing about typical lake level.
- No permissioned inspection details: licensed close-ups plus graphic checklist.
- No safe street footage: map animation and Taylor to camera.
