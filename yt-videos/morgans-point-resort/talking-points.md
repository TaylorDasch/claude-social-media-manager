## HOW TO USE THIS

Three kinds of content on this page. Treat them differently:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Riff** | Bullets. Say it your way. Don't memorize. |
| 🔒 **SAY EXACTLY** | Read it. Numbers and compliance language. Paraphrasing these creates either a wrong number or a legal problem. |
| **Land on** | The transition. Get to roughly this idea before you cut. |

**Pacing:** each beat has a time budget. Speaking free runs 15–25% longer than scripted — at 11:35 scripted you have 25 seconds to the 12:00 ceiling, so unmanaged riffing lands you at 13:00–14:30. Watch the clock on B4, B5, B6. If you're long, **cut B9's shop/RV counts. Never cut B9b.**

**The six sentences that are non-negotiable** (all marked 🔒 below): the water-provenance line in B4, the n=7 admission in B5, the "I am not telling you any specific home" line in B6, the list-price boundary in B7, and both compliance framings in B9b. ### ⚠️ COMPLIANCE — READ BEFORE SHOOTING

Discussing deed restrictions, zoning and property types as **resale variables** is legitimate buyer counsel. Discussing them in terms of **who lives there** is a fair-housing violation.

- **Never** characterize residents by income, class, family status, or any protected characteristic — not by word, not by tone, not by B-roll choice.
- **Never** say or imply manufactured homes lower values. That's an unevidenced valuation claim and the most screenshot-able line you could utter.
- **Never** film an identifiable manufactured home as an illustration of a downside.
- **Never** steer toward or away from any section.
- Give the variable and the method. The buyer decides.

**This is the beat where riffing is most dangerous.** Read the two 🔒 blocks. Ad-lib the setup only.
