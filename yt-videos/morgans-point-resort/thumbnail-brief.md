# 2. TITLE — RECOMMENDED + 8 ALTERNATES

**RECOMMENDED:**

> **Morgan's Point Resort TX: The Cheapest Home Isn't $315K**

55 characters — survives mobile truncation intact. Exact-match keyword front-loaded. States a price contradiction a buyer can act on, and asserts nothing about any portal, search engine, or third party — only about a price. The thumbnail supplies the other half of the contradiction ($205K) and the mechanism (11 of 15 say Belton), so title and thumbnail stack rather than repeat.

**On the open loop, stated honestly:** the *factual* loop ("then what is it?") closes at 0:14, inside the hook, by design. Front-loading the payoff is correct for a utility video — withholding a number the viewer came for costs more trust than it buys retention. The *retention* loop is different and stays open the whole runtime: three problems named at 0:40, resolved at 2:15, 5:05, and 6:35.

# 3. THUMBNAIL CONCEPT

**Composition — three visual elements, no fourth:**

1. **Background — drone frame, golden hour.** Belton Lake filling the left two-thirds, MPR shoreline homes along the right edge of the water, low and wide enough that the peninsula shape reads. Warm, real, slightly underexposed sky. No teal-orange grade, no flare, no vignette.
2. **Taylor, right third**, chest-up, looking out at the water rather than at camera. Neutral. Not selling.
3. **Text block, left**, over the water where contrast is highest.

**EXACT THUMBNAIL TEXT:**

```
$315K
$205K
11 OF 15 SAY BELTON
```

- Line 1: `$315K` at 60% scale, muted gray-white, with a heavy diagonal strike-through in amber.
- Line 2: `$205K` at full scale, heavy condensed sans, white with a 3px near-black stroke. This is the largest object on the card and must read at 168px wide.
- Line 3: `11 OF 15 SAY BELTON` at 30% scale, all caps, tight tracking, white.

**Emotional hook: *retrieval*.** Not curiosity — correction. The target viewer has already formed a price for this town and, in a meaningful share of cases, has already decided against it. The card tells them the number they are carrying is wrong and is wrong in their favor. That is a stronger click driver than a mystery, because it addresses a decision they have already made and quietly regret being unable to afford.

**Why the split works:** the title carries the *problem* ($315K isn't the floor), the thumbnail carries the *answer* ($205K) plus the *mechanism* (11 of 15 say Belton). Nobody has to hold a mystery. They have to hold a question about *why*, which the first ninety seconds pays off.

**Do not include:** a sunset silhouette, a boat, a FOR SALE sign graphic, a red circle or arrow, an open-mouth reaction face, a house photo, or the $869K build as hero art. No luxury framing — luxury framing is the exact impression this video exists to correct.

**Reserve variant (test at day 14 if CTR is under target):** same plate, text swapped to `THE $205K HALF` / `IS FILED UNDER BELTON`. Trades the price shock for message clarity and aligns the card to alternate title 1.
