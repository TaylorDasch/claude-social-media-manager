## 3. THUMBNAIL CONCEPT

### On-thumbnail text

**Primary (large, top third):**
> ONLY 1 IS LAKEFRONT

**Sub-text (smaller, bottom third):**
> Morgan's Point Resort · $250K

(Total visible words: 6. No emoji. No exclamation marks. No "EXCLUSIVE" / "INSIDER" / "SECRET" / "WARNING" tags.)

### Visual composition (16:9, 1280×720)

| Layer | Content |
|---|---|
| **Background** | Drone shot of Belton Lake at golden hour. Left ~55% lake/shoreline, right ~45% partial neighborhood overhead. Sky in upper third for text contrast. |
| **Midground** | Three small home photos arranged left-to-right in a horizontal row, sized small (each ~15% of frame width). Leftmost has a yellow checkmark overlay. Middle and right each have a red X overlay. Houses are exterior-only, identifiable as lake-area homes but no addresses, no MLS numbers, no recognizable owners' vehicles in driveways. |
| **Foreground** | Taylor positioned right third of frame, half-body, looking at the row of homes (not at camera). Right arm subtly pointing at the leftmost (the one with the checkmark). Wearing a casual technical-looking layered top (no suit, no logo polo). Natural composure — not "agent grin" face. |
| **Text overlay** | Top third: `ONLY 1 IS LAKEFRONT` in bold sans-serif, white with slight navy stroke. Bottom third: `Morgan's Point Resort · $250K` in smaller weight, white. |

### Color palette

- Lake blue / navy / deep teal — Belton Lake
- White / cream — text
- One yellow accent — the checkmark
- Red X marks (small, restrained)

High contrast. No saturated red bar across the top. No yellow circle overlay on Taylor's face.

### Emotional hook (one sentence)

> Three homes, same price — and most buyers can't tell which one is actually on the water.

The reader feels: *I need to know which one before I write an offer.*

### Hard NOs

- No "DREAM home" / "DREAM lake" anywhere in text or title.
- No red circle around Taylor's face.
- No giant arrow.
- No "STUNNING" / "GORGEOUS" / "PARADISE."
- No "TOP" / "BEST" / "INSIDER" / "EXCLUSIVE" / "SECRET."
- No Fair Housing-implied imagery (no family-with-children stock, no demographic signaling).
- No watermark or brokerage logo over the lake — disclosure lives in description.
- No fake "SOLD" banner on any of the three home photos unless the home actually sold.
