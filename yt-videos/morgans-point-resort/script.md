# 5. RETENTION OUTLINE — 10:00, WITH TIMESTAMPS

## 5.0 The two binding fixes, stated before the outline

**W-11 fix — the geography beat is deleted as a beat.** The prior structure put 85 seconds of drone footage and provenance disclaimer at 1:55, the steepest point on any retention curve, carrying zero data points. Two changes:

1. **Drone footage is demoted from segment to substrate.** It runs *under* data beats for the whole runtime instead of owning a slot. No segment exists whose reason-to-watch is "look at the lake."
2. **The geography argument is merged into the tier beat (B4) and given a number to carry** — the $151→$310 $/sqft spread `[CONFIRMED, §E]` and the subdivision reveal. Geography now explains a price column instead of narrating scenery. The water-provenance disclaimer shrinks from a 45-word standalone confession to **one sentence spoken on top of a data point** — it is still said out loud, in full, and it is still un-cuttable, but it no longer *is* a segment. The MLS-can't-verify point is delivered as a reason the tier map is drawn from $/sqft rather than from a shoreline field, which makes the disclaimer load-bearing instead of apologetic.
3. **Second sag fixed:** the 90-second "go verify with these agencies" block is cut to 65 seconds, made MPR-specific by carrying the HOA 27/2 count and the Belton ISD 29/29 count, and **no graphic is held longer than 8 seconds anywhere in the runtime** (the prior version held one card for 85 seconds against its own 7-second rule).

**W-12 fix — the runtime is budgeted in words, per beat, with the holds subtracted first.** Method, applied to every beat below:

```
spoken_seconds  = beat_seconds − hold_seconds        (hold = graphic hold / drone breath / silence, no VO)
word_cap        = spoken_seconds × 2.9167            (175 wpm ÷ 60)
```

Holds are subtracted **before** the word cap is computed, which is exactly the step the prior version skipped. Full arithmetic table at §5.2. **A beat that exceeds its word cap is cut in the script, not in the edit** — that rule exists because the first thing an editor cuts to save 90 seconds is the disclaimer language, and the disclaimers are the package.
