## 1. The public thesis

The video helps one viewer—someone considering a home in Morgan's Point Resort—make one decision: **how to compare the available homes before treating list price as the answer.**

The three checks are:

1. Compare the home with properties that compete for the same buyer: similar market area, site, size, age, condition, style, legal characteristics, and relevant property rights.
2. Price the ownership items that do not fit neatly in list price: major systems, insurance, and septic where applicable.
3. Separate a Lake Belton view or access statement from shoreline ownership and dock rights.

## 3. How to talk about comps

Fannie Mae's current comparable-sales guidance says comparable sales should have similar physical and legal characteristics and should come from the same market area when possible. It names factors including site, room count, finished area, style, and condition. It also says the selected sales should compete for the same market participants.

**Source:** https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.3-08/comparable-sales

### Shipped on-camera wording

> "When I run comps here, I start with nearby closed sales that compete for the same buyer—similar site, size, age, condition, style, and property rights. If I need to expand outside that immediate set, I explain why and adjust for the real differences."

### Retired conclusion

The earlier package compared the median price of four records whose MLS city field said `Morgans Point Resort` with eleven whose field said `Belton`, then treated the difference as a pricing signal. **That conclusion is retired.** The two subsets had different housing-stock composition, including the two newest and highest-priced developments in the four-record subset. The comparison does not isolate a city-field effect.

The city field may still matter to internal search discovery. It is not part of the public video's pricing argument, title, hook, thumbnail, graphics, description, Shorts, or CTA.

### USACE rule

The December 2018 Belton Lake Master Plan states that private shoreline uses are not allowed at Belton Lake. It specifically identifies privately owned floating boat docks and fixed or movable piers among the private uses covered by the rule.

**Primary source:** https://www.swf.usace.army.mil/Portals/47/docs/Lakes/Belton/MasterPlan/Belton_Lake_Master_Plan_Final_Complete_Dec_2018.pdf?ver=2019-01-03-114622-650

USACE has also publicly referred to people in Morgan's Point as adjacent landowners entering Government property. This supports the broad statement that federal land generally separates many residential lots from the water. It does **not** establish the boundary for every lot.

**Primary source:** https://www.swf.usace.army.mil/Media/News-Releases/Article/2529640/usace-officials-caution-pet-owners-about-recreation-at-belton-lake/

### Shipped on-camera boundary
