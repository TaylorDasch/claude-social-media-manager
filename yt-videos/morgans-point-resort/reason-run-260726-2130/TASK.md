## Core local insight to build from

The $330K median is nearly meaningless here — actives run $205K to $869K in the same small town
because water proximity, elevation, lot position, and *which development you're actually in* set the
price, not the floor plan. The real decision is which tier you're buying into, and whether a
1970s–80s house at a lake-town price survives inspection math (roof, foundation, systems) better than
a new build elsewhere at the same monthly payment.

## Honest tradeoffs that must appear (Scars and All)

- Median 1979 build year on actives = inspection/insurance/updating costs are the hidden second price tag
- The pre-1980 stock is the overhang, not the bargain — zero pre-1985 homes closed in this window
- 93-day median DOM on closed, both pendings sat 200+ days, 47% of actives have cut price — this is
  NOT a fast-turn market. Sellers overprice; buyers have room.
- Small-town services: groceries/dining/major retail are a drive away in Belton/Temple — state the
  real measured drive or tell the viewer to test it at their own hour
- Lake levels fluctuate — do not promise waterfront usability; send viewers to USACE for current
  Belton Lake levels before falling for a "lake view" listing
