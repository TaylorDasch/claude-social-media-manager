CODEX-FINDING-CHECK: **CONFIRMED** — Verified independently against GROUND-TRUTH §A. The MLS values behind `Active Under Contract 4, median $273,250` and `Pending 2, median $232,450` are **list prices**. No sale price exists for a home that has not closed; the AUC and Pending cohorts have no agreed number in the export, and the ground truth reports none. The draft's G-LADDER narration at 8:05 — *"what sellers are asking and what buyers are agreeing to are not in the same neighborhood right now"* — sits directly on top of a four-row graphic in which **three of the four rows are seller ask** (Active $330,000, Under Contract $273,250, Pending $232,450) and only one is an agreed price (Closed $220,000). Short 4 ships the same error stripped of the caveat: *"Sellers are asking above what buyers are agreeing to."* The draft anticipated the adjacent objection — it defends against reading the ladder as a time series (*"four different groups of houses, not one house over time"*) — but that caveat addresses the wrong error. The defect is not temporal, it is definitional: two of the four medians are not prices anyone agreed to. And the underlying descent is largely composition, since the cheaper end of the market goes under contract first by selection; the AUC and Pending cohorts are cheaper *because* cheaper homes convert, which is a sorting artifact narrated as a negotiation gap. Codex's finding is correct and, if anything, understated.
