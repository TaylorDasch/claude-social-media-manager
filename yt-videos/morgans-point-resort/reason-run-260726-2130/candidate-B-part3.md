# 8. SHOT LIST — ONE GOLDEN-HOUR TRIP

**Constraint:** public, permission-safe locations only. No trespass, no listing access, no filming a private
residence as the subject of a claim. Drive-by and drone-from-public-airspace only.

## 8a. Pre-flight — do this the day before, not on location

| Check | Why | Blocks the shoot if unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| **B4UFLY / FAA airspace** for Morgan's Point Resort + Belton Lake shoreline | Fort Cavazos restricted airspace (R-6302) sits west of Belton Lake. MPR is on the southeast shore and is normally clear, but verify the day of — restricted-area boundaries and TFRs are not a thing to eyeball. | **YES** |
| Part 107 currency, aircraft registration, battery charge (4+ packs) | 6 drone setups at golden hour is 3–4 packs minimum with no time to recharge | **YES** |
| Sunset time for the shoot date (late July, Temple TX ≈ 8:25 PM CDT) | Every time below is relative to it | **YES** |
| Belton Lake current pool level (USACE) | If the lake is well below normal pool, the shoreline shots tell a different story than the script assumes — and the script must not imply a water level it didn't verify | NO — but rewrite the water line |
| Confirm which streets are water-facing vs interior | **The MLS has no waterfront field.** This is Taylor's own knowledge, and shots D3/D4/G3 depend on it | NO — graphics-only fallback exists |

## 8b. The window is 50 minutes, not 75

Golden hour in late July runs roughly **7:35–8:25 PM**; usable blue hour extends to about **8:50 PM**.
Arrive **6:45 PM** — 100 minutes before sunset — so that setup, scouting, and the two light-insensitive
ground shots are already done before the light that actually matters arrives.

| Block | Clock | Light | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup + scout | 6:45–7:15 | flat, pre-golden | Park, airspace re-check, battery in, scout the two drone launch points |
| Light-insensitive ground | 7:15–7:35 | pre-golden | G1, G2 — shots that do not need golden light |
| **GOLDEN — drone priority** | **7:35–8:15** | **golden** | **D1, D2, D3, D4 — the only 40 minutes that cannot be recreated** |
| Golden — ground | 8:15–8:35 | late golden / blue | G3, G4 |
| Blue hour buffer | 8:35–8:50 | blue | D5 if batteries and light allow, else wrap |

**Scheduled: 46 minutes of setups inside a 50-minute golden window, with a 15-minute blue-hour buffer.**
That closes — but only because the two light-insensitive ground shots are moved *ahead* of golden hour and
the A-roll is not shot on this trip at all (see 8e).

## 8e. What is NOT on this trip, deliberately

- **A-roll (the talking-head script) is not shot at golden hour.** Burning irreplaceable golden light on a
  shot that can be filmed at any time on any day is the most common way this trip fails. Shoot A-roll
  separately. Only **G2** is on this list, because it must be shot on location to be real.
- **No interiors. No listing access. No filming any home as the subject of a claim.** G3's two exteriors are
  illustrative of an era, and the script must say so — never "this house has problems."
- **No drive-time number** unless G4 actually captures a measured clock with the departure hour visible.
  No measurement, no number on screen. That is a hard rule, not a preference.

| **G3** (1970s vs post-2015 exteriors) | Identifying era from the street | Replace with **G-VINTAGE** (§H1b): name-carrying median build year 2012.5 vs Belton-filed 1977, over D1/D2. Stronger, and it needs no house on screen at all. |

**Read that fallback column carefully: both fallbacks are arguably better than the shots they replace,**
because both move the claim from something observed to something recounted. If the golden-hour trip is
rained out entirely, this video still ships on graphics plus A-roll. That is by design.

## 9a. Two graphics that were cut, and why

- **The four-median ladder** ($330,000 → $273,250 → $232,450 → $220,000). Cut. Three of those four are
  *asking* prices — under-contract and pending homes have no sale price until they close — so the ladder
  narrates seller ask as buyer agreement, across four different cohorts of different-sized homes (§H4).
- **"Active median $330,000 vs sold median $220,000 = your negotiating room."** Cut. That 33% gap is
  mostly a size difference, not a discount (§H3). **G-10** replaces it with the real number, and says on
  screen why the bigger number is wrong — which is worth more trust than the bigger number was worth drama.

## 9b. Graphic-to-narration integrity check

Each graphic was checked against the line it plays under. The specific failure being guarded against: a
graphic sorted by list price under narration saying "list price is not the signal." **G-TIER is sorted by
$/sqft** for exactly that reason. If the edit re-sorts it by price to look tidier, the video contradicts
itself on screen.
