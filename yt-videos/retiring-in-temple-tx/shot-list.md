## SHORT 3 — "The over-65 tax freeze" *(little-known money beat)*
**Micro-hook (0–3s):** "The Texas retirement tax break nobody tells you about until it's too late."
**Body:**
> The year you turn 65, file the over-65 homestead exemption and your school district taxes get capped at that year's amount — even as your home's value rises. There are extra over-65 exemptions too. Your savings depend on your rate and home value, so run your real numbers with the appraisal district. And it can transfer if you move — but there's a one-time filing window, so ask which certificate to file the year you move.
