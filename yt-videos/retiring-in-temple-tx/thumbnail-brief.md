## ⭐ CONCEPT 1 — "TEMPLE vs GEORGETOWN" *(RECOMMENDED — money + healthcare in one frame, matches the contrarian hook)*
- **Overlay text:** `TEMPLE vs GEORGETOWN` (5 words counting the "vs"? — it's 3 words; well under cap)
- **Composition:** Vertical split. **Left (Temple):** BSW hospital tower photo + a bold number `~$255K`. **Right (Georgetown):** a generic suburban/Sun-City-style image + `~$425K`. Thin emerald divider down the middle.

## CONCEPT 2 — "THE 10-MINUTE HOSPITAL" *(healthcare-led)*
- **Overlay text:** `THE 10-MINUTE HOSPITAL`
- **Composition:** Taylor (medium shot) gesturing toward the BSW tower behind/beside him. A red `LEVEL I` stamp/badge over the building.

## CONCEPT 3 — "DON'T RETIRE HERE IF…" *(the honesty / catch angle)*
- **Overlay text:** `DON'T RETIRE HERE IF…`
- **Composition:** Taylor, arms crossed, calm and serious (not smiling), set against a clean Temple neighborhood or BSW backdrop. Bold text stacked beside him. Emerald accent underline on "DON'T."
- **Why it works:** Strong curiosity gap + signals the honest "who should NOT" beat that builds trust. The ellipsis forces the click. Pairs with the negative beat at 4:59.

## A/B suggestion
Launch with **Concept 1 (TEMPLE vs GEORGETOWN)**. If CTR underperforms after the first ~48h impressions, swap to **Concept 3 (DON'T RETIRE HERE IF…)** — the "don't" framing tends to over-index on an older, decision-stage audience. Don't run Concept 2 first; "10-minute hospital" is the weaker cold-open of the three for a feed that doesn't yet know the channel.
