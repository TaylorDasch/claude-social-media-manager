**Body:**

Most Texas retirement advice points you straight to Georgetown's Sun City. I want to give you the comparison nobody actually runs — and I'll tell you up front who *should* still pick Georgetown.

I'm Taylor Dasch with EG Realty here in Temple. I work with retirees and downsizers most weeks, so this is the real picture, not a brochure.

**The number that frames everything**
The median home in Temple is running around **$275K** right now (June 2026 MLS). In Georgetown it's about **$455K**. Same budget, roughly a **$180K gap** — and you don't trade away healthcare to get it.

**The healthcare edge**
Temple has a **Level I trauma center right in the middle of town** — the only one on I-35 between Dallas and Austin — plus a full VA hospital (the Olin E. Teague Veterans' Medical Center). A flagship hospital and a full VA in one midsize city is rare, and it's the #1 reason most retirees I meet land here.

**The tax piece people miss**
At 65, your school taxes on your homestead freeze. That freeze can even transfer to your next Texas home — but it's proportional, not dollar-for-dollar, and there's a filing deadline. Get it wrong and you can lose it. (Confirm the certificate and deadline with your county appraisal district — I walk through it in the full guide.)

**The honest catch**
Temple has no Sun City-style 55+ resort community. If that lifestyle — the master plan, the activities director — is your picture, **Georgetown is the better call.** I'd rather you hear that now.
