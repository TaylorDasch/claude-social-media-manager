## CORE EVIDENCE (use these — all from the live page; verify before stating any as current)

**Healthcare (the unfair advantage — lead with it):**
- Baylor Scott & White Medical Center, Temple: 636-bed **Level I trauma center** — the *only* one on the I-35 corridor between Dallas and Austin. 30+ specialties, 8,800+ employees.
- Specialist wait times ~2–3 weeks vs 4–6 weeks in Austin. Cardiology consults typically 2–3 weeks. Scott & White Research Institute = clinical-trial access.
- **Olin E. Teague Veterans' Medical Center** (VA) is in Temple — major draw for military retirees.
- Medicare Advantage plans in Bell County rated 4–4.5 stars (CMS).

**Money:**
- No Texas state income tax on Social Security, pensions, or retirement distributions.
- Over-65 **school-tax freeze** + extra over-65 exemptions → ~$2,400–$3,200/yr saved on a $300K home; freeze is **transferable** to a new Texas home (via the School Tax Ceiling Certificate / Form 50-272, within one year).
- Cost-of-living index ~82–86 (national = 100). Median home ~$255K. Retired couple monthly ≈ $3,530–$4,580.
- Comparison: Temple median ~$255K vs **Georgetown ~$425K** vs **Fredericksburg ~$490K+** — roughly **60% of Georgetown's housing cost with better healthcare access.**

**Housing for retirees (be honest):**
- Good fits: Bella Terra ($280–400K, newer, near BSW), Three Creeks ($260–380K, Belton Lake trails), Canyon Creek ($180–350K, mature trees/larger lots), Lake Pointe ($240–340K, single-story options).
- **Honest negative (the credibility moment):** Temple does NOT have a large 55+ age-restricted community like Sun City in Georgetown. If a gated, golf-cart, activities-director lifestyle is the dealbreaker, Temple is the wrong call — and the video should say so plainly.

**Other honest tradeoffs:** hot summers; smaller airport (fly via Austin/Waco; Amtrak available); it's a medical/working city, not a resort town.
