## FAQ copy for the future page

### What is a house hack?

A house hack is an owner-occupied primary-residence strategy in which the buyer rents part of the property—such as a room, a legal second dwelling, or another unit in a two-to-four-unit property—to help offset housing costs.

### What makes a good house hack?

A potentially strong candidate has a documented legal rental setup, an available and livable owner space, a borrower- and property-specific financing path, a full monthly cost that works under conservative assumptions, and a realistic plan for vacancy, repairs, management, and a future move.

### Is a fully leased duplex a good house hack?

It may be a valid rental property, but it may not fit an owner-occupancy plan if no legal unit is available inside the lender-confirmed occupancy timing. Review the leases, possession, loan, and borrower plan before drawing a conclusion.

### Can I count future rent to qualify?

Treatment varies by program, borrower, property, appraisal, documentation, and lender. Do not assume advertised or future rent will count. Ask the lender to review the exact address and scenario.

### Is house hacking passive income?

Not inherently. The owner may need to screen renters, administer leases and deposits, respond to maintenance, keep records, and manage turnover—or pay a manager to do some of that work.
