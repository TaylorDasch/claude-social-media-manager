## 🧱 BEFORE YOU WRITE AN OFFER
- FOUNDATIONS: most hospital-district homes are pier-and-beam on Central Texas clay — budget a structural engineer's report + 5–10% of ARV for foundation work.
- ADU: hospital/historic districts occasionally allow an extra dwelling unit. Real upside, but rare — never underwrite assuming you'll get one.
