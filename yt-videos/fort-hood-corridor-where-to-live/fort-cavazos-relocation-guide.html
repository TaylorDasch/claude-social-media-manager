The five-city corridor, at a glance

If you're PCS-ing to Fort Cavazos or relocating anywhere in the Central Texas corridor, you have five real choices — and they are not interchangeable. A median home is a $240,000 buy in Killeen and a $335,000 buy in Belton, the gate commute swings from 16 to 38 minutes, and the school district changes with the city line. Here are the real June 2026 MLS numbers for all five. Skim the table, find your priority in the decision matrix at the end, then read that city's page.
