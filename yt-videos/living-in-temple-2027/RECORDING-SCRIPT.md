## BLOCK 4 — The half that pays more — 9:00–11:00
**[LOC] Desk-insert or tablet on location. Fully scripted — no live wandering.**

> Temple's combined property tax rate is **2.3877 percent**. Seven separate entities.
> I did a whole video on it and I'll link it, so here's just the part that matters today.
>
> Some parcels in **76501** — east Temple — sit inside the **Elm Creek Watershed district**.
> That adds a little over two cents per hundred. Their combined rate is **2.4105 percent**.

**[LOWER-THIRD]** 76502 typical: **2.3877%** · 76501 + Elm Creek: **2.4105%**

> So the lower-value half of town can carry the **higher** rate. Not by a lot — about sixty-eight
> dollars a year on a three-hundred-thousand-dollar valuation — but it runs exactly opposite to
> what everybody assumes, and it does not show up on any listing.
>
> And while we're here: a **Temple mailing address does not mean inside Temple city limits, and
> it does not mean Temple ISD.** Bell CAD has **47,787** City of Temple accounts and only
> **32,059** Temple ISD accounts. Some homes with a Temple address are in Belton ISD, which is
> a different combined rate again.
>
> Look up the actual parcel at bellcad.org before you trust any number. Including mine.

## BLOCK 5 — What it costs to hold — 11:00–12:15
**[LOC] Walk-and-talk, any residential street.**

> Run it on a three-hundred-thousand-dollar house so it's concrete.

**[LOWER-THIRD]**
> No homestead exemption (investor): **$7,163/yr — $597/mo**
> With homestead (owner-occupant): **$5,030/yr — $419/mo**
> The homestead exemption is worth **$2,133 a year**

> If you live in it, you file the homestead exemption and it's worth **twenty-one hundred a year**.
> One form, filed once. I still meet people three years into a house who never filed it.
>
> If you're renting it out, you don't get it. That's the number out-of-state investors get wrong
> most often, and it's the difference between a deal working and not.

## BLOCK 6 — Honest negatives — 12:15–14:30
**This block is the only place the 27-minute version's retention curve went UP. Give it room.
Do not soften it.**

> Here's what I'd tell a friend, and none of it is good for my business.
>
> **The tax rate is genuinely high.** Two point three nine percent. If you're coming from a state
> with a one percent cap, your escrow is going to be a shock, and "no state income tax" doesn't
> change that you write that check whether or not you earned anything this year.
>
> **Temple is the most expensive city in Bell County to hold a house.** Not Belton. Not Killeen.
> Not Harker Heights. Temple.

**[LOWER-THIRD]** Temple 2.3877% · Killeen 2.0284% · Belton 2.0068% · Harker Heights 1.8327%

> Belton is **eleven hundred forty-three dollars a year cheaper** on the identical three-hundred-
> thousand-dollar house. And it isn't schools — Belton ISD charges *more* than Temple ISD. It's
> that Temple pays a junior college district and Belton pays none at all.
>
> **Your bill is not fixed.** Bell County reassesses every year. Your rate can hold perfectly flat
> and your bill still goes up, because it's the valuation that moved. People miss that constantly.
>
> **And this is not a big city.** If you need a major airport, a major venue, or a Saturday with
> twenty options on it, you are going to be driving to Austin an hour each way. Some people do
> that happily for four years and then leave. That's a real pattern and I'd rather you hear it
> from me now than find out in year two.
