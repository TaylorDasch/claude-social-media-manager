## FAIR HOUSING GUARDRAIL — READ BEFORE SCRIPTING OR FILMING

The low-value band (16th, 20th, 22nd, 24th, Central, MLK Jr, Ave H, Barton, 10th) is in
central and east Temple. **A "good neighborhoods / bad neighborhoods" framing here is a
fair-housing problem and it is why the crime-map video was killed in verification.**

**Required framing:**
- Present assessed values as **price bands**, never as a desirability ranking.
- Explain drivers factually: **age of housing stock, lot size, proximity, and the watershed
  district**. Never demographics, never "safety," never "avoid."
- Frame the low band as what it actually is for Taylor's business: the **investor and
  first-time-buyer entry point**. His stated buy box is $140K–$260K and his catalog is full of
  $75K BRRRR, sub-$100K rental and "costs less than a car" deals. That band *is* the deal flow.
- **Never** name or disparage a specific home, street or owner.
- Foundation-risk discussion stays restricted to Western Hills and River Oaks, per vault rules.

This framing is both defensible and more useful than a ranking. Do not deviate from it.
