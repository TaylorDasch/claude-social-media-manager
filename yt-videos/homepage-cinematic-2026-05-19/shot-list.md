# Homepage Cinematic Reel — Shot List
**Total runtime:** 1:05
**Aspect:** 16:9 master (1080p min, 4K preferred for crop reserve), 9:16 vertical crop for repurpose
**Look:** Subtle film grain LUT (Kodak 2383 emulation or similar, light). Grayscale push +8-12%. Contrast +5%. No heavy teal-and-orange. Hans Zimmer wind-down, not stock trailer.

---

## SHOT 1 — Drone, Temple downtown push-in
- **Beat:** 0:00-0:06
- **Type:** Aerial, slow forward push
- **Location:** Downtown Temple, framed eastbound. Acceptable framings: Avenue A toward Santa Fe Plaza, or rail corridor with Scott & White medical district in distance.
- **Time:** Golden hour, ~25 min before sunset. Soft side light. Light haze acceptable, hard sun is not.
- **Movement:** Slow push, ~3 mph equivalent. No yaw, no roll. Altitude ~150 ft.
- **Duration captured:** Shoot 20 sec, use 6 sec.
- **Sound design:** Faint train horn if natural. Otherwise add in post.
- **Compliance:** FAA 107 standard altitude rules, daylight ops, no overhead crowds.
- **Backup if drone weathered out:** Slow tripod-mounted slider shot across a downtown rooftop or balcony at the same hour. Less prestige but cuts.

## SHOT 2 — The Desk (interior, close)
- **Beat:** 0:06-0:12
- **Type:** Static medium-close, slight high angle (~15°) looking down at desk surface
- **Location:** Taylor's home/studio office. Wood desk preferred. Window light, no overheads.
- **Frame elements (left to right):** Coffee cup (handle facing camera), printed MLS hot sheet, notebook with handwritten numbers visible, edge of open laptop showing a chart (Bell County market data, not Zillow). Hand enters frame from right at 0:09, taps printout once with index finger, exits.
- **Lens:** 35mm or 50mm equivalent. Shallow DoF — desk surface in focus, background falls off.
- **Lighting:** Single soft key from window (camera left), unfilled shadow on the right. Practical bookshelf lamp in background, deep amber, defocused.
- **Sound design:** Pencil tap, faint paper rustle, a coffee cup setting down. Adds tactility.
- **Duration captured:** Shoot 15 sec, use 6 sec.

## SHOT 3 — Taylor on Camera, Bookend #1
- **Beat:** 0:12-0:22
- **Type:** Static medium-shot, framed left-of-center with negative space camera-right
- **Location:** Taylor's studio (same room as Shot 2). Background: shelf with books, framed Bell County map, single plant. Defocused at f/2.8.
- **Wardrobe:** Dark crewneck or open-collar shirt — no agent-branded polo, no logos. Earth-tone preferred (charcoal, olive, dark navy).
- **Framing:** Eyes on upper third. Looking slightly off-camera (just past lens, ~5° camera-left) — podcast feel, not direct sell.
- **Lens:** 50mm equivalent, f/2.8.
- **Lighting:** Soft window key camera-left. Hair light or kicker camera-right at 1/4 power. Background just barely lit.
- **Color:** Slight warm push (~+8 on temperature), neutral tint.
- **Audio:** Lav mic + boom backup. Cardioid pattern. Record room tone 30 sec before take.
- **Performance note:** Calm, low register, even pace. Pause between sentences. Do NOT smile. Do NOT do the agent-headshot grin. Three takes minimum. Slate the second take "the keeper" and use it as the reference.

## SHOT 5 — The Five Lanes (signature shots, 0:28-0:48)

### 5a. BSW Medical (0:28-0:32 — 4.0s hold)
- **Type:** Wide, static. Person walking away from camera.
- **Location:** Public parking lot adjacent to a Temple medical building (not BSW property itself — use the adjacent commercial lot to avoid trespass). End of shift, ~5:30 PM, side light.
- **Frame:** A woman or man in standard hospital-issue scrubs walking from the right of frame toward a parked sedan. Lanyard visible (no readable badge). Background: medical-district streetscape.
- **Compliance:** Talent is a hired non-staff actor in plain scrubs. NOT a BSW employee. NOT shot on BSW property. Lanyard is generic — no BSW logo. No patient interaction. Fair Housing compliant — no protected-class signaling.
- **Movement:** Talent walks away from camera, opens car door, scene cuts before they get in.

### 5b. Investor (0:32-0:36 — 4.0s hold)
- **Type:** Wide, slight handheld breath.
- **Location:** Modest single-family rental on a Belton side street. Mid-priced ($175-$225K range visually — not luxury, not distressed).
- **Frame:** A man (not Taylor — generic actor) in dark jeans, work boots, a worn ballcap and a button-down stands at the curb, holds his phone up, photographs the front of the house. Lockbox visible on door. He turns his phone to type a note. Holds 4 sec.
- **Compliance:** Property used with owner permission (Taylor's network — pull a current Taylor listing or a property where the owner consents on a release). No street address visible in shot.
- **Time:** Mid-morning, even daylight, no harsh shadows.

### 5c. Military (0:36-0:40 — 4.0s hold)
- **Type:** Wide, static.
- **Location:** Public residential street in Harker Heights or off-base Killeen. Suburban driveway. NOT on Fort Hood.
- **Frame:** A young family — two adults, two small kids — loading a moving truck (U-Haul, Penske, or unbranded box truck). Texas plates visible. Kids' bikes in foreground. One adult is handing a box to the other. No military uniforms in shot.
- **Compliance:** No on-post filming. No identifiable military insignia. Actors are a casting hire, not an active-duty family. Fair Housing compliant — diverse, but cast naturalistically.
- **Time:** Late morning. Even light.

### 5d. First-Time Buyer (0:40-0:44 — 4.0s hold)
- **Type:** Close-up, low angle, shallow DoF.
- **Location:** Front porch of a modest Temple home — 1950s-70s vintage, brick, mid-priced. Porch step level.
- **Frame:** Camera low, looking up. A house key (single key on a plain ring, no agency-branded fob) drops into an open palm. The palm is open and waiting — not grabbing. Drop happens in slow motion (record at 60fps, conform to 24fps for slow). Background is soft porch detail.
- **Compliance:** Actors' hands only — no faces, no identifying jewelry. Key is generic. Drop is staged, not a real closing.

### 5e. Seller (0:44-0:48 — 4.0s hold)
- **Type:** Pull-focus rack shot, static frame.
- **Location:** A Temple front yard with a "For Sale" sign. Use a GENERIC sign (rent or build a prop sign — do NOT use a branded Taylor sign or a competitor's sign — the story is the seller's experience, not the agent).
- **Frame:** Camera at grass level. Foreground: blades of grass, soft. Pull focus to the sign in the middle distance. Sign reads "For Sale" only — no agent name, no phone. Held 4 sec while the focus pull completes over the first 2 sec, sign locked for the last 2.
- **Time:** Late afternoon. Warm side light through grass.
- **Compliance:** Prop sign on a property where the owner has consented. No identifiable house number.
