## THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS

All thumbnails follow the brand system: deep navy/black background, subtle film grain, Italiana display type, emerald accent. NO finger-pointing, NO open-mouth shocked face, NO arrows or circles. This is brand-level, not algorithmic-bait.

### Concept 1 — "The Receipts" (recommended primary)
**Layout:** Two-column composition. Left column (40%): Taylor in studio, mid-shot from Beat 3, looking just off-camera. Right column (60%): the desk close-up from Beat 2 — open laptop with a clean Bell County chart, MLS printout edge, notebook with handwritten numbers.

**Typography:**
- Top-right, Italiana, large (~80pt visual): "Five Lanes."
- Below, Italiana italic, emerald, smaller (~50pt visual): "Never Blurred."
- Bottom-right corner, JetBrains Mono, small white: `templetxhomes.net`

**Why this works:** Two visual textures (human + data) in one frame = brand signal AND data signal. The "Never Blurred" line is the strongest two words from the script. Reads at thumbnail size (does it tell a story at 120px wide? — yes, the desk and the face do the work).

**Color:** Background pulled to #0a0d14, subtle emerald glow under the right-column desk edge.

### Concept 2 — "The Map" (best for prestige play)
**Layout:** Single image — Taylor in studio, three-quarter framing camera-left, looking at a large framed map of Bell County on the wall behind him. Camera is over his shoulder, soft focus. Map is just legible — viewer can see Temple, Belton, Killeen, Harker Heights, but only with effort.

**Typography:**
- Centered-top, Italiana display large (~90pt): "Six Markets."
- Centered-below, Italiana italic emerald (~55pt): "One Agent."
- Bottom-center, JetBrains Mono small: `Living in Temple — Channel Trailer`

**Why this works:** Most editorial of the three. Strong on prestige, weakest on CTR vs. a face-forward thumbnail. Best choice if Taylor wants to signal "this isn't a real estate YouTuber channel" to first-time visitors.

**Color:** Warm tungsten background, emerald single-pixel accent on the map's Temple marker.

### Concept 3 — "Five Doors" (most YouTube-native, highest CTR potential)
**Layout:** Five small thumbnail-style image tiles in a horizontal strip — each representing one lane:
  1. Stethoscope on a clipboard (BSW)
  2. A calculator on a property listing (Investor)
  3. A moving truck silhouette (Military)
  4. A house key (First-Time Buyer)
  5. A "Sold" sign top corner (Seller)

Below the strip, a single line of Italiana type and Taylor's face cropped to a strip below that.

**Typography:**
- Center, Italiana, large (~95pt): "Pick Your Lane."
- Subtitle, Outfit, small white: "Temple, TX Real Estate — Decided by Data."

**Why this works:** Tells the whole "five lanes" story at a glance — the visual sells before the title even reads. Highest CTR potential of the three because it pre-answers "what is this channel about" in one frame. Slight risk: it could feel too "infographic-y" for a prestige brand. Recommended if Taylor wants to maximize subscribe-rate on the channel trailer specifically.

**Color:** Each lane tile is monochrome-with-emerald-accent so the strip feels unified, not noisy.

## THUMBNAIL PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Resolution:** 1280x720 (16:9), with safe-area for YouTube's interface overlays (avoid putting critical type in the bottom-right 200x100px — that's where the duration timestamp lands)
- **Compression test:** Save at 85% JPEG, view at 320x180 in a browser. If the type isn't readable at that size, scale it up.
- **Mobile test:** View on phone at thumbnail-strip size. Should still read.
- **A/B testing:** YouTube Studio allows three thumbnail variants per video — upload all three concepts and let YouTube rotate them for the first 14 days, then pick the winner.
