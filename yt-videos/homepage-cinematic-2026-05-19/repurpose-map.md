## 2. LINKEDIN INTRO POST

LinkedIn gets the cinematic reel as an introduction post — Taylor's profile lead-in for any new connection. Native upload of the video, NOT a YouTube link (LinkedIn deprioritizes external video links).

### Post text (paste-ready)

```
Five years of real estate in Temple, Texas, distilled into a 60-second reel.

I work five lanes — BSW medical relocation, Central Texas investors, Fort Hood military families, first-time buyers, and sellers. The math for each is different. The playbooks only stay sharp if the lanes never blur.

$30M+ closed. 100+ transactions. Ranked #28 of 2,013 Bell County agents.

If one of those lanes is yours, the pillar pages at templetxhomes.net are built for it specifically — real MLS data, real math, real tradeoffs.

Taylor Dasch · EG Realty · Temple, TX
```

**Tone notes:**
- Opens with a frame ("distilled into a 60-second reel") not a hook — LinkedIn audience is professional, not feed-scrolling
- Numbers are buried in the middle paragraph — credibility, not lead — because the LinkedIn audience already expects them and they read as humble-brag if up top
- No hashtags. LinkedIn hashtag use is a credibility tax in 2026 for senior professional audiences.
- No URL shortener. Full domain visible.
- Sign-off is the same one used on the homepage and on the YouTube end card — entity consistency reinforces SEO/AEO/GEO across Google's properties.

### LinkedIn post settings
- **Post type:** Native video upload (NOT a YouTube link)
- **Audience:** Anyone (public)
- **Comments:** Open
- **Aspect for upload:** Original 16:9 (LinkedIn renders 16:9 fine in-feed; vertical is unnecessary)
- **Cover frame:** Beat 6 — Taylor on camera, Bookend #2 — set as the thumbnail
