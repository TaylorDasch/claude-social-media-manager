# Council Prompt — Homepage Cinematic Reel

## Brief

Build a 60-90 second cinematic homepage reel for templetxhomes.net. This video lives in the hero (or just below) and gives visitors a 60-second "who is Taylor and what is this site" before they pick a lane. Lives on the **Living in Temple** channel (it's a brand-level video, but Living in Temple is the public-facing brand channel — never put brand-trust video on the Investor channel since that audience prefers data-direct content).

**Target use:** Embedded on homepage as a cinematic reel (autoplay-muted, with caption track). Also stands alone on YouTube as a channel trailer.

**Companion page URL:** https://templetxhomes.net/

**Pinned comment link:** Just the homepage URL — no UTM since this is the routing entry point.

---

## Deliverables

### 1. Script (60-90 sec)
Cinematic, sparse, voice-over driven. Lines should feel like a New Yorker piece, not a real estate ad.

Suggested structure:
- 0:00-0:08 — Cinematic Temple establishing shots, voice-over opens: "Temple, Texas. Three highways. Two hospitals. A military base. A data center. A university. Six markets, running at different speeds."
- 0:08-0:25 — Taylor on-camera intro: "I'm Taylor Dasch. I've closed $30 million across 100 transactions. I work five lanes, I never blur them."
- 0:25-0:45 — Lane B-roll cuts: BSW medical · investor portfolio · Fort Hood gate · first-home keys · seller sign. Each cut 3-4 seconds. Voice-over: "Investor math is different from BSW relocation math. Military PCS math is different from first-time buyer math. The strategy bends to match the audience."
- 0:45-0:60 — Closer: "Pick your lane on the site below. The right pillar, the right data, the right short list — drafts only."

### 2. Title
"Temple Real Estate, Decided by Data — Taylor Dasch, EG Realty"

### 3. Description
2-line: "Cinematic homepage trailer for templetxhomes.net. Five lanes. Five playbooks. Pick yours at https://templetxhomes.net/"

### 4. Visual treatment
- Heavy on B-roll, light on talking-head
- Film-grain LUT (subtle, not aggressive)
- Drone shots of Temple at golden hour
- BSW medical campus signage (public-side only)
- Fort Hood Bernie Beck gate (public-side, no on-post filming)
- UMHB campus exterior
- A few model home walk-through cuts
- Taylor in-studio talking head bookends only

### 5. Music
Cinematic, restrained, piano + strings. Not stock-trailer-bombastic. Think Hans Zimmer "Interstellar" wind-down or "1899" piano motifs. Low-key prestige.

### 6. Captions / subtitles
Auto-generated then human-edited. Captions are critical because the video plays muted-autoplay on the homepage.

### 7. Companion Shorts
1 × 30-sec vertical cut: "Five lanes. One agent. Real numbers." — distilled from the lane B-roll section.

---

## Voice rules

- Cinematic, sparse, prestige-feel — but data-grounded.
- No "find your dream home" energy. No real estate ad clichés.
- Numbers are load-bearing — every claim has a source.
- Voice-over should sound like a podcast intro, not a commercial.

## Lane discipline

- Homepage video routes to ALL lanes, but doesn't mix the messages.
- Frames each lane briefly with a single signature shot — doesn't conflate.
- Cross-platform: YouTube channel trailer + homepage embed + IG Reel + Threads/X teaser.

## Compliance

- No exaggerated rank claims — "ranked #28 of 2,013 Bell County agents" is current. Verify before publish.
- No specific lender endorsements.
- No on-post Fort Hood filming.
- No Stark Law BSW outreach claims.
- Fair Housing language only.
