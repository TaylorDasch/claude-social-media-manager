## NOTES

- Description is intentionally short (under 70 words). This is a cinematic piece, not a tutorial — long descriptions undercut the prestige treatment.
- All five lanes are listed so the YouTube algorithm has clear topical anchors for related-video and channel-recommendation surfaces.
- No timestamps. The video is too short and too tonal for chapter markers.
- No CTA-stack of social links at the bottom — that's stock YouTuber energy, wrong for a channel trailer.
- Single URL. Single phone number. Single brand line.
- Pinned comment is JUST the homepage URL. No UTM because the homepage IS the routing entry point — once a visitor lands, they self-segment via the 5-rail audience picker. Adding UTM here would log the visitor as "youtube-sourced" before they pick a lane, which contaminates lane-level attribution downstream.

## HASHTAGS (none — recommended)

Channel trailers don't benefit from hashtags. The job is brand signal + subscribe rate, both of which are hurt by hashtag clutter at the description footer. Leave them off.
