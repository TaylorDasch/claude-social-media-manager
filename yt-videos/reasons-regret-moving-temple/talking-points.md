---

**1. Nightlife / boredom**
- Real options: Bose Barn, Playa Azul. People in bed by 9. Want more = ~68 mi / ~1 hr 5 min to Austin.
- WRONG for: singles 20s–30s who need a scene → Austin or Belton/university.
- RIGHT for: families, remote workers, done-with-bars crowd. Live line: "Temple is built for builders, not partiers."

**2. Property tax shock**
- "No income tax" ≠ cheap. Combined rate generally 2.2%–2.7% before exemptions (depends on exact jurisdictions).
- Hard number: City of Temple raised ITS portion +11.72% in FY2026 → $0.6999 per $100. Say the EXACT figure ("point-six-nine-nine-nine") on camera. Dare them to check it against the city budget.
- Trap: appraisal resets to purchase price; homestead + 10% cap slow it AFTER closing, going forward.
- Extra: SOME newer subdivisions sit in a MUD/PID that adds real annual cost. Ask "is this in a MUD/PID and what does it add?" before offering. Verify per property.
- WRONG for: anyone budgeting off the mortgage payment alone. RIGHT for: anyone who runs full PITI + any district first — total still wins for many vs. higher-cost TX metros.

**3. Job market = BSW or thin**
- BSW ~9,000 Bell County employees (8,884 per Temple EDC); healthcare 40%+ of Temple jobs; most jobs ~10–15 min from campus. Structural for nurses/residents/physicians/techs.
- Honest cost: non-medical/high earners find narrower options; entry-level + support roles regionally below national pay averages. The math of a one-industry mid-size market — NOT a knock on any employer.
- WRONG for: non-medical high earners needing a deep, diverse local market. RIGHT for: medical pros + remote workers (outside salary, cheap housing = best CoL trade) + retirees (income not local).

**4. Social fabric / "where's my crowd?"** (DISTINCT from #3 — it's about people, not paychecks; felt in month 3)
- Reddit, verbatim: "Temple sucks for young adults — a retirement community built around a huge hospital."
- Thin dating pool if young/single; "third places" tied to church, hospital, kids' sports, family life. Town doesn't hand you a friend group like a college city does.
- WRONG for: young singles building a social circle → Belton/UMHB or commute social life to Austin. RIGHT for: rooted families, coupled-up people, retirees who want the calm.

**5. Heat / retail / car-dependence**
- 3–4 weeks of 100°F+; Jul/Aug mid-90s; periodic drought summers → outdoors is morning/evening, Jun–Sep. (No single-season weather claim.)
- Temple Mall dead; retail moved to West Temple strips.
- Car-dependent; microtransit exists but limited; I-35 truck traffic + chronic TxDOT construction.
- WRONG for: walkability/transit identity. RIGHT for: people who already drive everywhere. Military (Fort Cavazos): car-dependent suburb, spouse job search is real, housing allowance stretches further here — eyes open.

**6. School district confusion**
- "Temple" address does NOT guarantee Temple ISD — could be Temple ISD, Belton ISD, OR Academy ISD. Address decides, not you.
- Belton ISD = A-ratings, Lake Belton High among top in the area → families target Belton zones and pay up for them.
- Academy ISD = 4-day week → great for some, a Friday childcare gap for dual-income.
- RIGHT for schools-first families IF you let the district drive the search and buy the right boundary on purpose. Regret = fell for the house first, checked the district second. Verify district BY ADDRESS, every time.
