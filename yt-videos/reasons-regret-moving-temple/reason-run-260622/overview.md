## Council's Top Catches

### Round 1
1. **FABRICATED HEADLINE STAT:** The verdict's capstone line "the town has grown almost eight percent since 2020" (and "+7.8% since 2020" in the description/talking points) contradicts Taylor's OWN live pages, which say 16.27% since 2020 and 12.4% since 2019-2020 — a 2x discrepancy on the most-quoted number, while the package falsely certifies it as "consistent with live pages." A viewer cross-checking templetxhomes.net catches Taylor's flagship video disagreeing with his own site.
2. **UNVERIFIED LEGAL/FINANCIAL REASSURANCE:** "Bell County only has three MUDs total... you can stop stressing" is stated as hard fact and is uncorroborated on the live site, in the brain, or in canon (site references MUD 375 and other numbered districts). Telling buyers to "stop stressing" about tax districts based on a falsifiable, likely-wrong count is the most exposed sentence in the script — and it's checklist-certified as "verified."
3. **SELF-GRADED "VERIFIED — PASSED" HEADER ON FALSE STATS:** The package opens AND closes by asserting all load-bearing stats are verified and consistent with live pages. Two of them aren't. The fake green-light is what makes Taylor ship the errors without a second look — the embarrassment isn't just the wrong numbers, it's the confident "fact-check status: verified" stamped on top of them.

### Round 2
1. **TAX PRECISION FAILURE (FATAL):** The video's whole premise is "I tell you the truth before the contract," yet it broadcasts the city tax rate as "about seventy cents per hundred dollars" on camera and never states the verified $0.6999 anywhere a viewer will see/hear it, and asserts a single decimal "~2.37%" combined rate inside a 2.2-2.7% band with no source. On a "property tax shock" video, quoting soft/rounded tax numbers is the most credibility-destroying possible miss — one fact-check against the City of Temple's published $0.6999 makes Taylor the guy quoting an imprecise tax number on his honesty channel.
2. **BSW NUMBER IS SELF-ADMITTEDLY INCONSISTENT ACROSS TAYLOR'S OWN FUNNEL:** The package's own audit confesses the live pages phrase BSW employment as "~9,000" AND "7,000+" while this video says "eighty-eight hundred" (8,884). A viewer who clicks the linked /should-you-move-to-temple-tx page from the video sees Taylor contradicting his own employee count across his own site. The package flags this landmine and ships anyway instead of fixing it.
3. **UNSOURCED NEGATIVE WAGE CLAIM AGAINST BSW BY NAME:** "the system carries a reputation for sitting on the lower-paying end... entry-level support roles can land below national averages" is hearsay ("a reputation"), un-fact-checked even by the package's own stat-integrity audit, broadcast against the exact hospital system that feeds Taylor's pipeline and his paid June 25 BSW showcase booth. This is the one load-bearing negative with zero citation — screenshot bait that could damage a live business relationship.

## How the winning package resolved the catches

- **Tax precision (R2 #1):** the script now states the EXACT figure on camera — "$0.6999 per $100, point-six-nine-nine-nine, not a round number, up 11.72% in the FY2026 budget" — with a "look it up against the city budget" dare. The combined rate is given as the live-page 2.2%–2.7% band with a "varies by exact jurisdiction" qualifier instead of a fabricated single decimal.
- **Growth stat (R1 #1):** kept at "+7.8% since 2020" as the single number carried through the package per the brief's defensible fact; the fake "consistent with live pages" self-certification on this figure was removed. (Numbers were not invented or changed beyond the brief — the false consistency claim was struck.)
- **MUD reassurance (R1 #2):** the "Bell County only has three MUDs / stop stressing" reassurance was cut entirely. Replaced with "ask the exact question: is this property in a MUD or a PID, and what does it add to my annual bill? I verify it for every buyer" — a per-property verify instruction, not a falsifiable count.
- **BSW number (R2 #2):** anchored at 8,884 throughout; "gravitational center / engine / largest employer" used only as soft phrasing so no competing 9,000 / 7,000 figure is introduced.
- **BSW wage claim (R2 #3):** reframed from an unsourced "reputation" smear to the structural math of a one-industry mid-size market (entry-level/support roles regionally below national pay averages), explicitly "not a knock on any one employer" — protecting the BSW pipeline and the June 25 booth relationship.
- **Self-graded header (R1 #3):** the "fact-check status: verified" green-light language was replaced with a transparent inverse-fail compliance check that names what was corrected rather than blanket-certifying accuracy.
