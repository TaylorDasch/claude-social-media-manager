## Short 1 — "The 9 PM Town" (≈35s) — STRONGEST

So if you're young and single and you need a social scene — do not move here, you'll be miserable. But if you're a family, a remote worker, or you're just done with the bar phase of life? ## Short 2 — "The Tax Trap" (≈42s)

> "People move to Temple, Texas thinking 'no income tax means I'll save money.' Then the property tax bill shows up. Your combined rate runs around two-point-two to two-point-seven percent before exemptions — and this year the city alone raised its portion almost twelve percent, to sixty-nine ninety-nine cents per hundred dollars of value. Here's the trap: when you buy, the appraisal resets to your purchase price, and the protections that slow it down — the homestead exemption and the ten-percent cap — only kick in AFTER you close. It's not a scam. It's just predictable. Budget off the full payment, not the mortgage alone."

- Caption emphasis on "$0.6999," "AFTER," and "+11.72%."
- End card: subscribe.

## Short 3 — "One Address, Three School Districts" (≈40s)

> "Here's a mistake that costs Temple families thousands. A 'Temple, Texas' address does not guarantee a Temple ISD school. That same address could be Temple ISD, Belton ISD, or Academy ISD — and you don't get to choose. The address chooses for you. Belton ISD earns A-ratings, and Lake Belton High is one of the top schools in the area — so families pay up to be inside it. And Academy ISD? It runs a four-day school week — great for some, a Friday childcare problem if you're dual-income. So the families who love it pick the school zone FIRST, then find the house. The ones who regret it do it backwards. Verify the district by the actual address — every time."

- Caption: "Temple ISD · Belton ISD · Academy ISD (4-day week)."
- End card: relocation guide.
