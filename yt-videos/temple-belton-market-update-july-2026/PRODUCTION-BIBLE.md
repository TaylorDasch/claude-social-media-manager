# Production Bible — The 60-Day Listing Test

## Opening promise

In the first 28 seconds, the viewer sees the deepest buyer-useful pattern in the July data: 13.7% of exact Active listings at 0–30 DOM had cut price, 66.7% at 61–90 DOM had reduced, and 81.2% after day 90 had reduced. The video then prevents the obvious mistake—assuming every older or reduced listing deserves a lowball—by showing that the latest qualifying Closed records finished at a median 99.76% of final list.

**Status:** `READY_TO_FILM`; FILMING GATE — CLEARED; Taylor approval and publication approval still required  
**Target:** 9:00–10:00  
**Format:** direct-to-camera local market briefing with original data graphics  
**Data cutoff:** 2026-07-20; current Closed window through 2026-07-20

## 1. Viewer and business case

- **One viewer:** a buyer relocating to Temple or Belton and likely purchasing within six months.
- **One decision:** whether to move quickly or press harder on a specific listing, then whether buying now fits the move.
- **One proof:** the `14% → 45% → 67% → 81%` listing-age/price-cut staircase, checked against actual closing behavior.
- **One conversion:** download the Temple vs. Belton Family Decision Guide, then contact Taylor with a move date and short list.
- **Revenue path:** browse/search click → proprietary local insight → guide download → qualified relocation conversation → representation → closing → review/referral.
- **Series role:** sequel to the May 22 update. This video answers `when and how`; the separate Temple-versus-Belton video answers `where`.

### Non-goals

- No national housing commentary or mortgage-rate forecast.
- No city winner, school ranking, safety claim, or demographic-fit claim.
- No blanket buyer’s market, seller’s market, crash, bottom, or appreciation prediction.
- No current builder offer or promotion.
- No investor or seller detour.
- No MLS address, private remark, showing instruction, tenant detail, or unlicensed listing image.

## 3. Final hook

### Hook delivery

- Start on a physical four-step card: `14 → 45 → 67 → 81`.
- Reveal `PRICE-CUT SHARE` only after the first spoken `cut their price`.
- Put scope/method in the footer: `Based on information from Central Texas MLS as of July 20, 2026. Temple + Belton Residential listings with Status = Active; source export covers multiple cities and was post-filtered to Temple/Belton; DOM groups are cross-sectional.`
- Hit `Taylor Dasch with EG Realty` as sentence three.
- Finish by about 0:28 at a natural pace; answer-first summary begins immediately.
- No greeting, logo sting, biography, or methodology speech before the promise.

## 4. Thesis and honest guardrail

The July snapshot shows a strong cross-sectional association between listing age and prior price correction. It does **not** prove that hitting day 60 causes a cut, and it omits active listings that already sold or withdrew.

The decision rule is:

> Listing age tells you where to investigate. Price history and true substitutes tell you whether to press.

The guardrail is the closing data:

- latest 200 qualifying Closed records: 50 median DOM;
- median close/final list: 99.76%;
- median close/original list: 97.01%.

Much of a concession may be absorbed in a public asking-price reset before contract.

- Meaningful crop, prop, label, chart state, or angle change every 5–8 seconds.

- No music under the staircase, final-list explanation, or methodology caveat.
- One conclusion per chart; no raw spreadsheet wall.
- Put the Central Texas MLS source notice on two high-contrast lines; keep it readable for at least four seconds and repeat it on every distinct active or closed proof graphic.

## 8. New-construction treatment

Builder-identified homes account for 222 of 870 exact Active rows, or 25.5%. Median DOM was 110 for builder inventory and 64 for non-builder inventory.

Do not translate older builder stock into a promised concession. Compare total ownership cost, completion/closing timing, taxes and any HOA/PID/MUD exposure, included features, lot, inspection rights, warranty process, and future resale competition. Recheck any time-sensitive builder term on the day it matters.
