# Packaging Lab

## Decision

The data supports two strong stories:

1. a tactical buyer consequence in the exact Active snapshot: `14% → 67% → 81%` by listing-age group;
2. an easier-to-understand market contradiction: the latest closed sample’s median DOM was `50`, 33 days lower than the May sample’s `83`, while 55.9% of the current Active snapshot had reduced.

The first wins because it is more actionable, more original, and less repetitive of the May price-cut video. The second remains the best A/B challenger.

### Closed-data alternate

- Title: `Temple & Belton Buyers: The Price-Cut Trap`
- Thumbnail: `97% vs 99.8%` with `ORIGINAL ASK` / `FINAL ASK`
- Hook territory: correction can occur before contract; a price-cut badge does not promise a second discount.

This alternate uses the 200 valid `Status = Closed` records from June 21–July 20 only. It is available as a different click hypothesis, but the Active filming gate is now cleared.

## Default native Studio A/B test

### A — tactical winner

- Title: `Temple & Belton Housing Market: The 60-Day Listing Test`
- Thumbnail: `14% vs 81%` + `0–30 DAYS` / `91+ DAYS` + `PRICE CUTS`

### B — easier contradiction

- Title: `Temple & Belton: 83 vs 50 Median DOM—Here’s the Catch`
- Thumbnail: `83 vs 50 DOM`

### C — relocation consequence

- Title: `The Temple–Belton Market Mistake Relocating Buyers Keep Making`
- Thumbnail: `DON’T TRUST THE AVERAGE`

These are different hypotheses. A tests proprietary tactical value; B tests immediately legible numerical conflict; C tests a consequence/identity frame.

## May performance interpretation

- 1,714 impressions.
- 4.32% CTR versus 5.27% across the local channel export.
- About 5:01 average view duration and 38.8% average viewed.
- Zero subscribers in the export window.

The opening premise was viable, but the May thumbnail was crowded and the title did not make the buyer consequence concrete. The new system simplifies the thumbnail and makes the offer decision the title.

## Cannibalization guardrail

Do not title the market update `Temple vs Belton`. The separate evergreen comparison publishes around July 21 and answers where to live. This update joins both cities to answer when/how to offer. Delay this upload at least 48 hours after that launch so its initial audience test is not split.
