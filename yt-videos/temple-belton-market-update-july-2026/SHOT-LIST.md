# Shot List

## Production principle

One authoritative desk setup, seven proof graphics plus one decision-tree visual, and two short local exterior proof beats. The data is the spectacle. Avoid generic lifestyle stock and avoid any MLS visual that exposes an address or private field.

## Opening coverage

Record these as separate clips:

1. `14%` held steady for one second.
2. Clean staircase reveal to `67%` and `81%`.
3. Tight face reaction with no speech for thumbnail/editorial flexibility.
4. `83 vs 50` card toward camera for the secondary re-hook.
5. Full exact hook.
6. Conversational safety hook from `HOOK-LAB.md`.

## Edit cadence

- 0:00–0:30: 6 visual states.
- 0:30 onward: meaningful reset every 5–8 seconds.
- Do not use a transition effect when a clean cut, crop, or chart build is clearer.
- Add a new proof graphic at least every 20 seconds during the data sections.
- Keep charts full-screen long enough to read; do not cover the key value with captions.
- Set the Central Texas MLS source/period notice on two high-contrast footer lines and hold each proof graphic for at least four seconds.
- Captions may emphasize no more than five words at once.

## Footage exclusions

- No current MLS address, parcel number, agent remark, lockbox/showing data, tenant name, plate, signature, email, or phone number.
- No active listing photo without documented public-use rights.
- No identifiable child or resident.
- No builder logo used as visual shorthand for the whole category.
- No drone shot that creates an avoidable permissions or scheduling dependency.

## Pickup priority

If anything feels unclear after the first edit, record pickups in this order:

1. final-list versus original-list explanation;
2. `14% versus 81%` DOM-bucket labels and sample sizes;
3. builder/resale limitation;
4. three-number test recap;
5. CTA handoff.
