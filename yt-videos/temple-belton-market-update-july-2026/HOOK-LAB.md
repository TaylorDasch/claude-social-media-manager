## Winner — the 60/90-day line

> Only about one in seven active Temple and Belton listings in the 0-to-30-day group showed a prior price cut in my July snapshot. In the 61-to-90-day group, it was two in three; after day 90, four in five. I’m Taylor Dasch with EG Realty. If you’re relocating here, listing age tells me when to investigate harder—not when to automatically offer less. I’ll show you the three numbers I check before an offer.

### Why it wins

- It comes from the deepest Market Monitor pattern, not the obvious citywide average.
- It is new; May already centered the broad 54% price-cut fact.
- It gives the buyer a temporal decision: fresh listing, 60-day listing, or 90-day listing.
- It targets the relocator and promises a reusable offer test.
- `Taylor Dasch with EG Realty` appears in sentence three.
- It avoids a crash claim, city contest, and universal lowball advice.

### Claim precision

- `Active` is explicit in the July 20 export; the source is all Residential and is post-filtered from 19 cities to exact Temple/Belton values.
- `About one in seven` = 28/204 = 13.7% at 0–30 DOM.
- `Two in three` = 76/114 = 66.7% at 61–90 DOM.
- `Four in five` = 298/367 = 81.2% at 91+ DOM.
- This is cross-sectional. Do not say day 60 **caused** a cut or that every listing cuts by day 90.
