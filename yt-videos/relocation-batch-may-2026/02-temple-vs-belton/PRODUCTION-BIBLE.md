#### ACT 1 — Tradeoff #1: SCHOOLS (1:15–4:00)

Talking points:
- Belton ISD = TEA **B (80–81)**. 13,800 students, 21 campuses. **Top-rated district in the Killeen-Temple metro.**
- Temple ISD = TEA **C (77, +8 points YoY)**. 15 campuses. Trending up.
- Belton ISD's rating is the **single biggest driver of Belton's $40K–$60K home price premium** over Temple.
- BUT — and this is the hidden gem most agents miss — **Temple High School has held International Baccalaureate (IB) authorization since 1992**. Only **46 IB-authorized high schools in all of Texas**.
- For college-prep families willing to navigate a Title I environment (70% economically disadvantaged at THS), the IB program is a **real edge that A-rated districts can't match**.
- Verdict: if schools are non-negotiable and your child is in the K-8 pipeline → Belton ISD. If you have a college-prep teen and budget matters → Temple HS IB program changes the math.

**Visual:** Side-by-side school district comparison graphic (TEA letter grade, student count, campus count, IB-status column). Drive footage Belton ISD + Temple ISD signage.

#### ACT 2 — Tradeoff #2: MONTHLY COST (4:00–6:30)

Talking points:
- Median home prices (verify Tuesday morning before filming — pull current MLS):
  - Belton: roughly **$300K–$310K** (most recent quarter Belton median ~$310K, was $330K 2-year avg, gap narrowing)
  - Temple: roughly **$260K–$290K** (current MLS, was $289,900 March 2026 page snapshot)
- The premium is **narrowing fast**. Late 2025 was a $40-65K gap. Recent quarter trending tighter.
- Property tax composite stack matters: city + Bell County + school district + special utility districts (MUD)
- **Belton ISD school taxes are HIGHER** than Temple ISD's. Adds to monthly nut.
- Both cities can have MUDs depending on which subdivision. **Three Creeks (Belton)** is a MUD subdivision — adds ~$200-400/mo over comparable non-MUD Temple home.
- Honest verdict: if you're rate/budget constrained AND not chained to Belton ISD → Temple is the better dollar.

**Visual:** Screen-share comparison spreadsheet showing same-spec home in Temple ($285K) vs Belton ($315K) with full PITI + MUD breakdown. Drive footage of comparable subdivisions in both cities.

#### ACT 3 — Tradeoff #3: COMMUTE TO BSW (6:30–8:30)

Talking points:
- Downtown Temple → BSW Medical Center main hospital: **under 10 minutes** in ordinary traffic.
- Belton → BSW: **~15 minutes** in ordinary traffic. Up to 20-25 minutes during shift change.
- Sounds small. Compounds over a year: 5 extra minutes each way × 200 work days × 2 (in/out) = **66 hours/year of additional commute** for the Belton commute.
- For BSW residents (long shifts, sleep-deprived, on-call): the 5-minute differential matters disproportionately.
- For Fort Hood commute: **neighborhood matters more than city**. Belton's access to I-35 is similar to Temple proper's. The decision-maker is which Bell County neighborhood is closest to your specific gate at Fort Hood, not Temple-vs-Belton.

**Visual:** Real-time drive footage — start at McLane Stadium / Lake Pointe area → drive to BSW (Temple commute). Then start at downtown Belton → drive to BSW. Show the clock both times.

#### ACT 4 — Tradeoff #4: LIFESTYLE (8:30–10:30)

- **Belton wins on commercial growth around you.** Lots of new retail, restaurants, density of newer-build subdivisions.
- **Belton wins on crime stats** — Belton's reported property crime rates are lower than Temple's city-wide average. (Worth nuancing: within Temple, West Temple subdivisions like Canyon Creek and Legacy Ranch are at or below Belton's rates — neighborhood matters more than city.)
- **Temple wins on employer access** — BSW, Wilsonart, McLane HQ, Niagara Bottling, SeAH Superalloy all here.
- **Temple wins on housing inventory diversity** — more starter-home product, more first-time buyer options, more pre-1990 stock for renovation plays.
- **Temple wins on restaurant + entertainment density** in the urban core (Avenue H, downtown revitalization, breweries).
