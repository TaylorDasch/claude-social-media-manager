## REJECTED CANDIDATES + WHY (so we don't re-suggest in future videos)

### Titles cut
- **T5** "Meta Is Building in Temple TX. Is Your Home About to Spike?" — bait/switch. Title implies bullish answer; video pivots negative. Destroys trust signal.
- **T7** "I'm a Temple Realtor and Here's What the Data Center Actually Means" — generic agent positioning. Any agent could write this title; no Taylor differentiation. Failure mode: undifferentiated in search and suggested.

### Thumbnails cut
- "Smiling Taylor in front of construction site with thumbs up" — wrong emotion for the hook's editorial position. Mismatch = lower CTR and worse retention.
- "Drone shot of the Meta build site with caption 'IT'S COMING'" — replicates every other generic data-center YT thumbnail in the market. No differentiation. Failure mode: no pattern interrupt.

### Hooks cut
- "Hey guys, welcome back to the channel, today we're talking about the data center in Temple TX..." — Universal Rule #1 violation (no greeting open) AND no curiosity gap AND no specific number in first 15s.
- "BIG news in Temple TX!" opener — generic excitement signal, no specificity, doesn't differentiate.
