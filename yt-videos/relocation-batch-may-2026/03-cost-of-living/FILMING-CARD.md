# 📋 ACT 3 — MUD vs PID: The $400 TRAP (5:00–7:00)

🔥 **THE ONE-LINER:**
> **A MUD is a tax you'll pay forever. A PID is a bill you can pay off and eliminate.**

- **MUD** = Municipal Utility District. Ad valorem (% of property value). On TOP of regular property tax. Common in new builds. Federally deductible (under $10K SALT cap).
- **PID** = Public Improvement District. Flat or fixed-period assessment. Can be paid off. Sometimes lump-sum at closing. Mostly NOT federally deductible.

⚠️ **VERIFY DAY-OF**: Three Creeks (Belton) vs specific non-MUD West Temple subdivision example. Get one specific street if possible.

- Example: Three Creeks adds **~$200-$400/mo** over same-priced non-MUD home
- Where to verify YOUR home: **BellCAD.org → search address → tax jurisdiction layer**
- **Don't trust listing or agent — pull it yourself.**

### Then talking points:
- Filing window: **April 30 of the year AFTER you close** on a primary residence
- Required: primary residence (NOT investment, NOT vacation)
- **Stack with**: Over-65 / Disabled Veteran / Disabled Person exemptions
- **100% VA disability → entire property tax bill = $0** on primary residence. If you have that rating, this is free money you're leaving on the table.
- PCS out + convert to rental? You LOSE homestead. May retain Disabled Veteran under specific conditions. Consult BellCAD directly.
