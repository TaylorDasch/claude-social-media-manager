#### ACT 3 — MUD vs PID: The $400/Month Trap (5:00–7:00)

Talking points:
- This is the biggest hidden cost newcomers miss.
- **A MUD is a tax you'll pay forever. A PID is a bill you can pay off and eliminate.**
- MUD = Municipal Utility District. Ad valorem tax on top of regular property tax. Funds infrastructure in the subdivision. Common in new-build areas.
- PID = Public Improvement District. Flat or fixed-period assessment for a specific infrastructure project. Can be paid off; sometimes a one-time lump sum at closing.
- Example: Three Creeks (Belton) is MUD subdivision. Adds ~$200-$400/mo on top of the standard property tax bill for a same-priced home in a non-MUD subdivision.
- MUD tax IS federally deductible (under the $10K SALT cap).
- PID assessment is mostly NOT federally deductible.
- Where to verify YOUR specific home: BellCAD.org → search the address → tax jurisdiction layer. **Don't trust the listing or the agent — pull it yourself.**

**Visual:** Screen-share BellCAD comparing two homes (one with MUD, one without). Show actual line items on the property records.

#### ACT 4 — Insurance + Utilities + HOA: The Other Monthly Items (7:00–8:30)

Talking points:
- **Homeowners insurance**: Texas-rated. Hail risk + wind risk. Average for $300K home: $1,800-$2,800/year depending on roof age, location, deductible.
- **Utilities**:
  - Electricity: Temple has BOTH **PEC (Pedernales Electric Cooperative)** zones AND **Oncor** zones depending on your specific address. Different rate structures. Pull current rates at signup.
  - Gas: **Atmos Energy** in most of Temple; some pockets of city natural gas. ~$60-$120/mo winter peak, $20-$30/mo summer.
  - Water/sewer/trash: City of Temple flat-fee tier + usage. ~$70-$110/mo typical household.
- **HOA**: varies widely by subdivision. Older Temple subdivisions: $0-$100/yr. Newer Belton ISD subdivisions: $300-$800/yr. Some master-planned communities (e.g., Wyndham Hill): higher.
- Average monthly utility nut for a Temple household: **$300-$400/mo total** depending on season and family size.

#### ACT 6 — Curiosity Payoff: The $2,133 Homestead Exemption (9:30–10:30)

⭐ **MEMORIZE THE OPENING:**

> Here's the $2,133-a-year line item: the **Texas Homestead Exemption**. You file it **once** with Bell CAD on your primary residence, and it removes **$40,000** from your taxable value plus caps your annual increase at **10%**. Bell County homeowners save an **average of $2,133 a year** just from filing this one form. The form is **free**, takes 15 minutes, and the BellCAD office has a portal — **bellcad.org**. Almost half of newcomers I work with didn't file it in their first year and lost the savings. Don't be one of them.

Then talking points:
- Filing window: File by April 30 of the year AFTER you close on a primary residence
- Required: must be your primary residence (not investment, not vacation)
- Stack with: Over-65 exemption, Disabled Veteran (100% VA = eliminates entire bill), Disabled Person
- 100% VA → entire property tax bill on primary residence = $0. If you have that rating, this is free money you're leaving on the table by not filing.
- If you're PCS-ing out and converting to rental: you LOSE homestead but may retain Disabled Veteran under specific conditions. Consult BellCAD directly.
