## Top 3 Catches the Council Locked In (vs. baseline one-pass draft)

1. **Anti-duplication frame collapse caught (FATAL R1):** Author-A's first draft executed "4 sequential single-subdivision tours dressed up as comparison" — the exact failure mode that made Canyon Ridge tour underperform at 1.5x. The R1 critic flagged this immediately as the FATAL weakness. Synthesizer restructured into 5 cross-cutting comparison passes (Square Footage at the Door / Lot & Street Reality / BSW Commute Clock / Real PITI + MUD / 5-Year Resale Risk) with enforced parallel camera angles across all 4 homes intercut. This single restructure was the council's biggest save.

2. **Hook upgraded from thesis to curiosity gap (FATAL R1):** Original hook was a thesis statement (informative but flat). The R1 critic predicted 35-40% drop-off in first 8 seconds. Synthesizer replaced with a 24-word contrast hook that creates a binary mystery ("a house your kid can grow up in, or a house your neighbor's kitchen window looks straight into") — viewer must keep watching to find out which house is which.

3. **Honest Moment escalated from softball to Taylor-defensible (MAJOR R1):** Author-A's Honest Moment was Texas insurance reality — true but a market-wide condition, not Taylor's negative. R1 critic flagged as structural softball. Synthesizer swapped to master-plan appreciation compression on Healy/Groves — a Taylor-observable career insight that costs him builder relationships and reads as a real local-operator confession. R2 audit found the new pick still needed MLS-auditable subdivision name verification, but the structural quality of the negative held.
