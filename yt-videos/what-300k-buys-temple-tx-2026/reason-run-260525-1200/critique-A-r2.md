WEAKNESS-1 [FATAL]: **The named precedent subdivisions in the Honest Moment are reputation-bombs that the package treats as throwaway specificity.** Line 214/579: *"twice in Killeen — Yowell Ranch in 2019 and Mountain Lion Estates in 2021 — and once in Salado at Mill Creek Springs in 2020."* This R2 depth-add explicitly invites audit ("All three are auditable on MLS comp history if you want to check me"), but the package never shows that Taylor actually pulled the comp history on these three subdivisions. **Yowell Ranch is a known Killeen subdivision, but "Mountain Lion Estates" in Killeen and "Mill Creek Springs" in Salado don't have the public footprint that "Yowell Ranch" does** — and if even one of those three subdivision names is wrong, misspelled, or attributed to the wrong city/year, the entire Honest Moment collapses on the first commenter who pulls the Bell County or Williamson County GIS records. The v1 said "twice in Killeen, once in Salado" — vague but unfalsifiable. R2 traded unfalsifiable testimony for falsifiable testimony **without auditing the names first**. That's a strictly worse risk position than v1 unless every name was verified, and the package contains no verification trace. This is the largest single point of failure in the package.

WEAKNESS-2 [FATAL]: **The "Tuesday 7:15 AM" commute methodology creates a falsifiable claim with no defensibility window.** Line 122/124: *"7:15 a.m. on a Tuesday morning — the standardized BSW shift-change baseline."* A BSW physician watching this video, who actually commutes from Three Creeks at 7:15 Tuesday, will arrive in 14 minutes one week and 22 the next — Tuesday commute time is not a fixed number, it's a distribution. Naming **Tuesday specifically** locks the claim to a single weekday and a single time window. Worse, the rationale given ("Monday is the worst commute day and Friday is the easiest — Tuesday is the honest middle") is asserted without data; it's not actually true that Tuesday is the median in every commute corridor (Wednesday is the more commonly cited median in traffic-engineering literature for hospital shift change). The script also conflates BSW shift change (7:00 AM hard) with arrival window (which spreads 6:30–7:15 for ER/ICU staff who need handoff buffer), so "7:15 arrival" actually misses peak by 15 minutes. A single physician commenter who drives this on a Tuesday and gets 22 minutes to Crimson Sky will tank the comment section. **The original "drive test" framing was strong; specifying Tuesday 7:15 AM as standardized methodology converted soft evidence into a brittle, falsifiable, viewer-replicable claim.** Strictly higher risk than v1.
