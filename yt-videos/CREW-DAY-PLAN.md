## The mistake to avoid

Single-neighborhood tours on this channel return **112 to 487 lifetime views**. Nine of the
twenty-two prepped folders in `yt-videos/` are exactly that format. Committing a professional
videographer to one of them is the most expensive low-yield option available.

Structure the day as a **flagship shoot with a b-roll bank riding along.**

Five long-form assets touched in one day.

## Three rules

1. **Skip drone** unless the operator already holds Part 107. Land is ~11% of total assessed
   value in Bell County, so aerials cannot show the price spread. The repo's own ultraplan
   defers drone until the channel is consistently over 10K views.
2. **Duck the music under dialogue.** There is a real viewer complaint on file.
3. **Publish the flagship in January, not next week.** The crew day and the publish date are
   separate decisions. Publishing into the 2026 version's peak is the one thing that would
   waste the shoot — it would split the only search query this channel owns.
