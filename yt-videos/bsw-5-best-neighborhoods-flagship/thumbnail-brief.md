# Thumbnail Brief — BSW 5 Best Neighborhoods Flagship (Pikzels-ready)

**Principle (from channel data):** a bare map reads "informational/boring" and map/ISD content is low-CTR. Use the map as BACKDROP, not subject. Number + face + relevance cue must read in 0.3 seconds.

## PRIMARY CONCEPT — "I drove every one" (test first)
- **Left 60%:** the branded commute map — 3 hospital pins (label "BSW"), 5 neighborhood dots, drive-lines — dimmed/darkened as a backdrop. Two drive times blown up HUGE as bold callouts: **"9 MIN"** and **"7 MIN"** (high-contrast, white/emerald on dark).
- **Right 40%:** Taylor, half-body, hand on the car door or pointing at the map, face clearly visible, confident (not smiling-stock). Faces lift CTR.
- **Top bar, 3–4 words max, high contrast:** **"I DROVE EVERY ONE"** (or "REAL DRIVE TIMES").
- **Medical cue:** small hospital badge / stethoscope / scrubs element so the audience self-IDs instantly.
- Brand palette: midnight `#1e293b`, emerald `#059669`, snow `#f8fafc`.

## VARIANT B — merge the two promises (price + time)
- Same layout; the giant callout becomes **"$280K · 9 MIN"** — fuses affordability + commute in one glance. Top bar: "BSW → HOME."

## VARIANT C — the stacked-budget split (lower priority)
- Split: **"RESIDENT $280K · 9 MIN"** | **"ATTENDING $430K · 15 MIN"** over two home photos, Taylor corner, sticker "+REAL COMMUTES."

## Rules
- ≤4 words of text in the main bar; the NUMBER is the hero, not a sentence.
- No banned words. No "dream/luxury/stunning."
- Test Primary vs Variant B head-to-head; keep the winner's number-style for the Shorts covers too (consistency = channel recognition).
- The same map art = the in-video hero graphic + the booth one-pager. Build it once, reuse three times.
