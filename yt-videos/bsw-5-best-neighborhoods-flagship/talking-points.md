## #1 Western Hills — established, closest (~1:40)
- The two closest are tied — established vs brand-new, same question.
- Established = Western Hills, 76504, against the district. **10 BSW / 7 McLane (closest) / 13 VA.** Median **$249,900**, range $150–460K.
- Honest: older (60s–80s), bigger lots, character, walkable; some need work → price the reno in; that's your equity. Verify the exact street for siren/traffic noise.
- For: resident who wants the shortest drive + doesn't mind a project.

## #2 Wyndham Hill — brand-new, value, Academy ISD (~1:35)
- Same close-in drive, but new. South Temple. **9 BSW (fastest) / 9 McLane / 11 VA.** Median **$280K**, tight $220–345K.
- Newer (last ~10–12 yrs, mostly Omega), open plans, fewer project items.
- **ACADEMY ISD, not Temple** — say it right (corrected on-cam before). Confirm school by address.
- Honest flip: moves fast (DOM ~48) = less negotiating room here; you're paying for a newer home that holds value + sub-10 commute.
- For: nurse wanting new; two-income couple; resident who'd rather buy new than renovate.

## #3 Hartrick Ranch — range + leverage (~2 min)
- Budget opens up; watch closest if buying in 90 days. West Temple. **13 BSW / 12 McLane / 14 VA.** Temple ISD (confirm).
- The range: new builder 3-bed ~$265K up to $700K+; median **$306K.** Grows with you — starter to move-up, same street.
- LEVERAGE: median DOM **~123 days** = 4 months = time = leverage FOR YOU. Room to ask for price cuts, closing-cost help, rate buy-down. "That's not a pitch — that's what a 123-day market is."

## #4 The Grove at Lakewood Ranch — attending tier (~1:30)
- Attending tier, west Temple. **15 BSW / 14 McLane / 19 VA.** Temple ISD. Newest, most-amenitied.
- Median **$430K**, high-$280s to **~$850K.** First tier where attending-budget homes (4–5 bed, study, 3-car) show up.
- Leverage here too: DOM **~138** even up high (fewer buyers) → new construction ≠ no negotiating room. (Tease the loan.)

## #5 Three Creeks, Belton — the trade (~1:45)
- Breaks the pattern: it's in BELTON. **20 BSW / 18 McLane / 23 VA.** ~10 extra min. What you get:
- (1) **Belton ISD** — different district, the reason for many families (confirm by address). (2) Big amenity community — lagoon/lazy river/parks — makes a move feel like a win for kids. (3) Surprise: NOT pricey — median **~$320K**, new D.R. Horton/Stylecraft $243–520K. Furthest neighborhood, lower median than The Grove.
- For: family that picks Belton schools + lagoon over 10 min; nurse with 2 kids wanting new + community.
- One-line nod: want land/small-town? Salado, ~same drive, ~$530K median, acreage. Different video.

## Affordability Bridge — the unlock (~2:30)
- "Turns this list from interesting into YOURS." Two numbers nobody puts in a relocation video.
- (1) "I'm not going to insult you by guessing your salary. You know your number." Math you can do tonight: **gross monthly × 28% = safe payment.** Anchor to real money: $250K (Western Hills/Wyndham) ≈ ~$2,400–2,600/mo all-in w/ TX taxes; The Grove ~$430K ≈ ~$4,000/mo. Lender places you in 10 min. The 5 are a budget ladder — you know your rung. *(Ballpark — say "confirm with a lender.")*
- (2) **Physician loan** (MD/DO/dentist): many programs = little/no down, no PMI, treat student debt gently (some ignore deferred, some count only IBR) — **varies by lender, use a physician-loan specialist.** Most let residents qualify on a signed offer letter, often before day one. The difference between "rent 3 yrs" and "buy the month I move"; gives attendings more room than sticker. "Ladder's a floor, not a cage."
- 2 honest cautions: (a) 3-yr resident matching out → renting can win, separate video (P2). (b) Nurses: most physician loans are doctor/dentist only — you've got down-payment help instead, your own video (P4). This video = the map; those 2 numbers = the money.
