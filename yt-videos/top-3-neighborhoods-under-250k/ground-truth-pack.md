## 0. Why these 3 (not the obvious volume picks)

The CSV has 358 distinct subdivisions with at least one record under $250K. The most common picks (Pecan Creek 33, Oak Ridge 24) are essentially the **same builder play** — NextHome Tropicana / Stylecraft new construction in the same growth corridor. Picking both wastes a segment.

Better framing: each neighborhood gives the viewer a **distinct buyer journey**, so every viewer sees themselves in at least one of the three.

| Pick | Buyer archetype | Differentiator |
|---|---|---|
| **Pecan Creek** | First-time buyer / move-up from rental | Brand-new build value. 17 active listings starting at $189K. Today's actionable inventory. |
| **Westfield Dev Ph II** | Buyer who wants "established, not brand new" | Mid-2010s homes. Sold-through proof of demand (only 2 active = scarcity story). |
| **Morgans Point Resort Sec 1** | Lifestyle / lake / relocator | Belton Lake waterfront community. Fastest velocity (20-day median DOM). 100% Belton, not Temple. |

Rejected alternatives and why:
- **Oak Ridge** — too similar to Pecan Creek (same builder, same growth pocket); redundant content.
- **Lake Pointe Ph II-A1** — strong but redundant with Morgans Point Resort (both lake-adjacent).
- **Western Hills 8th Ext** — solid 1990s pick, but lower data velocity (3 active, slower turn).
- **Tal-Coe Place / Temple Original / Temple Heights** — under $150K but heavy distressed/condition risk; off-bar for buyer-relocator audience.
