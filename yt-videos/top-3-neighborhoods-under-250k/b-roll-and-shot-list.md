## NEIGHBORHOOD 1 — PECAN CREEK B-roll

**Streets to film:** Clarkson Drive (primary), Newey Circle, Torbensen Court.
**Total runtime to cover:** ~3:00
**Time budget on-site:** 60 min driving + filming

### Required shots
1. **Drone establishing shot** (8 sec) — slow rise revealing the new-build subdivision from ~150 ft. Capture the "fresh asphalt + young trees + identical roofs" texture that visually says "new construction."
2. **Drive-in shot** (5 sec) — car POV slow drive down Clarkson Drive at golden hour or overcast midday.
3. **Subdivision entrance signage** (3 sec) — close on the Pecan Creek monument or street sign. Hold for readability.
4. **Sample home exterior** (4 sec) — exterior of a model home OR an under-construction home (active listing if accessible). NO house number readable.
5. **Builder yard signs** (2 sec) — close-up of one builder's "Now Selling" sign (Stylecraft or whoever's running this phase).
6. **Streetscape — wide** (5 sec) — wide shot showing 3–4 homes side by side, demonstrating the "tight lots, new trees" pattern.
7. **Cul-de-sac shot** (4 sec) — Torbensen Court is a court, shoot from the bulb facing back toward the entrance.

### Optional / bonus
- 8. Construction in-progress shot — a home framed but not finished — reinforces "still being built" claim.
- 9. Sticker shock proof — close on a builder spec sheet showing the $189,185 entry price.
- 10. Builder model home interior (if permission) — 3 sec of a kitchen.

### Talking-head plate option for this segment
- Standing in front of a Pecan Creek subdivision sign, EG Realty polo, mid-day shadow management.
- Single 30-second take: "From here, BSW is twelve minutes via Loop 363."

### Optional / bonus

## NEIGHBORHOOD 3 — MORGANS POINT RESORT SEC 1 B-roll

**Streets to film:** Bluebonnet Loop (primary), Blackjack Circle, N Winecup Drive, Artesian Drive.
**Total runtime to cover:** ~3:00
**Time budget on-site:** 90 min (longer because of lake B-roll + drive-distance from Temple)

### Required shots
1. **Drone over Belton Lake** (8 sec) — open water, distant shore, late-afternoon light. This is the segment's emotional anchor.
2. **Drone over peninsula** (6 sec) — show Morgans Point Resort as a peninsula community surrounded by water. Reveals the geographic differentiator.
3. **Drive-in shot** (5 sec) — entrance to Morgans Point Resort, slow drive past the community sign.
4. **Lake-side street view** (4 sec) — Bluebonnet Loop showing the older, smaller home footprint typical of lake communities.
5. **Public boat ramp** (4 sec) — drive to the nearest public boat ramp from Bluebonnet Loop, time it. Capture the ramp itself, a boat launching, or the parking lot.
6. **Drone shot over a boat trailer + home combo** (3 sec) — the "garage for the boat" lifestyle anchor.
7. **Sunset over lake** (5 sec, end-of-day shot) — emotional close for the segment.
8. **Older home exterior** (3 sec) — a 1979-2007 era home representative of the cohort. Smaller footprint, mature trees, sometimes elevated for lake views.

### Optional / bonus
- 9. Marina / boat-in-water shot if accessible.
- 10. Trail or community amenity (if Morgans Point has one).
- 11. Sunset reflecting on lake water — the YouTube algorithm rewards this kind of strong visual closer.

### Talking-head plate option (RECOMMENDED for this segment)
- Standing at lake's edge or on a dock at sunset: "Twenty days. That's the fastest velocity of these three." Lake visible behind. This is the segment's strongest possible visual moment.
