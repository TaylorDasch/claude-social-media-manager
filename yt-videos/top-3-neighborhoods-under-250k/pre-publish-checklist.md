## ROLLBACK

If something goes wrong after publish (data error caught, disclosure missed, wrong data shown):

1. **Within first 6 hours, < 100 views:** Unpublish (set to private), fix, re-upload as same video (preserves URL).
2. **6–24 hours, 100–500 views:** Add corrected pinned comment with the fix. Edit description to acknowledge correction.
3. **>24 hours or >500 views:** Cannot meaningfully fix. Issue a follow-up Short or community post acknowledging the error. Trust > 100% accuracy.

**Authority for any post-publish change: Taylor only.** No agent auto-edits live published videos.
