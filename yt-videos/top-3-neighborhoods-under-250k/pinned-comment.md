# Pinned First Comment

**Purpose:** Earn engagement, demonstrate the "I read every comment" trust claim, give the algorithm an engagement signal in the first 60 minutes.

---

## PRIMARY (RECOMMENDED) — invitation + offer

```
If you're under $250K in Temple and you're somewhere between these three — newer than Westfield but cheaper than Pecan Creek, lake life but a faster commute to BSW — drop your situation below.

I read every comment. The answer's usually one of about four other neighborhoods that didn't make this list but might fit you.

Three constraints I'll ask in return:
1. Your budget (real, not aspirational)
2. Your commute target (BSW Temple, Fort Hood, downtown, or remote)
3. Brand new vs. established preference

That's enough to point you to the right block.

— Taylor
```

**Why this works:** It earns top-of-comments visibility and re-states the channel's voice (analyst, helpful, not pitchy). Three-constraint ask makes the response feel like real work, not a giveaway gimmick.

---

## ALTERNATIVE 1 — direct CTA-focused

```
The full MLS pull I used for this video — every active and recently closed listing under $250K in Temple-Belton — is in the description. No email gate.

Three things people are already asking via DM that I'm answering here so others see:

— Schools at Pecan Creek: Belton ISD for the northern lots, Temple ISD south of Loop 363 — verify the exact lot before writing.
— Westfield $89,900 Hawthorn: confirmed condition-driven (foundation work needed per my drive-by). Not your comp.
— Morgans Point Resort STR rules: shifted in 2023 — primary-residence only on most lots now. Buy to live in, not as Airbnb.

— Taylor, EG Realty Temple
```

**Why this works:** Pre-empts the three most likely questions before they get asked. Demonstrates the data depth without doubling video length.

**Risk:** Three claims here that need verification — schools, Hawthorn condition, STR rules. Use this version only if Taylor has verified all three.

---

## ALTERNATIVE 2 — light decision-flow

```
Quick decision flow for the comments:

If you're 6+ months out and have flexibility → Westfield watchlist
If you need to close in 60 days and want new → Pecan Creek
If you want the lake → Morgans Point Resort, with a fast offer

If your situation doesn't match any of these — comment with your budget, commute, and timeline. I'll point you at the right neighborhood.

— Taylor
```

**Why this works:** Re-states the decision matrix in comment form for the scrollers. Lower commitment than primary version, less "share your details" friction.

---

## CHOICE GUIDANCE

| Goal | Use |
|---|---|
| Maximize first-hour engagement | **PRIMARY** |
| Pre-empt common DM questions | **ALTERNATIVE 1** (only if verified) |
| Minimize ask, maximize visibility | **ALTERNATIVE 2** |

**Default:** Primary. Pin within 5 minutes of publish. Re-up if engagement drops after 24 hours.

---

## REPLY STRATEGY (first 24 hours)

- Heart every comment that includes a real budget + commute combination.
- Reply to top 5 with a direct neighborhood recommendation (uses the "Want me to send you the comp list?" close).
- Do NOT reply to all comments — algorithmically rewards quality replies, not volume.
- Save spam / "first" comments for next-day cleanup.
- For "what about [X neighborhood]?" comments, give one honest sentence — that becomes content for next month's video.
