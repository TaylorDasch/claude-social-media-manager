# Titles + Thumbnails — Top 3 Temple TX Under $250K

**Method:** 3 title options + 3 thumbnail concepts, council-tested. Recommend a primary + A/B fallback.
**Voice anchor:** Analyst, data-first. No clickbait theatrics. The numbers do the work.
**Length cap:** 100 chars hard, ~70 chars ideal for mobile.

---

## 3 TITLE OPTIONS

### Title 1 (RECOMMENDED — data-anchored, search-friendly)
**"3 Best Temple TX Neighborhoods Under $250K (May 2026 MLS Data)"**
- **Length:** 60 chars — full display on mobile.
- **Angle:** straight value match for the high-intent search ("best neighborhoods Temple TX under 250k").
- **Search intent match:** "best neighborhoods Temple TX," "Temple TX homes under 250k," "Temple TX real estate under 250k."
- **Why it wins:** Direct match for the searcher's exact words + data-credibility ("MLS Data") + month anchor.
- **A/B trigger:** Use if first 48-hour CTR ≥ 4%. This is the safe-default winner.

### Title 2 (Decision-frame angle — broader appeal)
**"Where to Buy Under $250K in Temple TX Right Now (3 Neighborhoods)"**
- **Length:** 64 chars.
- **Angle:** action-oriented, decision-frame ("right now").
- **Search intent:** "where to buy Temple TX," "should I buy Temple TX," "Temple TX buying."
- **Why it wins:** Captures the high-intent "where" question — the searcher already knows the city.
- **A/B trigger:** Swap to this if Title 1 underperforms after 48 hours.

### Title 3 (Curiosity-frame — highest CTR potential)
**"I Pulled the Temple TX MLS — 3 Neighborhoods Won Under $250K (May 2026)"**
- **Length:** 78 chars.
- **Angle:** process-oriented, hints at a methodology the viewer wants to know.
- **Search intent:** Less direct match, but the "I pulled the MLS" framing earns clicks from data-skeptical viewers.
- **Why it might win:** First-person methodology framing differentiates from the generic "top 10" listicle.
- **Trade-off:** Slightly less search-anchored than Title 1.

**Recommendation:** Ship with **Title 1** for first 48 hours. If CTR < 4%, A/B test **Title 3** (curiosity drive) next. **Title 2** is the safe long-tail evergreen fallback for the 6-month search archive.

## 3 THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS

### Thumbnail 1 (RECOMMENDED — three-name reveal, analyst-tone)
**Composition:**
- Vertical split, 60/40 (left larger).
- **LEFT (60%):** Taylor on-camera, neutral expression, looking directly at viewer, EG Realty polo. Plain neutral background (light gray or off-white).
- **RIGHT (40%):** Three stacked rows of text, white-on-dark navy:
  - "PECAN CREEK"
  - "WESTFIELD"
  - "MORGANS POINT RESORT"
- Below the three names, smaller text in muted amber: "UNDER $250K · MLS MAY 2026"
- Bottom corner: small "TEMPLE, TX" tag

**Why it wins:** The three names are the differentiator. Viewers searching "Temple neighborhoods" who recognize ANY of these three names will click on recognition alone. The analyst-tone framing (no shocked face, no red arrows) matches channel voice.

**Avoid:** No "VS" overlays. No arrows. No fire emojis. No price tags hovering. Neutral facial expression — slight inquisitive at most.

### Thumbnail 2 (Map-anchored, geographic specificity)
**Composition:**
- Top half: a stylized line outline of Temple-Belton area with three pins (color-coded) — one north (Pecan Creek), one southwest (Westfield), one south on the lake (Morgans Point Resort).
- Each pin labeled with the neighborhood name in small white text.
- Bottom half: Taylor on-camera, plain background, with text overlay "3 BEST UNDER $250K" in clean caps.
- Color palette: muted blue + white + single warm accent on pins.

**Why this might win:** Search-anchored — visually disambiguates from generic "top neighborhood" thumbnails. A viewer who knows Temple geography will get instant orientation. Especially strong for repeat-watcher value.

**Trade-off:** Less immediate impact than Thumbnail 1 — the map takes a beat to read. Better for long-tail search than first-day CTR.

### Thumbnail 3 (Decision-frame, dual-state)
**Composition:**
- Three small vertical photo strips across the top (~33% each) — one drone shot per neighborhood: new construction street (Pecan Creek), mature shade tree street (Westfield), lake water (Morgans Point Resort).
- Bottom half: Taylor on-camera looking at viewer, smaller scale, with large text overlay: "WHICH ONE'S FOR YOU?" in clean sans-serif white-on-dark.
- Tiny tag: "Temple TX · Under $250K"

**Why this might win:** Pairs with Title 2 (decision-frame). Three-image grid earns the click from anyone debating between neighborhoods.

**Trade-off:** Three photos = harder to read at thumbnail size on mobile. Risk of visual clutter. Lower contrast than Thumbnail 1.

## Banned execution patterns (do not let designer drift)

- No price-tag overlays floating over Taylor's face.
- No red downward arrows.
- No "shocked face" — channel voice is analyst, not reactor.
- No "DON'T BUY" warning framing.
- No fake "before/after" splits.
- No competitor logos.
- No "exclusive" or "secret" language.
- Single, clean color palette per concept — never more than 3 colors plus neutrals.

- Export 1280×720 at 90% quality JPEG (YouTube cap: 2MB).
