## SHORTS POSTING RULES (from prior lessons)

- Burn captions into the video — Shorts viewers watch muted.
- Hook in first 1.5 seconds, not first 5.
- Avoid the YouTube Shorts "ringer-bell" CTA — kills retention.
- Music: low-energy, instrumental, low-volume. Analyst voice carries.
- Lane discipline: zero investor framing. Lake = lifestyle, not Airbnb/STR.
- No banned voice words.
