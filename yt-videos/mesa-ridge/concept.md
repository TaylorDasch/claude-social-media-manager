## Core thesis

Mesa Ridge sits above Temple's active median as a neighborhood snapshot, but it is not one higher-end product. It combines entry-production homes, executive-series homes, active construction, and recent resales. Buyers should compare the right tier, price what is already finished, and inspect backyard sight lines created by the mix of one- and two-story homes.

## The three checks

1. **Choose the right version of Mesa Ridge.** Compare production, executive-series, resale, and builder inventory as separate choices rather than treating the subdivision median as a comp.
2. **Price what is already finished.** Compare the effective deal after concessions and the cost of fencing, shade, landscaping, storage, and other usable improvements—not list price alone.
3. **Check the backyard sight lines.** Stand at eye level, look above the fence, and check rear/side second-story windows, grade, setbacks, lot orientation, and future construction.

## Honest fit

Mesa Ridge may fit a buyer who wants newer housing in Temple city limits, Belton ISD, access to SH 317/West Adams, and a choice of product tiers. It may not fit a buyer whose priorities are a uniform one-story streetscape, mature tree buffers, maximum backyard seclusion, or zero nearby construction.
