## Research conclusion

The generic “affordable new construction” angle is both stale and occupied by a local competitor. The current evidence supports a sharper buyer decision:

> Mesa Ridge's July 30 active median was $404,000 versus $285,000 across Temple active residential listings, yet the neighborhood still ranged from $284,052 to $484,000. Buyers are not choosing one uniform product. They are choosing among entry production, executive-series homes, newer resales, builder inventory, and lots with different backyard sight lines.
