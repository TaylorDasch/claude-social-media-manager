## Landing lines

- `Same neighborhood name. Different product.`
- `The range describes Mesa Ridge. The comp set prices the house.`
- `Base price is not finished cost.`
- `You can change the landscaping. You cannot move the windows behind you.`
- `Mesa Ridge can be the right fit. It is not the automatic fit.`

## Honest negative

> “If you need a uniform one-story streetscape, mature tree buffers, or a backyard with no second-story sight lines, some Mesa Ridge lots will not be the right fit. Choose the exact lot carefully or let me show you another area.”

This is the Scars & All beat. Keep it neutral and lot-specific.

## Fit decision

**Worth comparing when the buyer wants:**

- Newer construction or a recent resale
- Temple city limits with Belton ISD, subject to exact-address verification
- Several product tiers inside one search area
- SH 317/West Adams access
- A choice between one- and two-story options

**Compare other areas when the buyer requires:**

- The lowest possible new-build entry price
- A uniform one-story streetscape
- Mature tree buffers and maximum backyard seclusion
- No nearby active construction
- A community pool, clubhouse, or playground; none was officially verified in this research
