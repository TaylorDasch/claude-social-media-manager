# Mesa Ridge, Temple TX — Shot List

**Rule:** every shot must prove product tier, completed usability, location, or lot sight lines. No decorative filler.

## Capture settings

- Film horizontal master in 4K.
- Lock white balance per location; do not use auto white balance mid-shot.
- Hold every B-roll clip for 8–12 seconds with 2–3 seconds of clean handles.
- Record long-form A-roll with wireless audio and a backup track if available.
- Record four dedicated vertical pickups from `short-form-clips.md`.
- Avoid visitors, license plates, children, identifiable neighbor activity, seller/family photos, mail, medications, calendars, alarm/security screens, keys, and lockbox/access codes.

## Optional drone — only after the legal gate

The drone is optional. The eye-level backyard test matters more.

1. **Community establisher:** wide push showing street layout and mixed home scale.
2. **Phase/product contrast:** elevated static/lateral frame that distinguishes smaller and larger product.
3. **Subject lot relationship:** seller-approved rise showing the home and lot without hovering over adjacent yards.
4. **Neighborhood-to-SH 317 orientation:** only if it can be flown without crossing traffic or people.

Before launch: use a current Part 107-certificated remote pilot with current recurrent training, or operate under that pilot's direct supervision. Confirm aircraft registration/Remote ID, B4UFLY provider, current TFRs, LAANC if required, wind, visual line of sight, seller/listing-agent launch permission, and no flight over open-house visitors or moving vehicles. Otherwise, skip the drone.
