# Mesa Ridge, Temple TX — Ground Truth

**As of:** August 1, 2026  
**Video use:** Neighborhood tour anchored at 2032 Clear Sky Court  
**Primary market source:** CTXMLS export dated July 30, 2026, plus direct Matrix verification dated July 31, 2026

## The defensible story

Mesa Ridge is not one product or one price tier. The July 30 CTXMLS snapshot captured active Mesa Ridge homes from **$284,052 to $484,000**, with an active median of **$404,000**. The same export's Temple-wide active residential median was **$285,000**. Mesa Ridge therefore skewed above Temple's active median in that dated snapshot, while still containing lower-priced production inventory and newer executive-series homes.

The film-day house at **2032 Clear Sky Court** is a separately verified active listing at **$329,000**. It sits below the subdivision's July 30 active median, but it is not automatically a bargain. It must be compared against homes that compete for the same buyer, including recent resales and builder inventory, with condition, concessions, lot, finish level, and completed improvements considered.

Taylor's on-site observation is that Mesa Ridge contains many two-story homes. That creates a lot-by-lot backyard sight-line question: a privacy fence blocks ground-level views, but not necessarily second-story windows. The builder's current Mesa Ridge plan page confirms that two-story plans are offered in the community, but the MLS export has no reliable story-count field. Do **not** state a percentage or say every backyard has a privacy issue.

## Official public context

- Mesa Ridge is inside incorporated Temple, in ZIP 76502, and is served by Belton ISD.
- Safe location wording: west/northwest Temple, immediately east of State Highway 317 and north of West Adams Avenue/FM 2305.
- Temple planning records show a multi-phase subdivision. Phase records include I, II, and III; the subject is in Phase II.
- A recorded Mesa Ridge Property Owners Association and restrictive covenants exist. Current dues, transfer fees, architectural rules, and rental rules require the current resale certificate/manager confirmation.
- No official source in this research verified an onsite pool, clubhouse, or neighborhood playground. Do not promise them.
- Crossroads Athletic Complex is a nearby public recreation anchor. The official page lists fields, tennis, pickleball, playgrounds, disc golf, and a 1.4-mile trail. Avoid an exact drive-time promise unless routed live on film day.
- State Highway 317 and FM 2305 provide a corridor toward Temple's Lake Park. Do not describe Lake Belton as being inside Mesa Ridge.
