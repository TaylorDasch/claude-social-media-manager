## The format spec (keep every video identical — that's the brand)
- **Length:** 4–8 min long-form for YouTube; cut a 30–45s vertical Short from the single best stretch.
- **The device:** a **live on-screen timer** (top corner) that starts at 0:00 the moment you pull out of the subdivision entrance and runs unbroken until you stop at the destination (BSW ER entrance / Fort Cavazos gate). No cuts during the drive — the unbroken timer IS the proof.
- **Lower-third (persistent):** subdivision name → destination, current price band, ISD. (Per your graphics rule: lower-third, not full-screen.)
- **Open (0:00–0:15):** cold hook over the car rolling out — *"It's 6:52 AM. I'm leaving [subdivision], and I'm driving to the BSW ER entrance — real time, no edits. Let's see what your on-call morning actually looks like."*
- **During the drive:** narrate the honest stuff — the one light that always backs up, the school-zone slowdown, where it opens up, the Starbucks/HEB you pass. This is the "little gems" layer.
- **Arrival beat:** stop the timer on camera at the destination. Call the number. *"7-oh-4. Eleven minutes, door to door. That's your worst-case winter-morning drive."*
- **Close/CTA:** *"I've timed every subdivision near BSW — comment your start time and I'll tell you which neighborhood gets you the most sleep. Full commute map's in the description."* → link to `neighborhoods-near-bsw-by-commute` page.
