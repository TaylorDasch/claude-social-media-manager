### 🎬 13:00 — SECTION 5: THE PROPERTY-TAX TRICK  `[STUDIO + simple overlay]`
> "Next is the question I get more than any other in Belton: *how do I get my property taxes down?* Couple of things you have to understand.
>
> One — **inside city limits versus outside.** Homes outside Belton's city limits, out in the county, can carry a lower tax rate because you're not paying city taxes. The catch: the line is messy — one street's in, the next street's out. You can check any address yourself on **bellcad.org**, the Bell County Appraisal District site, or I'll just set you up on the MLS filtered to outside-city-limits.
>
> Two — and this is the trap — **don't chase a low tax rate straight into a high MUD.** A lot of the newer subdivisions sit in a Municipal Utility District, and that MUD fee can quietly erase the savings you thought you were getting. So we don't look at the rate in isolation — we look at the *total* yearly number: taxes plus MUD plus HOA. That's the number that actually hits your payment. Get that wrong and a 'cheaper' house costs you more every month." `[GRAPHIC: "Low tax rate − high MUD = no savings. Compare TOTAL: tax + MUD + HOA"]`
