## SHORT #2 — "The driveway that's worth nothing" (landlocked trap) · ~28s
**Hook (0:00–0:03):** [B-ROLL: clean gravel driveway onto an empty lot] "This driveway might be worth nothing."
[ON-SCREEN: **'There's a driveway' ≠ legal access**]
**Body (0:03–0:22):** "If the only way onto the land crosses a neighbor's field and nothing's recorded, that lot can be legally landlocked, and a lender may refuse to finance it. You need public road frontage, or a recorded easement that runs with the land, so it stays attached to the property no matter who buys that field next. A handshake with today's neighbor may not survive the day he sells."
[ON-SCREEN: "Recorded easement = runs with the land"]
**CTA (0:22–0:28):** "Confirm it in the title commitment before you offer. It's one of 7 checks — free checklist linked below."

## SHORT #3 — "The ag-exemption trap" (rollback) · ~28s
**Hook (0:00–0:03):** [B-ROLL: cattle / hay bales on a tract] "That ag exemption dropping your tax bill? It can come back to bite you."
[ON-SCREEN: **THE ROLLBACK**]
**Body (0:03–0:22):** "Agricultural valuation taxes qualifying land on its use instead of full value — real savings, every year. But take it out of that ag use — stop running cattle, build a non-qualifying homesite — and the county can hit you with a rollback that recovers years of those savings, plus interest."
[ON-SCREEN: "Change the use → back taxes + interest"]
**CTA (0:22–0:28):** "Rollback rules change — confirm the current period with the Bell County Appraisal District before you touch anything. Free land checklist, link below."
