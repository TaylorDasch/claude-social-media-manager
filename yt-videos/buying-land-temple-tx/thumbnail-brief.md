# Thumbnail Brief — Buying Land Near Temple, TX (7 Checks)

## Concept (recommended): "CHEAP DIRT ≠ CHEAP TO BUILD"
The thumbnail must promise the counterintuitive payoff, not just "land for sale."

**Visual:** A wide Bell County tract from a low drone angle — open grass, a lone FOR SALE sign in the foreground, a tree line in the back. Slightly under-exposed/moody (not a bright listing photo) so the text pops and it reads as a warning, not a brochure.

**Text overlay (2 lines, huge, high-contrast):**
- Line 1 (white): `CHEAP LAND`
- Line 2 (emerald/yellow accent): `≠ CHEAP TO BUILD`
- Small corner tag (emerald pill): `7 CHECKS`

**Optional face variant (A/B test):** Taylor on the tract, arms crossed or pointing at the ground, half-frame right; text stack left. Face thumbnails usually out-CTR pure-landscape for a local-authority channel — shoot both.

**Why it earns the click:** It states the video's whole thesis (price tag ≠ all-in cost) as a visual contradiction the viewer wants resolved. It does NOT use a banned listing cliché, a price number, or a stat — so it stays honest and evergreen.

## Color / type
- Brand emerald `#059669` accent on a desaturated land photo; white Outfit-bold or a heavy condensed sans for the overlay.
- Avoid red "SCAM"-style alarm styling — keep it analyst/operator, not clickbait-panic.

## Hard rules
- No banned words (no "dream," "perfect," "stunning," etc.).
- No specific price or the $469K median (keep numbers off the thumbnail — evergreen).
- Text legible at mobile size (test at 120px wide): ≤ 4 words total in the main stack.

- Canva (authenticated MCP) — fastest; use the brand emerald + a real Temple-area drone still from Taylor's library.
