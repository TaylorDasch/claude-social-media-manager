## METHODOLOGY (declared on-camera at 1:00; defends selection against bias)

A Temple corridor qualifies if AT LEAST ONE trigger applies, sourced to a public dataset:
1. **Build-era + soil overlap.** Slab-on-grade 1965–1985 OR pier-and-beam pre-1970, on Houston Black clay.
2. **Data-aggregation artifact.** Non-residential pin (hospital, retail, transit) that crime maps can't separate from adjacent residential blocks.
3. **Tracked price movement.** ±8% YoY on a public neighborhood tracker, mix-shift caveats acknowledged on-camera.

**Excludes:** race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability, school rating, income tract, "perceived character." Every Temple corridor meeting 1/2/3 was eligible regardless of demographics.
