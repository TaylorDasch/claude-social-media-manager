### #3 — WESTERN HILLS / RIVER OAKS

**Boundary:** Antelope Trail, Phoenix Dr, Arapaho Dr, Dakota Dr, Chisholm Trail. Centered on Western Hills Elementary at 600 Arapaho Dr.
**Caution category:** Slab-on-grade foundation risk on Houston Black clay from the 1965–1985 build era. **NOT a crime caution. Data does not support a crime caution here.**

**Data points (sourced):**
1. DoorProfit gives Western Hills a crime grade of A-. Ranks alongside The Oaks, Wildwood, and The Bluffs as Temple's safest neighborhoods. ~1,608 residents, median HH income $86,111. (https://www.doorprofit.com/crime-map/city/Temple-TX/neighborhood/western_hills/)
2. Western Hills Elementary opened Fall 1968 at 600 Arapaho Dr. Spot-check: 3505 Antelope Trail built 1965, 2,389 sqft. Core build era 1965–1985. (Bell CAD; ground-truth pack §A.)
3. Houston Black clay underlies the area — PI ~44, 4–8 inches of vertical movement, cracks 0.5–4 inches wide at 12-inch depth in dry periods. Slab-on-grade dominant 1965–1985. Central TX 2025–26 repair costs: $3,500–$12,000 typical, $30K+ for severe stabilization. 6-pier corner job $6K–$15K. Full slab with pilings $8K–$25K. Plumbing repair often equals foundation cost. Structural engineer evaluation $400–$850, ~$600 typical. (Ground-truth pack §A.)

**Local-knowledge add:** Highest-leverage move on any home in this build era: $600 structural engineer report inside the option period, not the inspector's foundation comment. Same engineer flags supply/drain lines under the slab — silent budget-killer in any piling job.

**Honest counter-argument:** Cleanest crime profile in the city. Solid lots, established trees, walkable elementary. Foundation risk is real but predictable, inspectable, budgetable. Most buyer-friendly "caution area" on the list.

## DELIVERABLE 8 — PHRASES TO AVOID (BY REASON)

**Coded steering — racial / national-origin / class proxies (HUD-flagged):** bad area, rough part, ghetto, sketchy, going downhill, transitional, up-and-coming, diverse (when describing demographics), ethnic (when describing demographics), urban (when describing demographics), quiet (when describing demographics).

**Familial-status / sex steering (explicit case-law):** safe for families, good for kids, kid-friendly, not safe for a woman alone.

**School steering (allowed only with named source):** good schools (without rating + source), bad schools.

**Subjective commentary that pivots info → advice:** don't buy here, I wouldn't recommend living here, you wouldn't want to live there, my expertise, insider, sneak peek.

**Salesperson clichés (Taylor brand voice — banned regardless of fair-housing):** dream home, charming, nestled, turnkey, white glove, hidden gem, perfect neighborhood, exclusive, paradise, oasis, stunning, gorgeous, dream.

**Why each cluster matters:** Cluster 1 triggers disparate-impact complaints even with neutral language. Cluster 2 triggers explicit protected-class steering. Cluster 3 fails for data-quality reasons. Cluster 4 pivots legally-defensible info into legally-actionable advice. Cluster 5 destroys investor-analyst brand voice — HUD-26-028 didn't move that line either way.
