## LEGAL FRAME (the news angle that powers the whole video)

**HUD-26-028 — issued 2026-04-26.** HUD Secretary Scott Turner's "Dear Colleague" letter explicitly reversed Biden-era guidance and clarified that real estate agents **DO NOT violate the Fair Housing Act** by discussing crime data and school quality with clients, provided the information is:
1. Objective, factual, and verifiable
2. Sourced from named third-party data
3. Applied **consistently** to all clients regardless of protected class

Quote: "Americans should not be left in the dark about vital facts like neighborhood safety or school quality." HUD directed Fair Housing Assistance Programs not to issue discrimination findings *solely* because an agent shared such data.

**What did NOT change:**
- NAR Article 10 still applies (no volunteering racial/religious/ethnic composition)
- Subjective commentary still risky ("bad area," "rough part," "don't buy here")
- Disparate-impact theory still operative — disproportionate steering away from majority-minority neighborhoods can trigger complaint even with neutral language
- TREC §531.19 fair-housing compliance unchanged

**Source URLs:**
- HUD release: https://www.hud.gov/news/hud-no-26-028
- Inman 2026-04-28: https://www.inman.com/2026/04/28/hud-rolls-back-fair-housing-guardrails-on-crime-stats-and-school-ratings/
- Inman 2026-05-05 (what didn't change): https://www.inman.com/2026/05/05/what-hud-just-changed-about-fair-housing-talk-and-what-it-didnt/

## AREA-BY-AREA VERDICT (data-driven)

### A) Western Hills / River Oaks (Antelope Trail, Phoenix Dr, Arapaho Dr, Dakota Dr, Chisholm Trail)

**Crime caution: UNSUPPORTED.**
- DoorProfit crime grade: **A-**
- Ranks with Temple's safest neighborhoods (tied with The Oaks, Wildwood, The Bluffs)
- ~1,608 residents, median HH income $86,111
- Assault index 124, larceny 144, burglary 81 (vs national=100)
- Source: https://www.doorprofit.com/crime-map/city/Temple-TX/neighborhood/western_hills/

**Foundation caution: SUPPORTED.**
- Western Hills Elementary opened **Fall 1968** at 600 Arapaho Dr — neighborhood developed late 1960s
- Spot-check: 3505 Antelope Trl built 1965, 2,389 sqft
- Era of construction: 1960s-1980s confirmed for core area; northern stretch extends later
- **Soil:** Houston Black clay (USDA Soil Survey Regional Office for Houston Black Clay is literally located in Temple, TX). Plasticity Index ~44. Swells 6-12% with moisture change. Cracks 1/2 to 4 inches wide at 12-inch depth in dry periods. Generates up to 4-8 inches of vertical ground movement.
- Foundation type: slab-on-grade dominant 1965-1985 (Western Hills era)
- **Repair costs (Central TX 2025-2026):** $3,500-$12,000 typical; severe stabilization can exceed $30,000. Per-pier underpinning $1,000-$3,000 installed. 6-pier corner job ~$6,000-$15,000. Full slab repair with pilings $8,000-$25,000. Drain/supply lines under slab commonly damaged during piling work — plumbing repair can equal foundation work cost.
- **Structural engineer cost:** $400-$850 standard residential evaluation; ~$600 flat typical. Taylor's $400-$700 estimate is accurate, slightly conservative.

**Council frame:** Reframe Western Hills entirely. NOT a crime caution. Foundation/structural caution only. The strongest single technical claim Taylor can make on camera and it's fully verifiable.

## FAIR-HOUSING-SAFE WORDING (verbatim — enforce in script)

### Safe phrasings (objective, sourced, applied consistently)
| SAFE | STEERING (NEVER USE) |
|---|---|
| "Per FBI 2024 data, this census tract reports 2,362 property crimes per 100,000" | "This is a bad area" |
| "CrimeGrade rates this zone D+; here's their methodology" | "I wouldn't live there" |
| "Property crime is 34% above national average citywide" | "It's going downhill fast" |
| "Here are the public crime-map links — pull what matters to you" | "Don't buy here" |
| "Vehicle theft odds are 1 in 282 citywide; verify on FBI CDE" | "You'd be safer in a nicer neighborhood" |
| "School ratings: GreatSchools 4/10 — here's the link" | "Schools here are terrible" |
| "Median household income is $X per Census 2020" | "It's a low-class area" |
| "This corridor has older housing stock from the 1940s" | "It's a sketchy part of town" |
| "Hospital-district pins aggregate incidents at the medical campus address" | "Hospitals attract bad people" |
| "Buyers should run their own crime, school, and commute analysis" | "Buyers like you won't want this neighborhood" |

### Words/phrases to NEVER use
- "Bad area," "rough part," "ghetto," "sketchy," "going downhill," "transitional," "up-and-coming" (coded for gentrification/racial change)
- "Good schools" / "bad schools" without naming the rating source
- "Safe for families," "good for kids," "kid-friendly" (familial-status steering)
- "Not safe for a woman alone" (sex steering — explicit case-law example)
- "Quiet" / "diverse" / "ethnic" / "urban" (HUD-flagged coded)
- Any race/ethnicity/national-origin/religion/family-status/disability reference
- "Don't buy here" / "I wouldn't recommend living here" (even with data — verbal pivot from info to steering)
