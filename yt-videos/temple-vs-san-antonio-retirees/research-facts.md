## 1. NURSING — the flip beat (strongest in the video)
- RN mean pay **dead heat:** Killeen-Temple **$92,400/yr** (BLS OEWS regional release, May 2024) vs San Antonio **$88,880/yr** (BLS OEWS, May 2023). 2023 apples-to-apples: $88,680 vs $88,880 — **~$200 apart.** **[H]**
- San Antonio has **~22,140 RNs** vs Killeen-Temple **~5,260–5,440** → **~4× the nurse supply for the same paycheck.** **[H]**
- SATX RN pay runs **~9% below the Texas average** (~$38.75/hr); new-grad market described as oversaturated (more grads than new-grad openings). Her "low paying" instinct = **correct.** **[M]**
- Temple = "the town built around a hospital," literally: **Baylor Scott & White – Temple is the ORIGINAL flagship** (founded **1897** as Temple Sanitarium; the whole $25B+ system traces to this building), **Level I trauma — only one on I-35 between Dallas & Austin, 636 beds**, Texas A&M teaching hospital, **31 residency/fellowship programs.** Plus **McLane Children's** (only children's hospital Dallas↔Austin), **Olin E. Teague VA** (Level 1-A), **Cedar Crest** psych (Belton), + **Fort Hood** healthcare demand. **[H]**
- **VERDICT — Temple wins for a nurse:** equal/higher pay, dramatically cheaper housing, recession-proof employer base, far less saturation. "Not as much nursing options" is a **misconception** — Temple is *concentrated* with nursing jobs per capita.
- ⚠️ Don't quote any SATX RN figure under ~$80K on camera (bad scrape artifacts). Safe framing: "about the same — roughly $90K mean in both, verify current BLS."

## 2. AFFORDABILITY & TAXES
- Median home **sale price ≈ TIE:** Temple ~$255K–$261K (Redfin/Zillow, early 2026, down YoY); San Antonio ~$260K (Redfin, Mar 2026, down YoY). **[H]**
- Median **rent ≈ TIE:** Temple ~$1,695 vs SATX ~$1,699 (Zillow all-types, 2025). SATX has far more inventory/variety. **[H for Temple/M for parity]**
- **Cost of living: Temple ~8% lower** — BestPlaces 83.7 (Temple) vs 91.3 (SATX). **[H]**
- **Property tax: Temple/Bell lower** — Bell effective ~1.6–2.0% vs Bexar ~2.27%. On a $260K home ≈ $700–$1,700/yr less in Temple. Verify exact ISD/parcel before quoting a dollar figure. **[H directional / M exact]**
- **Texas wins for both equally:** NO state income tax; **military retirement pay & pensions NOT taxed by TX**; over-65 homestead **+$60K** school exemption on top of standard $100K; **over-65 school-tax freeze** (caps school taxes at the year-you-turn-65 amount); **100% service-connected disabled vet = $0 property tax** on homestead. **[H]**
- ⚠️ Over-65 freeze TRANSFER form = **Form 50-272** (School Tax Ceiling Certificate, Age 65+), NOT 50-132. Keep exact form OFF camera ("ask the appraisal district which certificate"). **[H]**

## 3. DIVERSITY — be honest, SATX wins, but her premise is overstated
- Temple city: **~51% White-NH, 29% Hispanic, 12% Black, ~2% Asian.** Bell County: **42% White, 21.5% Black** (well above TX ~13%), 26% Hispanic — **Fort Hood drives this** (Killeen ~38% Black). **[H]**
- San Antonio: **~65% Hispanic-majority**, deep Mexican-American culture/food/festivals. **[H]**
- **VERDICT:** SATX wins Hispanic cultural density + big-city variety (real, concede it). But "Temple has no diversity" is **inaccurate** — Temple is ~half non-white; Bell County is one of TX's more Black/military-diverse regions. Different *flavor*: military melting-pot vs Hispanic-majority.

## 4. HOMES / PERRY / "UNDERDEVELOPED"
- **Perry Homes does NOT build in Temple/Bell** — only Houston, Dallas, San Antonio, Austin. **Nearest Perry = Georgetown (~45 min south).** Concede this plainly. **[H]**
- Builders that DO build in Temple/Belton: **D.R. Horton, Lennar, KB, Centex** + regional (Stylecraft, Carothers, Ashford, Kiella, John Houston). Mid-market, **not Perry-tier luxury master-planned.** **[H]**
- New-build value: **Temple ~$174/sqft vs SATX new build ~$248/sqft.** Temple wins new-build $/sqft. **[H Temple / M SATX]**
- $350–450K buys: **Temple = ~1,750–2,580 sqft, lots 0.25–0.8+ acre** (real MLS examples: $379K/1,954sf Mesa Ridge; $389K/1,900sf on 0.8ac no-HOA; $390K/2,016sf wooded lot near Lake Belton). **SATX = ~1,400–1,800 sqft new on ~0.12–0.17 acre.** **[H Temple / M SATX]**
- "Isolated/underdeveloped" = **OVERSTATED:** Temple on **I-35**, ~1 hr Austin, ~40 min Waco; **pop ~102K, growing ~4%/yr** (was 82K in 2020); BSW = **$2.5B economic impact**, 40%+ of workforce in healthcare; **Pepper Creek 5.6-mi trail**; downtown revitalization; $122M+ 2024 investment. **[H]**
- ⚠️ On-camera safe: frame the Temple side concretely; say SATX new-build sqft "roughly" / "verify."

## 5. MILITARY / SAFETY / FAMILY / CONSERVATIVE
- **Military medical — SATX wins decisively:** Brooke Army Medical Center (BAMC) at JBSA-Fort Sam Houston = **DoD's largest hospital + only Level I trauma center in the military health system** + the DoD burn center; **JBSA = 3 installations** (Lackland, Randolph, Fort Sam Houston); huge retiree community. **[H]**
- **Temple still competes for a VA retiree:** **Olin E. Teague VA Medical Center is IN Temple** (90-bed tertiary, surgical referral center for Central TX VA, 24/7 ER); **Fort Hood** (name restored June 11, 2025) ~35–40 min via Killeen with **commissary, exchange, Carl R. Darnall Army Medical Center**; Darnall serves **retirees + ~145K family/vets within a 40-mi radius** (Temple is inside it). **[H]**
- **Safety — Temple wins:** Temple ~**27/1,000 total, 3/1,000 violent**; San Antonio ~**52/1,000 total, ~6/1,000 violent** → ~**2× lower** in Temple (NeighborhoodScout, same methodology; rounds to whole numbers). **[M-H]**
- **Schools — Temple area wins:** **Belton ISD = B (80)**, beats all 3 major SATX districts (Northside, North East, San Antonio ISD = all **C**); **Temple ISD = C (77)** ties them. (TEA 2024–25 A–F, released Aug 2025.) **[H]**
- **Kid attractions — SATX wins big:** SeaWorld, Six Flags Fiesta Texas + Hurricane Harbor, San Antonio Zoo, Morgan's Wonderland, The DoSeum, Witte Museum, River Walk. Temple → drive to Austin (~1hr)/Waco (~45min) for marquee stuff. **[H]**
- **Conservative lean — Temple/Bell wins:** Bell County 2024 **Trump 57.4% / Harris 41.2% (+16)**; **Bexar went Harris (~54.9%)** — one of only 12 of 254 TX counties Harris carried (SATX trending redder but still blue). **[H]**
