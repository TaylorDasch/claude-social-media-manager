## Caption

Two homes can look identical online and still carry different annual costs or school assignments.

Before a Temple or Belton buyer writes an offer, I check three separate maps: city limits, MUD boundaries, and the school district assigned to the parcel. None should be inferred from the mailing address alone.

The honest negative is that every win has a tradeoff. Temple is usually the cleaner BSW commute, but its city tax stack is higher. Belton has the walkable courthouse square and the current district-rating edge, but buyers near Nolan Creek still need a real flood-zone determination. New construction outside either city can erase expected tax savings when a MUD is attached.

Taylor Dasch with EG Realty helps buyers run those checks before they get emotionally committed to a property. DM **COMPARE** for the Temple-vs-Belton relocation cost sheet.
