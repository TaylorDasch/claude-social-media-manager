## Caption

At the $350,000 price band, the median Temple sale was about 2,062 square feet and the median Belton sale was about 2,070 square feet. That is an eight-square-foot difference—not enough to choose a city.

The real decision starts after the listing photos.

First, check whether the property is inside city limits. The city line can change the annual tax bill by thousands of dollars on the same purchase price.

Second, check for a MUD. In Three Creeks, the MUD rate alone can add roughly $2,740–$3,325 per year on a $350,000 value before property-specific exemptions.

Third, verify the school district by address. A Temple mailing address does not guarantee Temple ISD; several northwest Temple neighborhoods feed into Belton ISD.
