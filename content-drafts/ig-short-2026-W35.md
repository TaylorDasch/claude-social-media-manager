## Caption

Taylor Dasch with EG Realty here. One of the easiest ways to under-budget a new-construction home in Temple or Belton is to copy the property-tax number shown on a listing site.

That record may reflect the lot before the house was completed. Once the finished home is appraised, the taxable value can rise, and a later escrow analysis can raise the monthly payment with it.

Before you compare a builder home with a resale, check the exact lot in the appraisal record, identify every local taxing entity, and ask the lender to model the payment from a reasonable finished-home value. Then add insurance, mortgage insurance when applicable, HOA dues, and any district assessment. Principal and interest alone is not the payment.

The honest negative: this estimate still is not a tax guarantee. Appraised value, exemptions, and local rates can change. The goal is to budget from a defensible number instead of the temporary vacant-lot snapshot.
