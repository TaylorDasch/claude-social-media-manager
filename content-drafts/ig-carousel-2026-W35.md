Taylor Dasch with EG Realty here. If you are choosing between Temple and Belton, the Aug. 24 MLS snapshot gives you a cleaner starting point than the usual “which city is better?” argument.

The honest negative: none of those citywide figures tells you what one house is worth. Condition, subdivision, price history, financing, taxes, HOA or district costs, and current competition can matter more than the city label.
