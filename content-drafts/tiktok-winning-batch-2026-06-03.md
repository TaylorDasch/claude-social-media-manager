## FORMAT 1 — Honest / contrarian relocation takes
*(Your "What no one tells you about Temple" = 12,800 views, 457 likes, 102 comments. People save and argue with honesty.)*

### 1A — "The Temple tax number nobody warns you about"
**Hook (on cam, 0–2s):** "Nobody moving to Temple talks about this number — and it costs them six hundred bucks a month."

**Word-for-word script (~40s):**
"Property taxes in Bell County run about 2.4 to 2.7 percent. On a $300,000 house, that's roughly $625 a month — before your mortgage, before insurance. Here's the trap: the tax number on the Zillow listing is usually *last year's*, on a lower value. Your real bill is city, plus county, plus school district, plus any MUD or PID the neighborhood added. Two houses at the same price can have payments $200 a month apart just on taxes. Before you fall in love with a house, ask for the *current* total rate at that exact address. Comment TAX and I'll send you the cheat sheet to check any Temple address in two minutes."

**Talking points:** Bell County ~2.4–2.7% → ~$625/mo on $300K · listing tax = last year's, misleading · real bill = city+county+school+MUD/PID · same price ≠ same payment ($200/mo swing) · check current rate at the exact address.
**On-screen text:** "2.4–2.7% in Bell County" / "$300K ≈ $625/mo in TAXES" / "the listing shows LAST year's number" / "comment TAX"
**Caption:** The Temple property-tax number nobody warns relocators about. Comment TAX for the cheat sheet. #templetx #templetexas #movingtotexas #propertytaxes #relocatetexas #bellcounty #honestrealtor
**Keyword:** TAX
