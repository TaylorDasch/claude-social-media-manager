Market gauge

Temple and Belton had 885 active residential listings in the August 17 snapshot, with a 73-day median DOM. 493 of 885 active listings (55.7%) were priced below their original list price, including 116 (13.1%) reduced at least 8%. The read: buyer room exists, but a price cut does not prove value. Condition, original pricing, comparable sales, and seller response still decide whether an offer makes sense.

The play this issue

Separate the clean offer from the low offer. Use current comparable sales and the documented listing history to explain the number, include solid proof of funds or preapproval, and keep the terms as simple as the buyer's risk tolerance allows. Price alone should not be a reason to waive inspection, title, financing, or other material protections.
