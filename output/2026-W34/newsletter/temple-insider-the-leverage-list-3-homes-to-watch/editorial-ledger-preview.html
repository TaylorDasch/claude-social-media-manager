Why it is on the watch

A matched Bell County public notice schedules an October 6, 2026 trustee sale. That separate record may create deadline pressure, but it does not prove the seller will discount the home.

Important distinction

A scheduled trustee sale is not a completed foreclosure or bank-owned sale. It may be cured, postponed, or canceled. Recheck the live listing, public notice, title, condition, and comparable sales.
03Where I’d pump the brakes 
Caution signal

Do not confuse a deadline with a discount.

Chisholm's public notice is a reason to investigate timing—not a basis for calling it a bargain. At E 27th, long market time does not answer the solar or condition questions. At Ambrose, a deep cut from the original list price still has to make sense against current comparable sales.
The play this issue

Separate the clean offer from the low offer.

Use current comparable sales and the documented listing history to explain the number. Include solid proof of funds or preapproval, and keep the terms as simple as the buyer's risk tolerance allows. Price alone should not be a reason to waive inspection, title, financing, or other material protections.
