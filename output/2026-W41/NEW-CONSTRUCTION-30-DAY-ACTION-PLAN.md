## Judgment calls (where this plan departs from the ChatGPT doc)

1. **Do not build a second cash tool.** The Oct 1 Year-Two Test offer already extends the first-time-buyer worksheet into year-one vs. year-two carrying costs. Route 1 points at `/first-time-home-buyers/` and the Year-Two Test. ChatGPT's "cash calendar" becomes a Phase 2 add-on only if intake shows lease-timing questions.
2. **Static before interactive.** Both proposed interactive tools (cash calendar, move planner) are multi-day build projects, not 45-minute tasks. A dated worked example on a page proves demand first. Build the interactive version only if people use the static one.
3. **Downsizer deferred.** It's the weakest overlap with your two priority personas (BSW/Fort Cavazos relocators and investors). The retiring page and video already exist. Investigation #2 serves downsizers anyway because the floor-plan test is their content. No workshop, no organizer outreach in this push.
4. **Case studies are gated, not blocking.** "First six months" and "delayed move" need consenting real people. Source them now; publish only when the evidence exists.
5. **Builder-relations risk on Investigation #1.** Publishing dated "what's actually unfinished" about named homes can irritate reps. Mitigation: factual, dated statements only; give each rep the chance to update before publish; frame it as what a remote buyer needs, not as a builder critique. Your builder-complaints research (`research/new-construction-builder-complaints-research.md`) stays separate from this.
6. **"30-day" is an editorial frame.** Never a promise. Say so on the page.
