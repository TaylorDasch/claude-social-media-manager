**Subject:** The Temple–Belton 60-day listing test

## The 60-day listing test

Fresh and older Temple/Belton listings are not giving buyers the same signal.

In my July 20 Active Residential sample, 28/204 listings at 0–30 days on market had recorded a price cut: 13.7%, or about one in seven. It was 84/185 (45.4%) at 31–60 days, 76/114 (66.7%) at 61–90 days, and 298/367 (81.2%) after day 90.

That does not mean every older seller will accept a low offer. The latest 200 qualifying closings still finished at a median 99.76% of final list.

My practical test is: compare relative days on market, current versus original price, and the pace of the closest substitutes. The new video shows how I use all three for a relocating buyer.
