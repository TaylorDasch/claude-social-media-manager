## 2. What this video covers

In my July 20 Temple/Belton Active Residential sample, 28/204 listings at 0–30 days on market had recorded a price cut: 13.7%, or about one in seven. That rose to 84/185 (45.4%) at 31–60 days, 76/114 (66.7%) at 61–90 days, and 298/367 (81.2%) after day 90.

But older does not automatically mean cheap: the latest 200 qualifying closings finished at a median 99.76% of final list. I break down what that means for fresh listings, older listings, new construction, resale homes, and a buyer deciding whether to move quickly or press harder.

The current Active sample contains 870 rows / 869 unique addresses, with a $299,440 median ask, 69.5 median DOM, and 486/870 price cuts (55.9%). The latest Closed sample has 200 records, a $278,670 median close, 50 median DOM, 99.76% median close-to-final-list, 97.01% median close-to-original-list, and 103/200 (51.5%) closings below final list.

Against the May continuity set, current Active count is down 2.1%, median ask is down 3.4%, median DOM is down 2.1%, and price-cut share is up 1.8 percentage points. May status was inferred, so those figures are continuity evidence—not a precise apples-to-apples comparison.

You’ll learn the three numbers I check:

1. relative days on market;
2. current price versus original price;
3. the pace and closing behavior of the closest substitutes.

May–July Active continuity: Based on information from Central Texas MLS as of May 14 and July 20, 2026. Temple + Belton; July uses Residential listings with Status = Active, while the May file lacks Status and PropertyType and uses its best-comparable first status block. Directional continuity only; changing mix.

Closed-sample comparison: Based on information from Central Texas MLS for April 15–May 14 and June 21–July 20, 2026. Temple + Belton; n=187 and n=200; medians; different samples and mixes; seller credits unavailable. July uses Residential listings with Status = Closed.

The current source includes explicit `Status` and `PropertyType` fields. The DOM/price-cut groups are a cross-sectional snapshot, not proof that listing age causes a reduction or that older listings sell for larger discounts. The May comparison uses an inferred status group and is continuity evidence only. Sales figures apply a $25,000 minimum sale-price filter. Medians do not value an individual property, and the export does not show seller credits.

Information is general and subject to change. Verify the exact property, price history, condition, financing, taxes, insurance, HOA/district obligations, inspection, title, and contract terms with the appropriate professionals.
