Which home gives a buyer more room to press?

- A fresh listing at 12 days
- The same kind of home at 75 days
- It depends on the price history and competing homes

My answer is the third—but the July Temple/Belton data shows why the 60 and 90-day marks deserve attention.

About 14% of active listings at 0–30 days had recorded a price cut—roughly one in seven. At 61–90 days it was about 67%, and after day 90 it was about 81%.
