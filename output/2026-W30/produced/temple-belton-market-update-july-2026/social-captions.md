# Native Social Captions

## Facebook

The broad headline is that 486/870 (55.9%) of the current Temple/Belton Active Residential sample had cut price. The useful part is **where** those cuts sit.

- 0–30 days on market: 28/204 (13.7%, about one in seven)
- 31–60 days: 84/185 (45.4%)
- 61–90 days: 76/114 (66.7%)
- 91+ days: 298/367 (81.2%)

That does not make every 90-day listing a bargain. The latest 200 qualifying closings still finished at a median 99.76% of final list.

## LinkedIn

The most useful housing-market number is often not the citywide average.

In the July 20 Temple/Belton Active Residential sample, prior price reductions appeared on 28/204 (13.7%) of 0–30 DOM listings, 84/185 (45.4%) at 31–60 DOM, 76/114 (66.7%) at 61–90 DOM, and 298/367 (81.2%) after day 90.

The closed side adds the necessary guardrail: the latest 200 qualifying closings finished at a median 99.76% of final list. Listing age helps locate the conversation; price history and substitutes determine whether the evidence supports pressing.
