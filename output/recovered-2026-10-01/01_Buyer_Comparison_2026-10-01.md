## What was supplied, and what this comparison can and cannot do

**Supplied:** three MLS "360 Property View" sheets printed 10/01/2026 (3604 Littleleaf Dr, Killeen; 2201 Westcliff Rd, Killeen; 2409 Patrick Henry St, Temple) and two MLS CSV exports of Killeen small-multifamily listings and sales (export date not stamped; latest closings 09/21/2026 and 09/24/2026).
**Not supplied:** the buyer's decision statement, must-haves, budget or pre-approval, timeline, commute destination, lender scenario, insurance quotes, seller's disclosures, inspection reports, leases, or surveys. No listing URLs were provided; private MLS links were not opened.
**Consequence:** all three properties are duplexes, so this comparison assumes the decision under consideration is **buying a two-unit property, either to live in one side or to hold as a rental**. That is inferred from the shortlist, not stated by the buyer. **Personal-fit rankings below are provisional** until the buyer confirms occupancy plans, financing type, budget, timeline and destination.
**Access gaps:** Bell County Appraisal District's property search was unreachable from this environment on 10/01/2026, so appraised values and current exemptions come only from the MLS sheets. FEMA flood zones were checked against the National Flood Hazard Layer on 10/01/2026 (point and short-radius queries; not a flood determination or elevation certificate).

## Short recommendation (provisional)

- **Next look: 2409 Patrick Henry St, Temple.** Lowest price ($219,000), largest living area (2,142 sf), two full baths per unit, one unit already leased at $995 through 02/28/2027 and the other vacant for an owner-occupant, Side B HVAC replaced in 2025, seller "may consider a concession," and a survey and seller's disclosure already on file. It is the oldest building (1981) and Temple's tax rate (2.39) is higher than Killeen's (2.03), so verify taxes and condition before leaning on the low price. Financing per the listing is Cash or Conventional only.
- **Conditional: 3604 Littleleaf Dr, Killeen.** Newest building (2003), both units vacant, and the only listing that accepts FHA and VA. It is sold AS-IS with "light TLC" and any lender-required repairs on the buyer, the units are 2-bed/1-bath (the smallest configuration), and the $4,761 tax figure reflects the current owner's homestead exemption, so a buyer's taxes will likely be higher. Worth a look if the buyer needs FHA/VA or wants vacant possession of both sides.
- **Weaker fit on current evidence: 2201 Westcliff Rd, Killeen.** Highest price per square foot ($131.71), 2-bed/1-bath units, and an unresolved flood question: the MLS sheet says "FEMA Flood Plain: Partial" and "Res Flooded: Unknown," and the FEMA layer shows Zone AE and a regulatory floodway within about 30 meters of the geocoded site on the 0.823-acre lot. The sheet also lists one heating unit, one A/C unit and one water heater (possibly shared systems) and conflicting jurisdiction fields. It is not ruled out: the garages and large lot are real advantages, and the flood status of the building itself is unknown, not bad. It needs the flood answer before it earns a showing.

**Observed facts** are in the tables below with their sources. **Judgment** is confined to this recommendation and the "fit and trade-offs" rows, and it is provisional.
