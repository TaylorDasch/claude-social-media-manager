# Reusable Shortlist Template and Minimum Input Checklist

## Minimum inputs before a comparison is decision-grade
- [ ] Buyer decision in one sentence (what they are buying and why)
- [ ] Must-haves and nice-to-haves; deal-breakers
- [ ] Budget ceiling and pre-approval (loan type, down payment, max payment)
- [ ] Timeline (move date, lease end, PCS or job start)
- [ ] Commute destination(s) and times; measure drives on two weekdays at the real hour
- [ ] MLS sheet per property with print date; listing URL
- [ ] Appraisal district record (value, exemptions, taxing units)
- [ ] Seller's disclosure; survey; leases and rent ledger if occupied; HOA documents if any
- [ ] Lender scenario in writing (rate, points, MI, reserves, concession cap, fees)
- [ ] Insurance quotes (homeowners; flood if in or near an SFHA)
- [ ] Inspection reports once under contract

**Cost scenarios:** use the workbook (Inputs → Scenarios → Sensitivity). Keep identical global assumptions across properties; expose property-specific differences (financing accepted, flood, HOA, taxes, rent in place). Label every placeholder. Never call a scenario a Loan Estimate.

**Call questions:** rank by whether the answer could change the decision, affordability, offer or timeline; for each give the question, who answers, why it matters, the evidence to request, and the decision that changes. Deal-stoppers first.

**Internal notes (separate file):** agent-only remarks, negotiation observations with verification steps, comp review with adjustment limits, compliance reminders.

**Handoff:** what the agent verifies, what the lender confirms, the next buyer decision, and the three actions that would most improve the decision (owner and finished output for each).
