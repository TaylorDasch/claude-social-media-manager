## THE 4 RULES (read first)

1. **Go to Relax mode.** If you're on the Standard plan or higher, Relax mode = **unlimited generations** — burn through this whole file and then some. (On Basic you're capped by fast-time, so do the ⭐ priority prompts first.)
2. **Lock your style code FIRST** (Step 0 below), then append `--sref YOURCODE` to *every* prompt so the entire library looks like one brand. This is the single most important step — it's your brand identity without a logo.
3. **Midjourney can't do text.** Every prompt below ends with `--no text, watermark, logo, signage`. Headlines, your name, and brand bars get added later in Canva. Prompts are built to leave **copy space** for that.
4. **Download everything before you cancel.** Web app → your archive → select all → download. Don't cancel until the files are on your drive.

## BRAND DNA (what makes these "yours")

- **Palette in words:** deep midnight-navy, emerald-green accents, snow-white highlights, warm amber money-tones. (Hex for the Canva step: Midnight `#1e293b`, Emerald `#059669`, Snow `#f8fafc`, Amber `#d97706`, Red `#ef4444`.)
- **Look:** clean editorial real-estate photography, natural light, premium but grounded — NOT stocky, NOT fantasy, NOT over-saturated.
- **Place (keep it real to Temple):** Central Texas / Bell County — modern Texas suburban, brick & limestone, board-and-batten modern farmhouse, mature live oaks, big open skies. **No mountains, no desert, no beaches, no skyscraper skylines.**
- **The tail you append to most prompts:**
  `--style raw --sref YOURCODE --no text, watermark, logo, signage, deformed`
  (add `--ar` per use; drop `--style raw` only on the abstract/texture prompts in sections J–K)
