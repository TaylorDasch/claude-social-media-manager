Quick one that saves a lot of stress later: before you start touring homes, it helps to know your *comfortable* number — not the maximum a lender will approve you for. They're usually pretty far apart.

The simplest way to find it is the 28/36 rule:

• Keep your house payment (with taxes + insurance) under 28% of your gross monthly income
• Keep all your monthly debt under 36%

On a $7,000/month income, that's about a $1,960 payment — which in Temple, with our property taxes, points to roughly the $260K–$280K range (right around our $270K median). Spend much past 32% of your income on housing and life gets tight fast.
