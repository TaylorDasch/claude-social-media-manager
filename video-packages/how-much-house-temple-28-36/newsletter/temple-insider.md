## SECTION: How much house can you actually afford in Temple?

The bank will hand you a maximum. It's almost never the number you should spend.

I just published a full breakdown of the rule I'd give my own family — the **28/36 rule** — using real Temple numbers instead of generic national math. Three things worth pulling out of it:

**1. Your payment, not your ceiling.** Keep housing (principal, interest, taxes, insurance) under 28% of gross monthly income, and total debt under 36%. On a $7,000/month income, that's about a $1,960 payment — which in Temple, with our ~2.25% property taxes, lands you around the $260K–$280K range. (Temple's median is $270K.)

**2. The 32% trap.** Cross 32% of your income on housing and you're "house-poor" — nice home, no room to save, invest, or fix what breaks. Approval is a ceiling, not a target.

**3. Down payment beats rate.** On a $260K home, going from 5% to 20% down can cut your payment by ~$350/month. Most buyers obsess over the rate and underestimate this.

I also cover the four loan programs to compare (don't default to FHA) and the $2,000/year tax credit I used on my own first home.
