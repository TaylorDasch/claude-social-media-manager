**How much house can you actually afford in Temple? Use the 28/36 rule.**

The bank will quote you a maximum. The 28/36 rule gives you a comfortable number — and they're rarely the same.

I'm Taylor Dasch with EG Realty. Here's the quick version for Temple buyers:

• Keep your housing payment — principal, interest, taxes, AND insurance — under **28%** of gross monthly income.
• Keep your **total** monthly debt under **36%**.

On a $7,000/month income, that's about a **$1,960** payment. With Temple's effective property-tax rate near **2.25%**, that points most buyers to the **$260,000–$280,000** range — right around Temple's **$270,000** median price.

Push past 32% of your income on housing and you're "house-poor": nice home, no room left to save, invest, or cover a repair.
