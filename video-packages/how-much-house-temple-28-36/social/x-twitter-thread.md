**1/**
The bank says you qualify for $360K.

That's not how much house you can afford. That's how much debt they'll let you carry.

Here's how to find YOUR number if you're buying in Temple, TX 🧵

**2/**
The 28/36 rule.

→ Housing payment under 28% of gross monthly income
→ Total debt (housing + cars + cards) under 36%

That second number is the one that keeps you out of trouble.

**3/**
"Housing payment" = PITI:
Principal + Interest + Taxes + Insurance.

In Central Texas, taxes + insurance alone can add $650–$750/mo. Most buyers forget that part and wonder why the "affordable" payment feels tight.

**4/**
The math on a $7,000/month income ($84k/yr):

28% = $1,960/mo
That's your target housing payment.

**5/**
With Temple's ~2.25% effective property tax, $1,960/mo lands you around the $260K–$280K range.

Temple's median price? $270K. Right in the pocket.

**6/**
Plot twist: your DOWN PAYMENT moves the answer more than the interest rate.

$260K home, 5% down → ~$2,318/mo
$260K home, 20% down → ~$1,969/mo

Same house. ~$350/mo difference. PMI + a bigger loan.

**7/**
The "house-poor" line is 32%.

Cross it and you've got a nice kitchen, a nice bathroom, and no money to save, invest, or fix the AC.

Approval is a ceiling. Not a target.

**8/**
Don't default to FHA, either. Compare all four:
FHA · VA · USDA · Conventional

And ask your lender about the Mortgage Credit Certificate — up to $2,000/yr back. I used it on my own first home.
