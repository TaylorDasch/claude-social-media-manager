"How much house can we afford?" 🏠

It's the first question almost every Temple buyer asks me — and the honest answer is NOT the number the bank approves you for.

I'm Taylor Dasch with EG Realty, and I bought my own first home right here in Temple. Here's the rule I'd give my own family:

The 28/36 rule 👇
✅ Keep your house payment (with taxes + insurance) under 28% of your monthly income
✅ Keep ALL your debt — house, cars, cards — under 36%

For a household making $7,000/month, that's about a $1,960 payment. With our Central Texas property taxes (~2.25% in Temple), that lands you right around the $260K–$280K range — basically Temple's median price of $270K. And it leaves you room to actually live: save, travel, handle the AC unit that always seems to quit in July. 😅
