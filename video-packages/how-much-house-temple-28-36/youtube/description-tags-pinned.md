📌 THE QUICK VERSION
• Housing payment (principal, interest, taxes, insurance) under 28% of gross monthly income
• Total monthly debt under 36%
• $7,000/mo income → ~$1,960 payment → ~$260K–$280K in Temple (median is $270K)
• Past 32% on housing = "house-poor"
• Compare FHA, VA, USDA, and conventional — don't default to FHA
• Ask about the MCC (up to $2,000/yr back)
