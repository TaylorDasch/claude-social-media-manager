## ⭐ MASTER SCRIPT ARCHITECTURE (Applies to Every Format Below)

Every long-form video — regardless of pillar or format — follows this 4-part architecture. The format-specific structures below define the BODY only; the Hook, Early CTA, and End Screen Handoff are universal.

### 1. HOOK (0:00–0:30) — Open Loop + Stakes

The hook is a contract with the viewer: state what they'll get + add ONE curiosity-gap promise saved for later. Without the open loop, retention falls off a cliff at 1:30–2:30.

**Template:**
> "In this video I'm going to cover [N specific things — usually 3-5]. But stay until [moment — usually 'the end' or 'a few minutes in'] because there's one thing most [target persona] miss that costs them [specific consequence — money, time, regret]. I'll save that for [later moment]."

**Example (Living in Temple — relocation):**
> "In this video I'll break down the 5 neighborhoods most BSW residents are buying in for 2026, what each one actually costs all-in with taxes, and the school district breakdown. But stay until the end because there's one neighborhood that looks great on Zillow and is a trap I see physicians get burned on every single year — and I'll tell you exactly which one and why."

**Hook rules:**
- Never start with "hey guys welcome back" — open with the contract.
- First 15 seconds include at least one specific number (price, count, year, %)
- The open-loop promise must be honored. Lying about the payoff destroys trust.
- Entity declaration ("Hi, I'm Taylor Dasch with EG Realty in Temple, TX") goes at 0:15–0:25, AFTER the hook lands — not before.

---

### 2. EARLY CTA (0:30–1:00) — Immediately After Hook

The single biggest leverage point on the entire channel. ~70% of viewers see this position vs ~30% who watch through to an end-of-video CTA → ~2.5× lead conversion.

**Why post-hook (not end):**
- End-of-video CTAs only reach the small minority who watch through.
- Post-hook CTAs reach the engaged majority who just committed to staying.
- Reframing "like & subscribe" → "private 1:1" turns viewers into leads, not vanity metrics.

**Canonical CTA template (Taylor voice — no fluff):**

> "If you're thinking about buying, selling, investing, or relocating to Temple or anywhere in Bell County, I get a handful of folks every week reaching out for a private 1:1. No pitch — I'll run the numbers on your specific situation and give you the honest read. Calendar's in the description, or text me directly at 254-718-4249."

**Variants by persona (pick one per video — match to topic):**

| Persona | Variant |
|---------|---------|
| Investor / Deal of the Week | "If you're looking at rentals in Temple or Bell County, I get a few investors a week sending me deals to underwrite. Send me yours and I'll run the actual numbers — no pitch, just the honest read on cash flow, comps, and what the risk really looks like. DM keyword UNDERWRITE or text 254-718-4249." |
| BSW physician / relocator | "If you're a BSW resident or attending heading to Temple, I get a handful of physicians every week reaching out 6–18 months before they actually move. We get on a private call, I send you the timing playbook, the loan options, and the neighborhoods that actually fit. Calendar's in the description." |
| Seller (market update video) | "If you're sitting on a Temple-area home and trying to figure out whether to sell now or hold, I get sellers every week asking me to run the actual numbers on their property — not a generic CMA, the real read. Text me at 254-718-4249 and I'll get you the analysis." |
| Buyer (neighborhood / tour) | "If you're house-hunting in Temple or Bell County, I get a few buyers every week reaching out for a private 1:1 — no pitch, I just walk you through the neighborhoods that fit your situation, what each one actually costs all-in, and what to avoid. Calendar's in the description." |

**Rules:**
- NEVER use "like, comment, subscribe" as the primary CTA. That gets you likes; you want leads.
- ONE CTA per video — drive them all to a single action (calendar, DM keyword, text).
- The CTA goes on-screen as a lower-third text overlay at the same time (visual + audio reinforcement).
- The same CTA can be soft-restated at the end (~9:00 mark in a 10-min video), but the PRIMARY is post-hook.

### 3. BODY (~90% of runtime) — Format-Specific

Use one of the 6 formats below. The body delivers the contract from the hook AND the curiosity-gap promise (usually at 60–70% of runtime — the "tease early, deliver late" rule).

### 4. END SCREEN HANDOFF (last 20–30 sec) — Drives Session Time

NOT a generic "like and subscribe." A curiosity tease into the next logical video on Taylor's channel. Full template + pairing rules in `YOUTUBE-GROWTH-PLAYBOOK.md` → END SCREEN HANDOFF section. Every script must end with a specific recommended-video handoff, not generic CTA.

**Quick template:**
> "We covered [TOPIC], but if you're [TARGET ACTION], the next thing you need to know is [RECOMMENDED TOPIC] — and there's one piece of that most people miss. I broke it down in this video right here. Click it and I'll walk you through it."

## FORMAT 1: DEAL BREAKDOWN (8-12 min)
**Best for:** Deal of the Week, property walkthroughs with numbers
**Channel:** Investing in Temple

### Structure:
- **0:00-0:25** — NEGATIVE HOOK (state what's wrong or surprising about the deal)
- **0:25-1:30** — PROPERTY CONTEXT (neighborhood, year built, condition, why it matters)
- **1:30-4:00** — THE NUMBERS (purchase price → down payment → PITI → rent → cash flow)
- **4:00-6:00** — THE RISK SECTION (foundation age, HOA, insurance, what could go wrong)
- **6:00-8:00** — THE VERDICT (would I buy this? at what price? for which strategy?)
- **8:00-9:00** — COMPARISON CONTEXT (how this stacks vs your buy box, vs last week's deal)
- **9:00-end** — CTA (specific: "if you want me to run numbers on a property like this...")

**Filming style:** Desk + screen share for numbers, cut to property B-roll during context
**Retention lever:** Put the verdict LAST — tease it in the hook ("I'll tell you if I'd buy this")

## FORMAT 2: NEIGHBORHOOD TOUR (10-15 min)
**Best for:** Living in Temple channel, relocation buyers, BSW/military
**Channel:** Living in Temple

### Structure:
- **0:00-0:20** — PROBLEM HOOK ("Moving to Temple and don't know where to live?")
- **0:20-1:00** — THE 3-SENTENCE VERDICT (give the answer immediately — don't make them wait)
- **1:00-3:00** — COMMUTE TEST (real-time drive to BSW or Fort Hood gate, show the clock)
- **3:00-5:00** — SCHOOLS (specific ratings, not just district name)
- **5:00-7:00** — PRICE RANGES + WHAT YOU GET (examples at low/mid/high end of neighborhood)
- **7:00-9:00** — THE NEGATIVES (mandatory — this is your differentiator)
- **9:00-11:00** — WHO THIS NEIGHBORHOOD IS FOR (persona match: investor? BSW resident? family?)
- **11:00-end** — CTA + related video card

**Filming style:** Drive-through POV + walk-and-talk at key locations + desk for data
**Retention lever:** "Red flags" section — tease in hook, deliver at 7:00

## FORMAT 3: NEGATIVE HOOK / "TEMPLE TRAP" (5-8 min)
**Best for:** Both channels, algorithm boost, trust building
**Channel:** Either

### Structure:
- **0:00-0:15** — BOLD NEGATIVE STATEMENT ("Don't buy on south even streets in Temple")
- **0:15-1:00** — WHY (data-backed explanation of the problem)
- **1:00-3:00** — THE EVIDENCE (specific examples, addresses if appropriate, real numbers)
- **3:00-5:00** — WHAT TO DO INSTEAD (the alternative that works)
- **5:00-end** — BROADER LESSON (what this teaches about Temple/investing in general)

**Filming style:** Desk with graphics, or on-location at the problem area
**Retention lever:** Controversy in the title + thumbnail drives clicks; substance retains

## FORMAT 4: EXPLAINER / EDUCATIONAL (6-10 min)
**Best for:** Physician loan, BAH calculator, tax protest, BRRRR explanation
**Channel:** Either (depends on topic)

### Structure:
- **0:00-0:20** — OUTCOME HOOK ("This loan lets you buy a house with zero dollars down")
- **0:20-1:30** — WHO THIS IS FOR (specific persona)
- **1:30-4:00** — HOW IT WORKS (step-by-step, screen share if math involved)
- **4:00-6:00** — THE CATCH (what they don't tell you — again, your differentiator)
- **6:00-8:00** — REAL EXAMPLE (actual numbers from Temple market)
- **8:00-end** — NEXT STEPS CTA

**Filming style:** Desk + screen share heavy, B-roll of relevant locations
**Retention lever:** "The catch" section — everyone clicks to hear what's wrong

## FORMAT 5: YOUTUBE SHORT / TIKTOK (21-45 sec)
**Best for:** Algorithm discovery, repurposed clips, quick data drops
**Channel:** Either

### Structure:
- **0:00-0:03** — PATTERN INTERRUPT (bold statement, visual change, or question)
- **0:03-0:20** — ONE SINGLE POINT (one stat, one fact, one tip — never two)
- **0:20-0:30** — RESTATE + IMPLICATION ("which means if you're investing in Temple...")
- **0:30-0:45** — SOFT CTA or loop back to hook

**Filming style:** Vertical, face-to-camera, fast cuts, text overlays for numbers
**Retention lever:** Loop structure — last sentence connects to first

## FORMAT 6: COMPARISON / VS (10-15 min)
**Best for:** City comparisons, neighborhood matchups, strategy comparisons
**Channel:** Either

### Structure:
- **0:00-0:20** — BOLD FRAMING ("Everyone says Temple and Killeen are the same. They're not.")
- **0:20-1:00** — What you're comparing and why it matters
- **1:00-3:00** — METRIC 1: Price / affordability (side by side)
- **3:00-5:00** — METRIC 2: Taxes / appreciation (side by side)
- **5:00-7:00** — METRIC 3: Schools / safety / lifestyle (side by side)
- **7:00-9:00** — METRIC 4: Investment potential (side by side)
- **9:00-11:00** — THE VERDICT: who should pick which, and why
- **11:00-end** — CTA

**Filming style:** Split-screen data, desk + Google Maps, B-roll of both locations
**Retention lever:** Don't reveal the verdict until the end. Tease it: "By the end you'll know exactly which one fits your situation."

## UNIVERSAL RULES FOR ALL FORMATS

1. First sentence is NEVER "hey guys welcome back" — it's always a hook with an open loop (see Master Script Architecture above)
2. Every script includes at least ONE specific number in the first 15 seconds
3. Every script includes at least ONE negative/honest moment
4. **Primary CTA goes RIGHT AFTER THE HOOK (0:30–1:00), not at the end.** Use the canonical "private 1:1" template (Master Script Architecture, section 2). End-of-video CTAs reach ~30% of viewers; post-hook CTAs reach ~70% → ~2.5× lead conversion.
5. **Every hook contains an open-loop curiosity tease** — promise ONE specific thing they'll only get if they stay (a trap to avoid, a number that shocks, an answer saved for the end). Honor the promise or destroy trust.
6. Specific CTA, never generic — "DM UNDERWRITE for me to run the numbers" / "text 254-718-4249 for a private 1:1" beats "like and subscribe"
7. Temple-specific data beats generic advice — always localize
8. Tease the most interesting part early, deliver it at the 60–70% mark (retention architecture)
9. Entity declaration goes at 0:15–0:25 (AFTER the hook lands, not before): "Hi, I'm Taylor Dasch with EG Realty in Temple, TX"
10. Visual/audio change every 7 seconds in short-form, every 15–20 seconds in long-form
11. **End-screen verbal handoff is required** — script the curiosity tease into a specific next video (Master Script Architecture, section 4). Generic "thanks for watching" is forbidden.
12. **Pillar tag required** — every script must declare its pillar (1 Relocation / 2 Market Update / 3 Neighborhood Deep Dive / 4 Home Tour / 5 Lifestyle & Community) so registry can track pillar performance vs leads.
13. Never use: "dream home," "white glove," "charming," "nestled," "turnkey," "Fort Hood" (use "Fort Hood")
14. Always use: "buy-and-hold investors," "Fort Hood," specific numbers over approximations
15. **Raw > polished.** Default to talking-head + screen-share + minimal cuts. Heavy B-roll stacks and decorative text overlays are no longer rewarded by the algorithm (that was the 2021–2024 style). Less polish = more output + better algorithm signal.
